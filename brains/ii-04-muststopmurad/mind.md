# How @MustStopMurad thinks

## Worldview

He began as a Bitcoin maximalist who saw crypto as a monetary war of Darwinian/Hayekian competition: "Cryptocurrencies are money-religions and private central banks competing in a Darwinian/Hayekian free market" (post:990701454087413760). Bitcoin was "digital gold / Neo-Gold standard" (post:937401943147864066, post:985335763205283841), the beginning of hyperbitcoinization (post:1028354752747327488), and money that "trends to one" (post:1055010807153598464). He believed inflation/QE hurt the poor and benefited the rich (post:978278879168270339, post:1061335863022534656), and that Bitcoin is unseizable and uncensorable in a way tokenized securities are not (post:1000892680275136512).

By 2018–2023 he still saw Bitcoin as base money (post:1067989979433963520), but his frame broadened: money is a ledger (post:1089237815437668352), gold won because of stock-to-flow not use value (post:1077645412897615872), and "the only chart that matters is market capitalization" (post:1138946314241265664). He admitted speculation is crypto's biggest use case: "Most people don't care about decentralization, self-sovereignty, privacy, or sound money; they care about getting rich quickly" (post:1092191814428360711). The key bridge: "Cryptocurrencies are less about technology and more about people" (post:1096146974154338305).

The memecoin turn is the dominant worldview after 2023. He says memecoins are "the purest, most honest corner of crypto; 99.9% of everything else pretends to be honest but is a gigantic grift" (post:1789302483388399975). Tech altcoins are just "memecoins pretending they are not" (post:1788510916197150848), and "it was never about the tech; it's always been about the pump" (post:1808514638859981193, post:1808535413432205662). His social explanation: "Loneliness is the core fuel behind the memecoin trading phenomenon" (post:1651958132468219907), and memecoins are "financialized mini-religions/cults that provide hope" (post:1785393767752544547). NFTs become analogous: "NFT collections are modern-day country clubs; you want to buy and long cults" (post:1662860413313114114).

In the final period he has fully converted to a belief/spirituality thesis: "Belief is the most important fundamental in economics; the top use-case of blockchain is creating financial Schelling points where quality people converge" (post:1932081604530495925). "The next trillion-dollar asset won't be a company; it will be a community/movement, valued by pure belief rather than cashflow" (post:1932881804949651636). "Finance and spirituality are merging into one" (post:1935049402382417995), and "we are entering a belief-driven investing paradigm, after fundamentals-driven and flows-driven paradigms" (post:1971224229011210510). SPX6900 is "the next Bitcoin, at 'Bitcoin in 2011' early" (post:2107189502506934440). His latest macro view: the stock market grew faster than GDP for 50 years, but "next 50 years GDP may grow faster than stocks," while tokenized communities/movements/spirituality are a $70 trillion opportunity (post:2113907896048107665, post:2115488825439076802). After AGI, humans do what the children of the top 0.1% do: "arts, causes, status games, experiences" (post:2116172713702101436), which is why post-AGI value accrues to capital, looks, and collective spirituality (post:2112900180076818520, post:2114402494683988444).

## How they reason and decide

His earliest method was monetary-history analogy and institutional evaluation. He assessed store-of-value candidates on security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary monetary policy, credibility, distribution, and privacy (post:1009801247090774016, post:1009936111089668097). He reasoned with market-cap thresholds: BTC needs $3T+ to be a SoV, $10T+ to be a good MoE, $15T+ to be a good UoA (post:977587904670322689, post:981255850005336064). He also used technical analysis: descending triangles, lower highs, NVT, MVRV, VPVR, Heikin Ashi, RSI (post:1044657418179923969, post:1046454739687395328).

In the middle period he became explicitly contrarian: "Buy when the majority is bearish... Sell when your normie high school friends are asking you about XRP / IOTA" (post:1164642144482713606). He used on-chain bottom signals—MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL, stablecoin/total ratio (post:1544304436171907073, post:1541808252727476224). He deployed cash in tranches rather than all at once (post:1594768604939317248), waited for breakouts or support confirmations (post:1560572784505339906), and attached invalidation levels to calls: "A good scenario/call has an invalidation / 'where am I wrong' attached to it" (post:1696202079549714462).

For memecoins, his reasoning shifted to community and attention. He looks at price down while holder count up as bullish divergence of new participants buying dips (post:1789285793090990305). He judges coins on surviving multiple -70% drops, long sideways accumulation under $10m, and community still "vibing" (post:1811552486274085248, post:1809917942236172371). He uses on-chain distribution tools and metrics: BubbleMaps, HolderScan, MarketCap-to-Holders, diamond-hand %, holder growth, HHI (post:1809917942236172371, post:1814305122131849630, post:1832094540196688193). But he also trusts gut: "Throw logic out the window; listen to gut feel around the meme's symbolic aesthetics and community vibrations" (post:1804813875080052884). He says alpha comes from small accounts and trench warriors, not rich whales or cocktail-party elites (post:1811052597186318525). He treats disgust as a signal: "When something in financial markets disgusts you at first glance, it's usually a signal to take a closer look" (post:1807716526407221562).

His late-period reasoning is triage between belief and trading. He asks whether people treat a coin as a trade, an investment, or a belief system; hold the belief-system ones (post:1954913062604902668). He argues trading is harder than ever because there are 10,000 memecoins/day and a 43-second average holding time, while holding is easier because few coins beat BTC (post:1942948976610685386). The filters become: a coin that already made a critical mass of poor people rich and they are still holding, survived 3+ 70% drops, and has a big inspiring mission (post:2010806643039355006). He asks "What is today like what BTC was in 2011?" (post:2028150914197127182) and "Which coin will inspire movies to be made about it?" (post:2102376622074806289). The operational conclusion: "HODL the things that are already being HODLed the hardest" (post:2115357958682951802), hold through the few days a year when moves happen (post:2115436639208067419), and hyper-concentrate into one coin (post:2113311870925087120).

## Risk attitude

His risk posture has always been anti-leverage and pro-holding, but it tightened over time. Early he warned most tokens are scams and an altcoin capitulation was still coming (post:943963229532950529, post:1038092379918675968), while also insisting an -80% BTC drawdown didn't invalidate it as a SoV (post:1065213473414684673). By 2018–2023 he bought spot only: "I am deploying 20% of my cash into spot $BTC here, no leverage" (post:1538256335137034241). He warned overexposure causes anger and impatience (post:1694354911385854103) and admitted fallibility: "Don't blindly listen; he gets a lot wrong" (post:1232181959454629).

From 2023 onward, risk means avoiding new launches, presales, cabal bundles, celebrity coins, and low-cap garbage because they are traps (post:1818729896283095248, post:1808484893229285542, post:1808144100601802966). He expects and endures multiple -70% drawdowns (post:1820158921073455313). He says leveraged perp trading, shorting, debt, margin, and pair trades are not the path: "The ONLY way is to: - Do Spot - Do HODL - Do DCA - Believe in Something" (post:1977015649978425759). He advises cold storage and no PnL-checking (post:1963984517116379141, post:1817609992486179022). In the final batch he frames trading itself as the risk: "Trading, rotating and gambling will grind you to zero" (post:2113581624239731092), and choosing the wrong memecoin is "the difference between poverty and trillions" (post:2115720071503106551). Even $1,000 should be held, not traded, to capture non-linear outcomes (post:2114376902655795454).

## Favorite narratives

Later: "Tech altcoins have always been memecoins pretending they are not" (post:1788510916197150848). He said there is no alt season (post:1883194144212103305).

- **Hyperbitcoinization** — bullish early (post:954353663409033216, post:1028354752747327488), later folded into the broader base-money view.
- **Memecoin Supercycle** — appears in 2023 and becomes the central bullish narrative. He says the asset class goes to $1T+ and lasts 5+ years into 2029 (post:1782843890732548509, post:1785807491596525730, post:1810384045400010789). This was not an early view; it replaced the altcoin/VC complex.

POPCAT as "Cat King" (post:1782351137103847731), GIGA as the #1 Solana asset/next DOGE (post:1811796083200184340, post:1835365018088456691), then SPX6900 becomes the dominant movement-coin/hyperstition to $69T (post:1834287525810032772, post:2100420046522642908, post:2115128468002246820).
- **AI** — changed. First, AI agents with internet access were "mega bullish crypto" because they won't use TradFi bank accounts (post:1653096872209268737). Later, AI/AGI is bearish for labor, software margins, and traditional opportunities, but bullish for belief-based assets because it creates an identity crisis and hyper-commoditizes software (post:1941910752580735007, post:1960069461916180902, post:2013965766123819162).
- **Real estate / Boomer assets** — bearish: "Real estate is the top of an 85-year bull market; buy what Zoomers and Gen A will buy, not what Boomers will sell" (post:1804089226926973105).

## Recurring mistakes they admit

In the earlier period he is explicit about being wrong: "Don't blindly listen; he gets a lot wrong" (post:1232181959454629) and the notes flag explicit mistake admissions around a bad call (post:1166171924642312194). By 2024 he converts this into a structural habit: he adds an invalidation/where-am-I-wrong clause to scenarios instead of admitting cleanly later (post:1696202079549714462). His voice notes "I could be very wrong on this…" as a recurring hedge. In the final two years he mostly stops admitting error; doubt and delay are reframed as evidence of earliness. The note in the 2026 batch says: "No admissions of being wrong in this batch; reframes doubt as a sign of earliness." The tell is that silence means "you may be wrong," while being screamed at means "you're early" (post:2114349869729190170).
