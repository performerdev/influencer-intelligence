# How @MustStopMurad thinks

## Worldview

The account starts as a Bitcoin maximalist who sees crypto as money-religions competing in a Darwinian/Hayekian free market; the reinvention of money is "the biggest TAM and is mindblowingly underhyped" (post:937405934992752647). Early posts treat Bitcoin as digital gold / Neo-Gold whose core proposition is not quick transactions (post:937401943147864066), while most tokens are "quick financing money grabs" that cannot capture underlying network value (post:943963229532950529). Hyperbitcoinization is not optional: "Everyone in the world will become a Bitcoin Maximalist whether they like it or not; resistance is futile and financially dangerous" (post:1028354752747327488). Money is a ledger — all money is "a kind of Excel spreadsheet" — and cryptocurrencies offer stronger guarantees (post:1089237815437668352). By mid-period, this becomes "the only chart that matters is market capitalization; claiming otherwise is crypto virtue signaling" (post:1138946314241265664). Bitcoin is framed as giving the 99% a way to preserve labor while giving the 1% an unseizable asset (post:1102622360086159361).

The change begins around 2023. The notes say crypto probably has 3 or 4 boom-and-bust cycles left before equilibrium (post:1548306855792824321), and NFTs are "modern-day country clubs; you want to buy and long cults" (post:1662860413313114114). From 2023-08 on, the account pivots hard into memecoins: "It was never about the tech; it's always been about the pump" (post:1808514638859981193). Tech altcoins are "memecoins pretending they are not," and memecoins "rip the bandaid off and steal their speculative energy" (post:1788510916197150848). Memecoins become "the purest, most honest corner of crypto; 99.9% of everything else pretends to be honest but is a gigantic grift" (post:1789302483388399975). The market is "routing itself around the toxic VC-altcoin complex; buy BTC and memecoins" (post:1788254986918350891).

By 2024-2026, the worldview hardens into a social and belief doctrine. The strongest driver of value is "community, belief, and cult-like conviction, not technology or utility" (post:1847290720400351564). The next trillion-dollar asset "won't be a company; it will be a community/movement, valued by pure belief rather than cashflow" (post:1932881804949651636). Finance and spirituality are "merging into one" (post:1935049402382417995). AI/AGI will destroy jobs, freeze mobility, and collapse profit margins, making belief-based assets more valuable (post:1941910752580735007). The market is "a device for transferring wealth from degens to the non-degens" (post:2104629190712860827). In the final notes, the model is absolute: "You're not investing in Coins, Memes or Projects. You're investing in *COMMUNITIES OF PEOPLE*" (post:2105997468903088370), and SPX6900 is "the next Bitcoin" and "a once-in-a-decade asset" (post:2107189502506934440, post:2106418748181598418).

## How they reason and decide

Early reasoning is systematic and historical. The account evaluates store-of-value candidates on security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary monetary policy, credibility, distribution, and privacy (post:1009801247090774016, post:1009936111089668097, post:1009354979281338369). It uses monetary history: gold won because of stock-to-flow, not use value (post:1077645412897615872). It reasons in market-cap thresholds: BTC needs $3T+ to be a SoV, $10T+ to be a good MoE, $15T+ to be a good UoA (post:977587904670322689, post:981255850005336064). Money trends to one because of monetary network effects and liquidity (post:1055010807153598464, post:1055065511514529792). Technical analysis is explicit: descending triangles, lower highs, NVT, MVRV, VPVR, Heikin Ashi, RSI (post:1044657418179923969, post:1045358253570490370, post:1052611501813510145).

In 2018-2023, the process adds on-chain and market signals: MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL, and stablecoin/total ratio for bottoming zones (post:1544304436171907073, post:1541808252727476224). The account is contrarian: "Buy when 65% of CT influencers calling for an e c h o b u b b l e ... Sell when your normie high school friends are asking you about XRP / IOTA" (post:1164642144482713606). It waits for confirmation rather than knife-catching: "The better long / add is either after a break out from the downward channel or somewhere in the green zone" (post:1560572784505339906). It adjusts price for money supply (post:1539645197872762882) and uses a "whales torture normies" framework (post:1129408042213285889).

The 2023-2024 period turns qualitative. A good call carries an invalidation / "where am I wrong" (post:1696202079549714462). Strength is selection: outperformers on a dip become outperformers on the bounce (post:1785592475039478071), and the play is to "identify the #1 coin in each Memecoin sub-category and simply Buy & Hold" (post:1806372643957792882). Distribution is checked with BubbleMaps and HolderScan (post:1809917942236172371, post:1790359427347845621). The signal is holder count up while price down (post:1789285793090990305). Disgust is alpha: "When something in financial markets disgusts you at first glance, it's usually a signal to take a closer look" (post:1807716526407221562).

By 2025-2026, the method is almost entirely social. The account asks which coins people treat as "a trade, an investment, or a belief system; hold the belief-system ones" (post:1954913062604902668). The filter: a coin must have already made "a Critical Mass of Poor people Rich (AND THEY ARE STILL HOLDING)," survived 3+ 70% drops, and have "a Big Inspiring Mission" (post:2010806643039355006). It looks for "a core base of fanatical zealots who proved loyalty for years" (post:2103150481929376011). Attention is easy and short-lived; belief is hard and long-lasting (post:2085456786488742313). By 2026, the reasoning is compressed to: "To find the coin with the biggest upside, look for the people with the biggest levels of belief" (post:2105641789747339540). The old chart-first method is explicitly rejected: "spx6900 commandment #5: there is no chart" (post:2092954507437036025).

## Risk attitude

The risk attitude starts volatile but stoic. In 2017-2018 he warns most tokens are scams (post:943963229532950529) and non-BTC altcoins have not yet had deep capitulation (post:1038092379918675968), but also says BTC down -80% from ATH does not make it a bad SoV (post:1065213473414684673) and advises staying calm during the bear market (post:1055558974387470336). He acknowledges the risk of new technology and BTC social-contract collapse (post:970504359736946689).

In 2018-2023, risk management becomes explicit: deploy cash in tranches — "bullet 2 of 5" (post:1594768604939317248), no leverage on spot buys (post:1538256335137034241), and overexposure leads to anger and impatience (post:1694354911385854103). He admits "he gets a lot wrong" and says don't blindly listen (post:1232181959454629). The macro risk is named: a global financial crisis could affect BTC differently than ever before (post:1096797523065348104).

From 2023 onward, the main risk emphasis flips from entry to holding and custody. The danger is not just drawdown but selling: "95% of your profits will come from doing nothing" (post:1789046659965452453), and "the number one mistake people make in crypto is that they can't HOLD" (post:1940473255808364584). New launches are increasingly -EV (post:1790341286471348562). By 2025-2026 the rules are absolute: no leverage, no margin, no debt, no pair trades; only spot, HODL, DCA, belief (post:1977015649978425759); put all coins in cold storage (post:1963984517116379141). He warns "they want to shake you out before the ultra parabola" (post:2001756665478365639). The final 2026-09 to 2026-10 notes contain no risk entries, matching the euphoric SPX6900 view.

## Recurring mistakes they admit

The account explicitly says in the middle period: "I get a lot of shit wrong" and "Don't blindly listen; he gets a lot wrong" (post:1232181959454629). It also admits mistakes in prior calls (post:1166171924642312194). Later the admissions become rarer. In 2023-2024 he allows "I could be very wrong on this…" as a framing device, and the 2024-2025 voice note says he "rarely admits being wrong. Doubles down on belief and patience, framing dips as tests of conviction." By 2025-2026, delay is reframed as accumulation and inevitability: "It always takes a little longer than you think. And always goes way higher than you think." The shift is from "I was wrong" to "the schedule moved."
