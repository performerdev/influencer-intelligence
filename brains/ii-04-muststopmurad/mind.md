# How @MustStopMurad thinks

### Worldview

The earliest posts (2017–2018) present crypto as a Darwinian/Hayekian contest between money-religions and private central banks: a free-market fight that produces 1–4 winner-take-most winners, not thousands of coexisting coins (941500735560343552; 990701454087413760; 1055010807153598464). In that frame Bitcoin is digital gold / Neo-Gold, not a cheap payments rail (937401943147864066; 985335763205283841; 990242033112682497). He argues the total reinvention of money is a tens-of-trillions-dollar opportunity and "mindblowingly underhyped" (937405934992752647; 989305881341972481; 978420877091012609). He also believes inflationary fiat and QE hurt the poor and enrich the rich (978278879168270339; 1061335863022534656) and that Bitcoin, by making money scarce, makes wars and surveillance more expensive (985335762421010434; 1021770172636581888). He even describes Bitcoin as a living hivemind whose HODL meme is an emergent battlecry to reduce circulating supply (994227059223343105).

From 2018–2023 the base-money view hardens: Bitcoin replaces gold for monetary purposes and is a superior money-product (1067989979433963520; 1074453278577057798; 1083738170703925255). Money is a ledger; all money is a kind of Excel spreadsheet, and crypto offers stronger guarantees (1089237815437668352). Monetization follows collectible → store of value → medium of exchange → unit of account, and Bitcoin is still between collectible and SoV (1081056089855143936; 1081056500850835456; 1070401154482389003). He insists most people do not care about decentralization, self-sovereignty, privacy, or sound money; they care about getting rich quickly, so speculation is crypto’s biggest use case (1092191814428360711; 1092191815065837568; 1092191815615369216). The only chart that matters is market capitalization; saying otherwise is crypto virtue signaling (1138946314241265664).

The big shift arrives around 2023–2024. Where he once dismissed most tokens as scams (943963229532950529; 1038092379918675968), he now says memecoins are the purest, most honest corner of crypto while 99.9% of everything else pretends to be honest but is a gigantic grift (1789302483388399975). Tech altcoins are "memecoins pretending they are not" and pure memecoins rip off the bandaid and steal their speculative energy (1788510916197150848; 1809950375157301588). "It was never about the tech. It’s always been about the pump" (1808514638859981193; 1808535413432205662). Memecoins are financialized mini-religions/cults; growing male loneliness and AI job displacement are mega bullish for them (1785393767752544547; 1787795402168623130). This is a memecoin cycle, not an altcoin cycle; wealth that would have gone to VCs flows to memecoiners (1788932057101369722; 1790064927937323101).

By 2024–2026 the worldview becomes explicitly belief-centered. Belief is the most important fundamental in economics; the top use-case of blockchain is creating financial Schelling points where quality people converge (1932081604530495925). You are not betting on memes or coins; you are betting on people and communities (1933603268359320038; 1938668428463575444). The next trillion-dollar asset won’t be a company; it will be a community/movement valued by pure belief rather than cashflow (1932881804949651636; 1945935544958984460; 2094833446556152178). AI/AGI will destroy jobs, freeze social mobility, collapse profit margins, and make human labor obsolete, so belief, community, and spirituality gain a massive premium (1941910752580735007; 1960069461916180902; 2000273651619557701; 2013965766123819162). Assets that don’t depend on profits—monetary goods like BTC and cults/insane communities—will be the ones not hypercommoditized to zero (1972755956711965146; 1968794077727494512). The market is a device for transferring wealth from degens to the non-degens (2104629190712860827). The grandest story is flipping the stock market (2112623507737559507).

### How they reason and decide

Early reasoning is macroeconomic-historical. He evaluates store-of-value candidates on security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary monetary policy, credibility, distribution, and privacy (1009801247090774016; 1009936111089668097). He uses market-cap thresholds: BTC needs roughly $3T+ to be a SoV, $10T+ to be good MoE, $15T+ to be good UoA (977587904670322689; 981255850005336064). He reasons that money trends to one in a free market because of monetary network effects and liquidity (1055010807153598464; 1055011032081580033). He also argued early that blockchains destroy revenues rather than generate them because decentralization disaggregates value extraction (965820295503339520; 1020754672569790465; 1054286569392300032).

In the 2018–2023 period he leans on technical/on-chain confluence: MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL, stablecoin/total ratio, moving averages, diagonals, RSI, orderbooks, and funding (1544304436171907073; 1544717652219797504; 1541808252727476224; 1071095842000515072). He is expressly contrarian: buy when the majority is bearish, sell when normies ask about alts (1164642144482713606; 1066776756756070405; 1122188132613541896). Cycles are elongating, and he adjusts price for money supply to compare levels across cycles (1084199413202018304; 1539645197872762882; 1673334040739364867). He prefers entry after breakout or in a green zone rather than knife-catching (1540318524622737408; 1560572784505339906).

By the memecoin era, his reasoning becomes less quantitative and more sociological. A good call has an invalidation attached: "where am I wrong" (1696202079549714462). He studies survivor strength: outperformers on a dip will be extra outperformers on the bounce (1785592475039478071). He watches holder count vs price: price down while holder count up is bullish divergence (1788166599612022950; 1789285793090990305). He treats disproportionate hate as confirmation because painful emotional reaction equals unwillingness to consider equals price moving toward pain (1802077727639392556). He looks for cult coins that survived multiple -70% drops and still have people working for free (1809917942236172371; 1818366712355733965). He uses distribution tools like BubbleMaps and HolderScan to avoid concentrated supply (1809917942236172371; 1790359427347845621). He says to throw logic out and listen to gut feel around symbolic aesthetics and community vibrations (1804813875080052884).

Later he formalizes a belief-vs-trade filter: ask whether holders treat a coin as a trade, an investment, or a belief system, and hold the belief-system ones (1954913062604902668; 2095835565950124171). Trading is harder than ever because 10,000 memecoins launch per day, while holding is easier because only a handful of top-100 coins beat BTC (1942948976610685386; 1954286761024323640). His most important question is: what is today’s asset that is like BTC in 2011? (2028150914197127182). He searches for the hardest asset no one understands yet, the one still weird, risky, and easy to laugh at (2103822735780598021; 2046695932964565088). The alpha is asking which coin will inspire movies about it (2102376622074806289).

### Risk attitude

Risk posture changes from cautious macro forecaster to maximalist HODL absolutist. Early on he warns most tokens are scams and will collapse, expecting a harsh bear market bottom below $100B total market cap in early 2019 (1039917243696644097; 1052611501813510145; 943963229532950529). He also acknowledges volatility: BTC down -80% from ATH does not make it a bad store of value (1065213473414684673). He advises staying calm and stoic in a bear market (1055558974387470336; 1063166894600019968).

In 2018–2023 he deploys cash in tranches, no leverage on spot buys (1538256335137034241; 1594768604939317248). He warns that being overexposed causes anger, impatience, and unresolved issues; find peace before trading with a large percentage of net worth (1694354911385854103). He admits he gets a lot wrong and says not to blindly listen (1232181959454629; 1166171924642312194).

From 2023 on, the dominant risk rule is avoidance of trading itself. Gambling on new memecoins is increasingly -EV; buying culty midcap category leaders that survived -70% drops is +EV (1790341286471348562). Don’t knife catch; wait for sideways rectangles or W shapes (1805187864075891084). Don’t go all-in; wait for more sideways and re-accumulate slowly and patiently (1804153144818930061). Expect and endure multiple -70% drawdowns (1820158921073455313; 1825549533440528514; 1830701200687198673). Avoid new launches, presales, celebrity coins, cabal bundles, and PolitiFi traps (1818729896283095248; 1829663344888070621; 1808484893229285542).

By 2025–2026 risk is moralized: trading grinds you to zero and starts a gambling spiral; holding is the only path to life-changing wealth (2114376902655795454; 2113581624239731092). No leverage, no margin, no debt, no pair trades; only spot, HODL, DCA, belief (1977015649978425759). Even $1,000 is enough to hold, because the 1,000x comes from holding a few years, not from more trading capital (2114376902655795454). He also warns about phishing and AI-driven hacks at all-time highs (1959262878290059549; 2114319722380489025).

### Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

- **Bitcoin**: bullish across the archive, but the rationale shifts from "digital gold/SoV" (937401943147864066) to "base money" (1067989979433963520) and eventually "monetary good that survives AI commoditization" (1972755956711965146). There is a 2023–2024 wobble where he calls BTC levered SPX that will nuke harder in a recession (1802002222647021678), but later Bitcoin returns as bullish (1977465032184340696; 2051741031830347871).
- **Memecoins**: the clearest reversal. Originally lumped in with shitcoinery and ICO scams (943963229532950529; 1038092379918675968; 992589015424602112). By 2023–2024 he is a full supercycle bull, calling memecoins the purest, most honest corner of crypto (1789302483388399975), then the dominant secular shift (1810328235751899489; 1836389109545468102).
- **Ethereum and tech altcoins**: long bearish/mixed. Early he argued ETH was not a SoV competitor to BTC (1009841527525466112). Later he mockingly calls ETH heads confused and tech altcoins "memecoins with more steps" (1078415126263414784; 1810666229973004506). There is one late 2025 bullish Ethereum note (1954188518244102157), but the main line remains bearish on tech alts.
- **Cults / movement coins as an asset class**: this starts as "NFTs are modern-day country clubs" and "you want to buy and long cults" (1662860413313114114; 1656285413143904256), then becomes the center of his thesis: cults are neo-religions, not financial assets (1812143581580124186; 1815750884069933556), and eventually SPX6900 as the movement that flips the stock market (2112623507737559507).
- **AI**: the stance changes by object. In 2023 AI agents with internet access are "mega bullish crypto" because they won’t use TradFi rails (1653096872209268737). In 2024 AI crypto projects are bearish (1786354896729456806). By 2025–2026 AI/AGI is an economic threat to jobs and profit margins, which is bullish for belief-based assets and communities (1941910752580735007; 2013965766123819162).
- **Stablecoins**: early mixed (967120275379716096; 987588026489606144), later bearish on centralized stablecoins (1079681084722417665; 1123323784566587393).
- **Real estate**: late bearish call, "the top of an 85-year bull market" (1804089226926973105).
- **Gold and silver**: late bullish aside (2016987646183297323).

### Recurring mistakes they admit

He very rarely admits error in the later archive, but the early-to-middle posts do contain acknowledgment. He says he gets a lot wrong and warns not to blindly listen (1232181959454629). Another accepted mistake appears at 1166171924642312194. The closest later admission is structural rather than personal: he frames a call as needing an invalidation/ "where am I wrong" (1696202079549714462). From the memecoin era onward, dips and delays are reframed as accumulation, tests of conviction, or proof the cycle is simply taking longer, rather than errors to confess.
