# How @MustStopMurad thinks

## Worldview
Early Murad saw crypto as money-religions and private central banks competing in a Darwinian/Hayekian free market that would produce 1-4 winner-take-most winners, not 10,000 coexisting ones (941500735560343552, 990701454087413760). Bitcoin was digital gold / the Neo-Gold standard; its core proposition was hard money, not quick transactions or low fees (937401943147864066, 985335763205283841). He believed hyperbitcoinization was inevitable: everyone would become a Bitcoin maximalist whether they liked it or not, because resistance was futile and financially dangerous (1028354752747327488, 985335773556768768). Inflation disproportionately hurt the poor and benefited the rich through central banking and QE (978278879168270339, 1061335863022534656). Bitcoin was unseizable and uncensorable, while tokenized securities and real estate derived their value from physical reality, approval, and authority (1000892680275136512, 1001205079943385089). Blockchains destroyed revenues rather than generated them; decentralization led to disaggregation and no leech points to extract value (965820295503339520, 1020754672569790465).

By 2019-2023 the monetary framework broadened. Money is a ledger; all money is a kind of Excel spreadsheet, and crypto offers stronger guarantees (1089237815437668352). Bitcoin monetizes through collectible -> store of value -> medium of exchange -> unit of account, and was still between collectible and SoV (1081056089855143936, 1081056500850835456). Gold won because of its stock-to-flow ratio, not use value; silver has more industrial uses but is far less valuable (1077645412897615872, 1080203704941375493). Most people do not care about decentralization, self-sovereignty, privacy, or sound money; they care about getting rich quickly, and speculation is crypto's biggest use case (1092191815065837568, 1082749938906394626). He concluded crypto was less about technology and more about people (1096146974154338305), and the only chart that matters is market capitalization (1138946314241265664). AI agents with internet access are mega bullish because they won't use TradFi bank accounts or debit cards (1653096872209268737). The FTX collapse, he argued, made this bear market shorter, not longer, and was bearish Ethereum and bullish Bitcoin in relative terms (1593305561893847042, 1594072945387003904).

The decisive shift came around 2023-2024. Memecoins became the purest, most honest corner of crypto; 99.9% of everything else pretends to be honest but is a gigantic grift (1789302483388399975). Tech altcoins have always been memecoins pretending they are not; memecoins rip the bandaid off and steal their speculative energy (1788510916197150848, 1809950375157301588). "It was never about the tech; it's always been about the pump." (1808514638859981193). Crypto's three core use cases were money, payments, gambling, plus a long tail of bullshit (1783698268423147968). Memecoins are financialized mini-religions/cults that provide hope; growing male loneliness and AI job displacement are mega bullish for them (1785393767752544547, 1787795402168623130). He predicted the memecoin asset class would go to $1 trillion+ and the bull wave would last 5+ years into 2029 (1782843890732548509, 1785807491596525730). This cycle was a memecoin cycle: wealth that would have gone to VCs flowed to memecoiners instead (1788932057101369722, 1790064927937323101). Cults cannot be manufactured top-down; they must emerge bottom-up from the trenches, from poor people becoming rich (1787549737953976789). Real estate was the top of an 85-year bull market; buy what Zoomers and Gen A will buy, not what Boomers will sell (1804089226926973105).

By 2025-2026 the thesis hardened into belief investing. Belief is the most important fundamental in economics; the top use-case of blockchain is creating financial Schelling points where quality people converge (1932081604530495925). You are not betting on memes or coins; you are betting on people and communities (1933603268359320038, 1938668428463575444). The next trillion-dollar asset won't be a company; it will be a community/movement valued by pure belief rather than cashflow (1932881804949651636, 1945935544958984460). Finance and spirituality are merging into one; this is a multi-trillion-dollar market nobody talks about (1935049402382417995, 1955288533297426609). AI/AGI will destroy jobs, freeze social mobility, collapse profit margins, and make human labor obsolete; belief, community and spirituality will gain a massive premium (1941910752580735007, 1960069461916180902). Assets that don't depend on profits — monetary goods like BTC and cults/insane communities — are the ones that won't be hypercommoditized to zero (1972755956711965146, 1999574063107264769). The system is broken: inflation, unaffordable housing, dead social contracts, rising inequality; SPX6900 is the escape vessel/lifeboat for the left-behind 95% (2012933575662846382, 2013588961877192729, 2015861365077098835). The market is a device for transferring wealth from degens to the non-degens (2104629190712860827). Crypto coins are a social phenomenon, not a technological one; the best coins bring together and inspire the greatest number of people (2103217636037292388, 2104206231485489627).

## How they reason and decide
Early reasoning was explicitly historical and Austrian. He used gold, silver, and fiat monetary evolution as a framework to reason about crypto winners (985871914034958337, 1009936071982047233, 1019913276690452482). He evaluated SoV candidates on security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary monetary policy, credibility, distribution, and privacy (1009801247090774016, 1009354979281338369). He used market-cap thresholds: BTC needed $3T+ to be a good SoV, $10T+ to be a good MoE, and $15T+ to be a good UoA (977587904670322689, 981255850005336064). He used technical analysis: descending triangles, lower highs, NVT, MVRV, VPVR, Heikin Ashi, RSI (1044657418179923969, 1045358253570490370). He reasoned that money trends toward one in a free market via monetary network effects and liquidity, winner-take-all (1055010807153598464, 1055011032081580033). Game theory framed state adoption vs. attack on Bitcoin (1060416367906734085).

In 2018-2023, reasoning moved to on-chain and market indicators: MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL, and stablecoin/total ratio to identify bottoming zones and DCA levels (1544304436171907073, 1541808252727476224, 1542538420857315330). He combined multiple technical confluences — moving averages, diagonals, Gaussian supports, RSI, orderbooks, funding rates — before acting (1071095842000515072, 1124752236008153088). He was explicitly contrarian: buy when the majority is bearish or calling for lower, sell when normies ask about alts (1164642144482713606, 1066776756756070405). Cycles lengthen; he used fractal and cycle-length analysis to project bottoms and tops (1084199413202018304, 1597698387075137541). He adjusted price for money supply (M1/M3) to compare levels across cycles (1539645197872762882, 1673334040739364867). He waited for breakouts from bearish channels or key support zones before adding, preferring safer buys after confirmation (1560572784505339906, 1540318524622737408). Timing and execution matter more than charting or prediction (1694835507799228860).

The memecoin era added new filters. A good scenario or call has an invalidation attached: "where am I wrong" (1696202079549714462). He studied strength: outperformers on a dip will be extra outperformers on the bounce (1785592475039478071, 1789285793090990305). Holder count up while price down means new participants buying dips, a bullish divergence (1789285793090990305, 1788166599612022950). When he gets disproportionately more hate/ad-hominem, the probability of his outlined scenario increases, because painful emotional reaction = unwillingness to consider = price goes toward pain (1802077727639392556). Winners win; strength begets strength; buy category leaders rather than spraying into many coins (1787818532677279778, 1804485939592343816). New coins of the cycle outperform; old coins of past cycles underperform (1790088628523127245). Don't knife catch; wait for sideways rectangles or W shapes; most reversals aren't V-reversals (1805187864075891084). The best trades are the obvious ones (1785713782108397909). He used BubbleMaps and HolderScan to check distribution and avoid concentrated supply (1809917942236172371, 1790359427347845621). When something in financial markets disgusts you at first glance, take a closer look (1807716526407221562). The key to undervalued memecoins is emotion: they need to make you feel something; throw logic out the window and listen to gut feel around the meme's symbolic aesthetics and community vibrations (1808567795317362927, 1804813875080052884).

By 2025-2026, reasoning became simpler and more religious. Ask which coins people treat as a trade, an investment, or a belief system; hold the belief-system ones (1954913062604902668, 2095835565950124171). Trading is harder than ever — 10,000 memecoins/day, 43-second average holding time, institutions on BTC only — while holding is easier than ever because few coins beat BTC (1942948976610685386, 1954286761024323640). The market literally tells you which coins to hold: outperformers from 2024 keep outperforming; only a handful of top-100 coins beat BTC (1942948976610685386, 1959986822127165926). Identify coins that already made a critical mass of poor people rich and they're still holding, survived 3+ 70% drops, and have a big inspiring mission (2010806643039355006). Ask how inspired the holders are; if the asset is truly inspiring, the community will move mountains and hold until Valhalla (2092224371087552989). Look for communities with a core base of fanatical zealots who proved loyalty for years (2103150481929376011). The most important question: what is today like what BTC was in 2011? (2028150914197127182). Find the hardest asset that no one understands yet; look for what still looks weird, risky, and easy to laugh at (2103822735780598021, 2046695932964565088). Which coin will inspire movies to be made about it? That's the alpha (2102376622074806289). Attention is easy and short-lived; belief is hard and long-lasting — bet on retention and expansion (2085456786488742313, 2096569748070842499). A 100,000x is only possible when you see something almost no one else does (2108592037532741999).

## Risk attitude
Early on he warned most tokens were scams and would collapse; non-BTC altcoins had not yet experienced deep capitulation (943963229532950529, 1038092379918675968). He expected a harsh bear market, with bottom below $100B total market cap in the first half of 2019 (1052611501813510145, 1039917243696644097). But he advised stoicism: stay calm during the bear (1055558974387470336, 1063166894600019968), and argued BTC down -80% from ATH did not make it a bad SoV (1065213473414684673).

Middle years: he deployed cash in tranches (bullets) and used no leverage on spot buys (1538256335137034241, 1594768604939317248). Overexposure leads to anger, impatience, and being not at peace; find peace before trading with a large percentage of your net worth (1694354911385854103). He acknowledged getting things wrong and said not to blindly listen (1232181959454629, 1166171924642312194). He warned the macro environment and global financial crisis could affect BTC differently than ever before (1096797523065348104, 1096797542849880071), and paper numbers aren't real; not everyone can withdraw profits at the top (1075745935395823617).

Memecoin years: as a long-term committed hodler, price doesn't matter as much as time; a multi-year period of nothingness and stagnation is the real risk (1696202072582959546, 1696202074160087053). Max pain is ETF rejection or approval with underwhelming flows, deflationary recession breaking the halving cycle, and heavy Fed regulation (1733556734323507528). Every hard landing looks like a soft landing at first (1738146109061558446). Don't go all-in; wait for more sideways and re-accumulate slowly and patiently (1804153144818930061). The most important skill in crypto is HODL (1787612015965421817); 95% of your profits will come from doing nothing (1789046659965452453). Gambling on new memecoins is increasingly -EV; buying culty midcap category leaders that survived -70% drops is +EV (1790341286471348562, 1789647774113210686). Don't buy celeb coins; you will get dumped on (1808484893229285542). Any memecoin that didn't spend weeks trading sideways under $10m market cap is a scam (1808144100601802966).

Final period: no leverage, no margin, no debt, no pair trades; the only way is spot, HODL, DCA, belief (1977015649978425759). Put all coins in cold storage; don't store coins on daily devices; don't click links; update MacOS & iOS (1963984517116379141). Gambling and trading are negative-EV and will get worse; 97% of traders lost money over the past year (2100277714737389753, 2098004237636530343). The number one mistake people make in crypto is that they can't HOLD (1940473255808364584, 2093322467477537261). They want to shake you out before the ultra parabola (2001756665478365639). Anyone urging you to gamble on new memecoin launches does not have your best interests at heart (2091159414954750157). Phishing scams and social engineering will target the community as it grows (1959262878290059549). By 2026-09/10, risk is reframed: diamondhand HODLing is the required behavior, not a risk to manage (2109721885449859449).

## Recurring mistakes they admit
Early and mid-era he admitted mistakes more openly: "Don't blindly listen; I get a lot wrong" (1232181959454629), "That was a mistake" (1166171924642312194). He learned to attach invalidation levels to scenarios (1696202079549714462). In the memecoin era he would say "I could be very wrong on this…" and move on, framing calls with invalidation levels. By 2025-2026 he rarely admits being wrong; he reframes delay as accumulation and inevitability: "It always takes a little longer than you think. And always goes way higher than you think." The recurring admitted mistake is usually around timing or local bottoms, not the underlying thesis; he has said he gets calls wrong while the long-term direction remains fixed.
