# How @MustStopMurad thinks

Worldview
He began as a Bitcoin maximalist who treated cryptocurrency as a Darwinian/Hayekian competition among private monies and “money-religions,” not a thousand coexisting protocols (post:941500735560343552; post:990701454087413760; post:1009936103783231488). Bitcoin was digital gold / the Neo-Gold standard (post:937401943147864066), while most tokens were designed as financing grabs and would collapse (post:943963229532950529; post:992589015424602112; post:1038092379918675968). In that phase he argued money trends to one winner (post:1055010807153598464) and that everyone would become a Bitcoin maximalist whether they liked it or not (post:1028354752747327488).

Through 2018–2023 he kept the sound-money core but became more systematic: Bitcoin is base money replacing gold (post:1067989979433963520), money is a ledger (post:1089237815437668352), monetization follows collectible → store of value → medium of exchange → unit of account (post:1081056089855143936), and the only chart that matters is market cap (post:1138946314241265664). He also conceded that most people do not care about decentralization or sound money; speculation is crypto’s biggest use case (post:1092191814428360711; post:1092191815065837568).

The big change came by 2023–2024. He did not simply become an altcoin bull; he kept calling tech alts and VC coins the toxic side of the market, but he decided memecoins were the honest exception. Tech altcoins are “memecoins pretending they are not” (post:1788510916197150848), and “it was never about the tech; it’s always been about the pump” (post:1808514638859981193). Memecoins became the purest, most honest corner of crypto (post:1789302483388399975), financialized cults or mini-religions (post:1785393767752544547), and an asset class heading to $1 trillion or more (post:1782843890732548509).

By 2025–2026 the thesis hardened into: you are not betting on coins, you are betting on people and communities (post:1933603268359320038; post:2105997468903088370). Belief is the most important fundamental in economics (post:1932081604530495925). SPX6900 moved from a trade to the overriding mission: it will flip the stock market and is the next Bitcoin / Bitcoin in 2011 (post:2107189502506934440; post:2113660045653094881; post:2114475743170220039). He wrote that 99.9999999% of memecoins are the literal Devil and only about 20 are good communities (post:2110819059990442175). In the latest phase he argued AGI will destroy jobs and margins, so scarce belief, community and “collective spirituality” become the next 10,000x trade (post:2112900180076818520; post:2114644461963927627).

How they reason and decide
His reasoning changed with the object of his conviction. In the Bitcoin era he used monetary history and store-of-value criteria: security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary policy, distribution and privacy (post:1009801247090774016; post:1009936111089668097), plus market-cap thresholds for SoV/MoE/UoA (post:977587904670322689; post:981255850005336064) and technical analysis such as triangles, NVT, MVRV and VPVR (post:1044657418179923969; post:1052611501813510145).

In the 2018–2023 period he became a macro/on-chain contrarian. He used MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL and stablecoin/total ratio to find DCA zones (post:1544304436171907073; post:1541808252727476224; post:1542538420857315330). He wanted multiple confluences before acting (post:1071095842000515072; post:1090762552102084614), adjusted price for money supply across cycles (post:1539645197872762882), and bought when CT influencers turned bearish while selling when “normie high school friends” asked about XRP/IOTA (post:1164642144482713606). He explicitly said timing and execution matter more than charting or prediction (post:1694835507799228860).

From 2024 onward the analysis became social and community-fixated. He looked for price down while holder count is up (post:1789285793090990305), for coins that survived multiple -70% drops and long sideways accumulation (post:1809917942236172371), and for holder decentralization via BubbleMaps/HolderScan (post:1846573632278917441; post:1809917942236172371). He also trusted gut/aesthetic vibes more than valuation: “The key to finding undervalued memecoins is emotion; they need to make you feel something” (post:1808567795317362927). His latest filter is almost political: opposition is proof of being early (post:2114349869729190170), and “visionaries don’t wait for ‘confirmation’” (post:2113228998759141798). He asks which coin is like Bitcoin in 2011 (post:2028150914197127182) and which will inspire movies (post:2102376622074806289).

Risk attitude
His risk attitude is consistently anti-leverage and anti-trading, though the flavor changed. Early he warned most tokens are scams and will collapse (post:943963229532950529; post:1044809720081633282), while preaching stoic calm in bear markets (post:1055558974387470336). By 2018–2023 he managed exposure in tranches (“Deploying another 20% of my stables (bullet 2 of 5)” post:1594768604939317248) and said no leverage on spot buys (post:1538256335137034241). He warned overexposure makes you angry, impatient and not at peace (post:1694354911385854103).

In the memecoin years risk became mainly about holding through drawdowns and avoiding traps. He said “the most important skill in crypto is to be able to HODL” (post:1787612015965421817) and “95% of your profits will come from doing nothing” (post:1789046659965452453). He warned new launches, presales, cabal bundles and celebrity coins are extraction traps (post:1818729896283095248; post:1808484893229285542), and that any memecoin that did not spend weeks sideways under $10m is a scam (post:1808144100601802966). By 2025–2026 the rule was absolute: no leverage, no margin, no debt, no pair trading, spot/HODL/DCA only (post:1977015649978425759), plus cold storage (post:1963984517116379141) and accepting 80%+ drawdowns as foundation (post:2114789183814328794).

Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)
- **Bitcoin**: bullish from start (post:937401943147864066), remained bullish as base money (post:1067989979433963520), but its role changed. Early it was the endgame; later it is the safe backdrop while the real asymmetric upside moves to SPX6900 and memecoin movements. Late 2026 he still says BTC bull cycle has not begun (post:2112527077840982404) but also says a memecoin will flip Bitcoin (post:2111161113794732298; post:2107189502506934440).
- **Ethereum / smart contract platforms**: early bearish (post:1002274761169358848), later mixed-to-bearish (post:1078415126263414784; post:1594072945387003904). One late isolated bullish post exists (post:1954188518244102157), but ETH never becomes central.
- **Altcoins, ICOs, tech/VC coins**: bearish throughout. Early: pre-sold tokens have already failed (post:1062385597841588224). Later: tech alts are “memecoins with more steps” and VC coins are toxic (post:1810666229973004506; post:1788254986918350891; post:1830349804716912895).
- **Stablecoins**: mixed early (post:967120275379716096), then bearish/cynical about centralized stablecoins (post:1079681084722417665; post:1123323784566587393).
- **DeFi / SocialFi / Solana DeFi**: bullish in the middle period (post:1663522699837157378; post:1683447629185687552), later mostly replaced by the memecoin cult thesis.
- **Memecoin Supercycle**: absent early, became central by 2024. “This cycle is a memecoin cycle” (post:1788932057101369722) and the asset class goes to $1 trillion+ (post:1782843890732548509). It remained the master narrative through 2025–2026 (post:1935774753555726343; post:2089329532230607322).
- **SPX6900 / movement coins**: emerged as a bullish narrative in 2024 (post:1834287525810032772), then became the main thesis: flip the stock market, next Bitcoin, escape vessel for the left-behind 95% (post:2100420046522642908; post:2110073620051669485; post:2012933575662846382).
- **AI / AGI**: first bullish for crypto because AI agents cannot use TradFi rails (post:1653096872209268737). Later the stance split: AI/AGI is bearish for jobs and software margins, but bullish for belief-based assets and financialized spirituality (post:1941910752580735007; post:1968794077727494512; post:2112900180076818520).
- **Trading / gambling**: increasingly bearish. By 2025–2026 trading is “harder than ever” and gambling grinds you to zero (post:1942948976610685386; post:2113581624239731092).

Recurring mistakes they admit
The admission pattern changed. In the 2018–2023 period he was relatively open: “I get a lot of shit wrong” and “Don’t blindly listen; he gets a lot wrong” (post:1232181959454629; post:1166171924642312194). By 2024–2026 he rarely concedes; dips are reframed as tests of conviction, and in the final batch there are no admissions at all—doubt is reframed as other people’s blindness (post:2114349869729190170; post:2114760791350231153).
