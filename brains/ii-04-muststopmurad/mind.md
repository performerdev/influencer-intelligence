# How @MustStopMurad thinks

The single biggest shift in his thinking: he began as a Bitcoin maximalist monetary historian and ended as a memecoin-movement maximalist who says you are not buying coins or tech, you are buying communities and belief. Early on he argued money trends to one winner in a Hayekian free market (post:1055010807153598464). By 2026 he still argues winner-take-most, but now the asset is a tokenized movement.

## Worldview

He started with a monetary lens. Cryptocurrencies are "money-religions" and private central banks competing in a Darwinian free market; there will be 1–4 winners, not 10,000 coexisting ones (post:941500735560343552, post:990701454087413760, post:1009936103783231488). Bitcoin was the best store of value, a digital Neo-Gold standard, not primarily a cheap/fast payment network (post:937401943147864066, post:985335763205283841, post:990242033112682497). Money is a ledger; all money is an Excel spreadsheet with differing guarantees (post:1089237815437668352). Monetization follows collectible → store of value → medium of exchange → unit of account, and gold won because of stock-to-flow, not use value (post:1081056089855143936, post:1077645412897615872, post:1080203704941375493). He argued most ICO-era tokens were financing grifts and pre-sold coins had already failed (post:943963229532950529, post:992589015424602112, post:1062385597841588224).

Even in the Bitcoin years, he was clear that speculation was crypto's biggest use case and most people did not care about decentralization or sovereignty; they wanted to get rich (post:1092191814428360711). The only chart that mattered was market capitalization; caring about anything else was crypto virtue signaling (post:1138946314241265664). That cynical core never left; it became the seed of his memecoin thesis.

The pivot arrives around 2023–2024. He starts saying crypto has three real use cases—money, payments, gambling—plus a long tail of bullshit (post:1783698268423147968). Then the harder turn: memecoins are the purest, most honest corner of crypto, and 99.9% of everything else pretends to be honest but is a gigantic grift (post:1789302483388399975). Tech altcoins are just "memecoins with more steps" and always were (post:1810666229973004506, post:1822664714163212635). It was never about the technology; it was always about the pump (post:1808514638859981193, post:1808535413432205662).

His social explanation is specific and repeated: memecoins are financialized mini-religions/cults that provide hope to lonely, economically stranded young men: "Loneliness is the core fuel behind the memecoin trading phenomenon" (post:1651958132468219907, post:1785393767752544547). AI and automation destroying jobs accelerate the demand for belonging and identity (post:1787795402168623130, post:1819001253197664761). Cults cannot be manufactured top-down; they must emerge bottom-up from the trenches (post:1787549737953976789, post:1787535855218823332). Attention dominates over fundamentals in late-stage fiat capitalism; attention replaces utility (post:1791038381541228839, post:1807817185940889915).

By 2025–2026 the worldview becomes openly spiritual and macro-dystopian: "Belief is the most important fundamental in economics" and the top blockchain use case is creating financial Schelling points where quality people converge (post:1932081604530495925). You are betting on people and communities, not memes or coins (post:1933603268359320038, post:2105997468903088370). Finance and spirituality are merging into one multi-trillion-dollar market (post:1935049402382417995, post:1955288533297426609). AGI will destroy jobs, collapse profit margins, and make human labor obsolete, so scarce intangibles—belief, belonging, community, spirituality—will command a premium (post:1941910752580735007, post:1960069461916180902, post:2112900180076818520, post:2114644461963927627). Assets that do not depend on profits—monetary goods like BTC and cults/insane communities—are the only ones that will not be hypercommoditized to zero (post:1972755956711965146, post:1999574063107264769, post:1968794077727494512).

His final macro wager is utopian and specific: the stock market grew faster than GDP for 50 years, but in the next 50 years GDP may grow faster than the stock market; tokenized movements/communities/religions are a $70T market; SPX6900 will flip the stock market and is Bitcoin in 2011 (post:2113907896048107665, post:2115488825439076802, post:2107189502506934440, post:2113660045653094881). The price of intelligence trends toward the price of compute, then electricity (post:2114402494683988444). The vocal intolerant minority wins: the 3% who love something obsessively beat the 97% who call it delusion (post:2116216736332615876). People want to be part of a story, and flipping the stock market is the grandest story of our time (post:2112623507737559507).

## How they reason and decide

His reasoning has three distinct phases.

**1. Monetary historian / technical analyst (2017–2019).** He evaluated stores of value on explicit criteria: security, censorship-resistance, immutability, disinflationary monetary policy, credibility of monetary policy, distribution, and privacy (post:1009801247090774016, post:1009936111089668097, post:1009354979281338369). He used market-cap thresholds: BTC needed roughly $3T+ to be a store of value, $10T+ to be a good medium of exchange, $15T+ to be a good unit of account (post:977587904670322689, post:981255850005336064). He did technical analysis with descending triangles, NVT, MVRV, VPVR, Heikin Ashi, and RSI (post:1044657418179923969, post:1045358253570490370, post:1052611501813510145). He argued blockchains destroy revenues rather than generate them; decentralization disaggregates fee leech points (post:965820295503339520, post:1020754672569790465).

**2. On-chain / cycle / contrarian trader (2019–2023).** He used MVRV, NVT, CVDD, Reserve Risk, NUPL, and the stablecoin/total ratio to locate bottoming zones and DCA levels (post:1544304436171907073, post:1541808252727476224, post:1542538420857315330). He combined technical confluences—moving averages, diagonals, Gaussian supports, orderbooks, funding—before acting (post:1071095842000515072, post:1124752236008153088). He was contrarian: buy when 65% of Crypto Twitter infliuencers are calling for a crash, sell when normie friends ask about alts (post:1164642144482713606). Cycles were lengthening, and he adjusted prices for money supply to compare across cycles (post:1084199413202018304, post:1539645197872762882, post:1673334040739364867). Timing and execution mattered more than charting or prediction (post:1694835507799228860).

**3. Community / belief / cult investor (2024–2026).** The method is now almost entirely social. He asks whether a coin has already made a critical mass of poor people rich and they are still holding, survived 3+ 70% drops, and has a big inspiring mission (post:2010806643039355006). He looks at holder count up while price is down as a bullish divergence (post:1788166599612022950, post:1789285793090990305). He uses MarketCap-to-Holders, diamond-hand share, holder growth, and top-holder decentralization/HHI to confirm his gut feel (post:1814305122131849630, post:1824825801839427873, post:1832094540196688193). He wants coins that inspire holders to hustle for free; free cult labor is the growth engine (post:1818366712355733965, post:1868626774957187443, post:1897663441013272969).

He says the best trades are the obvious ones (post:1785713782108397909). He does not knife-catch; he waits for sideways rectangles or W shapes, because most reversals are not V-reversals (post:1805187864075891084). When something in financial markets disgusts him at first glance, he treats it as a signal to look closer (post:1807716526407221562). Emotional resonance is the key metric for undervalued memecoins: they need to make him feel something, and he listens to gut feel around symbolic aesthetics and community vibrations (post:1808567795317362927, post:1804813875080052884).

In the final period he categorizes participants into gamblers/traders, holders of consensus assets, and holders of non-consensus assets; only the third outperforms, and it must show years of survival, 80%+ drawdowns, growing community, lore, IRL presence, and DCA culture (post:2114789183814328794, post:2116883161183383847). He reasons the biggest gains come in roughly 10 days a year, so only holders are positioned for them (post:2115436639208067419). Long accumulation precedes a parabola: gradually, then suddenly (post:2117182577056563695, post:2106118634271875534). Silence may mean you are wrong; vehement screaming that you are wrong means you are early (post:2114349869729190170). Visionaries do not wait for confirmation; you must see it before it is there (post:2113228998759141798, post:2115769924274528487).

## Risk attitude

He has never liked leverage. In 2022 he bought spot BTC "no leverage" (post:1538256335137034241). By 2024 that hardens into "Don't use Leverage. Don't use Margin. Don't use Debt. Don't Pair Trade. Stop Trading" (post:1977015649978425759). He frames trading, rotating, and gambling as nearly guaranteed ruin: 99% or 97% of traders lose, and the market is a device for transferring wealth from degens to non-degens (post:2100277714737389753, post:2104629190712860827, post:2113581624239731092). He says the number one mistake in crypto is that people cannot HOLD (post:1940473255808364584, post:2093322467477537261).

Yet he accepts enormous drawdowns. Cult memecoins must have survived multiple -70% drops, and he says if you cannot take a beating you do not deserve to make it (post:1809917942236172371, post:1820158921073455313, post:1830701200687198673). Later he raises the bar to 80%+ drawdowns as a necessary foundation for outperformance (post:2114789183814328794). The real risk in his view is not price volatility but time: a multi-year period of stagnation and boredom, or selling too early (post:1696202072582959546, post:1696202074160087053). He says 95% of profits come from doing nothing, and the most important skill in crypto is the ability to HODL (post:1789046659965452453, post:1787612015965421817).

He is extremely wary of new launches, presales, celebrity coins, cabal bundling, and microcap "dogshit": they are traps designed to extract money, and any memecoin that did not spend weeks trading sideways under $10m is a scam (post:1818729896283095248, post:1808144100601802966, post:1821586375713644692, post:1830245289174688054). From 2025 he adds operational security: put coins in cold storage, do not click links, update MacOS/iOS, and expect phishing as the community grows (post:1963984517116379141, post:1959262878290059549). One unusually self-aware warning: he tells people not to blindly listen because he gets a lot wrong (post:1232181959454629).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

- **Bitcoin as digital gold / base money: bullish throughout.** Early it was "Neo-Gold" and the best store of value (post:937401943147864066, post:989282637813608448). In the 2018–2023 bear he stayed a buyer and DCA'd the 14–19k range (post:1544304436171907073). Later he kept Bitcoin bullish as a monetary good that cannot be hypercommoditized (post:1977465032184340696, post:2051741031830347871), though he briefly called it "levered SPX" that nukes harder in a recession (post:1802002222647021678).

- **Ethereum and smart contract platforms: bearish/mixed, then a late bullish turn.** Early he mocked ETH maximalists and did not accept ETH as a store of value (post:1009841527525466112, post:1002274761169358848). He was still bearish/ETH-relative through 2024 (post:1784955160189374735, post:1805210771736944998). But by the 2025–2026 batch his Ethereum stance flipped bullish (post:1954188518244102157).

- **Altcoins / ICOs / tech altcoins: bearish, but the object changed.** Early he warned most tokens were scams and non-BTC alts had not capitulated (post:943963229532950529, post:1038092379918675968). Later he still called tech altcoins the losing side, but reframed them as "memecoins with more steps" with extra hack/unlock risk (post:1810666229973004506, post:1830349804716912895, post:1846600450188951830). He declared there would be no altseason as people knew it (post:1883194144212103305).

- **Memecoin Supercycle: bullish from 2024 onward.** This is his dominant thesis. He called the memecoin asset class going to $1T+ and lasting 5+ years (post:1782843890732548509, post:1785807491596525730), then a permanent secular shift rather than a fad (post:1810328235751899489, post:1836389109545468102). By late 2026 it becomes "this run will be the biggest and most concentrated in history; the cycle hasn't even begun" (post:2112527077840982404, post:2117216792674255102).

- **Specific cults: POPCAT → GIGA → SPX6900.** He backed POPCAT as the Cat King and prepared to diamondhand it for months (post:1782351137103847731, post:1786516920637747653). Then GIGA became the #1 Solana asset and his target was raised from $15B to $50B (post:1811796083200184340, post:1835365018088456691). By 2025–2026 SPX6900 is the overwhelming obsession: it will flip the stock market, reach $69T as hyperstition, and is the next Bitcoin (post:2100420046522642908, post:2115128468002246820, post:2117279485745561610).

- **AI and AGI: two-sided.** In 2023 he was excited that AI agents with internet access would be mega-bullish crypto because they cannot use TradFi bank accounts or debit cards (post:1653096872209268737). Later he became bearish on AI crypto projects specifically (post:1786354896729456806), but wildly bullish on the social consequences of AGI: AI destroys jobs and margins, making belief-based assets more valuable (post:1941910752580735007, post:2112900180076818520).

- **DeFi / SocialFi / STFX: bullish in 2023, later backgrounded.** He was early on SocialFi and STFX, saying crypto would absorb fintech and DeFi had 1,000x ahead (post:1547251779846406144, post:1683447629185687552, post:1663522699837157378). Memecoins then shoved DeFi aside in his content.

- **Stablecoins: mixed to bearish.** He mocked centralized stablecoins and called the stablecoin/total ratio a useful bottom signal, but treated them as inferior to Bitcoin (post:1079681084722417665, post:1541808252727476224, post:1600228724045008897).

- **Trading / gambling: increasingly bearish.** Early it was "don't fight the trend" (post:1164937989774090241). By the end it is "Stop trading and believe in something"; trading is a road to zero, holding is the only edge (post:2105752918020300964, post:2113994592630817215, post:2116148966828245020).

## Recurring mistakes they admit

In the early/mid period he was willing to admit error. He said "I get a lot of shit wrong" and "don't blindly listen; he gets a lot wrong" (post:1232181959454629, post:1166171924642312194). He built invalidation into calls—"a good scenario/call has an invalidation / 'where am I wrong' attached to it" (post:1696202079549714462).

From the memecoin era onward, explicit admissions mostly disappear. Instead he reframes delay, drawdowns, and criticism as confirmation that he is early: "Hearing people vehemently scream that you are wrong, is how you know that you are early" (post:2114349869729190170). Dips become tests of conviction, not evidence of a mistaken setup. His standard line is that it always takes a little longer than you think, and always goes way higher than you think (post:2117182577056563695).
