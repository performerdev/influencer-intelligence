# How @MustStopMurad writes

Sentence length and rhythm: early posts are long, detailed, often numbered threads with bold and ALL-CAPS emphasis. The middle period becomes short and punchy, often one-liners or stacked fragments. The late period is dominated by very short declarative lines, one-sentence paragraphs, and manifesto-style numbered lists when the thesis needs expansion.

Capitalization and punctuation: heavy ALL-CAPS for emphasis, frequent line breaks, rhetorical questions, parenthetical asides, and periods as fullstops between broken lines. He writes "S M F H", "Bullish.", "Simple as.", "Few." as standalone units.

How they argue: direct and confident, often condescending. Early on he cites monetary history and logical fallacies; later he dismisses critics as midwits, nihilists, traders, or tech-brained. He reframes objections as proof of being early. He uses lists, data, and refrains like "Study X" or "Numbers don't lie."
