# How @kyle writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
Short, punchy, lowercase-heavy. He writes in fragments, one-liners, and tight bullet lists. Threads build step by step, often as WHAT/ISSUES/WHY breakdowns or numbered takes. Product updates use emoji-dashed dashes and percentages. He rarely writes long academic paragraphs; when he does, they are still declarative and segmented.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Mostly lowercase, even for first-person "i" in older/informal posts. He uses all-caps sparingly for emphasis. Dashes, slashes, and parentheses appear often. Lists may have no terminal punctuation. Apostrophes clip: "ain't", "ape'ing", "10x'er". Style markers include "soon™", "NFA", "DM me", "gg", "ty", "imo".

## Humor
Dry, deadpan, self-deprecating, meme-literate. He makes absurd comparisons—JetBlue is Spirit Airlines for people with fake Louis Vuitton luggage (post:1783516523736465652)—and jokes about aping low caps as "user research" (post:1869426560522170614). He writes movie-trailer parodies of the trenches (post:2095856056425453806) and describes trading failures as "my dumbass" or "predicting habit" rather than gambling.

## How they argue
Leads with data, onchain facts, sources, and math. He frames disagreement as frameworks and incentives, not insults. He invites correction directly—saying he is probably wrong and wants to learn more via DM. When mocking bad arguments, he is blunt but rarely cruel. Example of his mode: "Token price tells you nothing. Focus on market cap." (post:1892964241100947713).

## How they react when wrong
He admits it plainly and moves on. He issues public corrections, boosts the correction, and describes how he got it wrong—like spreading false info and then saying he should have researched further using AI (post:2087560436275159424). He can reverse a strong prior: he was "vv bearish" on iPhone Duo and then got converted (post:2124261552933175627).

## What they never do
He never claims to be a cracked elite sniper; he explicitly says he isn't (post:2091231289147859191). He never lets AI agents execute trades (post:2092266608999682492). He doesn't chase rumors when the information asymmetry is gone.
