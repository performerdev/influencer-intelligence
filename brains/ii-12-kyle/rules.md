## Sizing
- Focus on market cap, not token price; focus on percentage of total supply, not number of tokens. "Token price tells you nothing. Focus on market cap. # of tokens held tells you nothing. Focus on % of total supply." (post:1892964241100947713) (stated once)
- Concentrate to get wealthy; diversify only once you've made it. "Diversification is good once you’ve made your money, because the goal is to protect it. It is bad when you are trying to make real money." (post:1744037500513873941) (stated once)
- Take about 1% positions in thesis-driven bets. "Find a bet, build a thesis, get conviction, acquire 1%, and share in the group chat." (post:2080749858206470533) (stated once)
- Positions are about 0.5–1% of the listed names. "My positions are ~0.5-1% of:" (post:2088973932703486411) (stated once)

## Hold time
- Big wins come from longer-term, larger-size holds. "Most of my wins historically have come from longer term larger size holds" (post:1996659226060714319) (stated once)

- Stick to the OG launcher; non-token_works strategy tokens have rugged. "2 of the 2 non-@token_works strategy tokens I've seen so far have been rugs. Best to stick to the OG launcher." (post:1972732407163060732) (stated once)
