## Sizing
- Diversification is dumb. "diversification is dumb" (post:1784298467768070302).
- Keep 5–20% for high-risk fun, hold cash for high-conviction bets. "use 5-20% of your cash for high risk fun shit if you want" (post:1974843290865238200; post:1974032793303986655).
- Concentrate on ~3 assets max and research extremely deeply. "concentrate on ~3 assets max at once and do extreme research" (post:1989047146302394844).
- Double down on winners, not losers. "always better to double down on the winners than to spend that time trying to salvage the losers" (post:1969825357865037910).
- Only hold SOL or angel cheques, 80/20. "I don't like holding anything but SOL or angel cheques in an 80/20" (post:1892638784329519504).
- Raise as little as possible; higher valuation isn't always better. "raise as little as possible" (post:1856157717095649436).

## Hold time
- Expand time horizon during turbulence. "If you're worried, expand your time horizon" (post:1533128448893403138).
- 5–10 year horizon. "if your time horizon for this space isn't at least 5-10 years — you're a tourist" (post:1709629712211829198).
- The biggest edge is a horizon longer than one cycle. "the biggest edge in crypto is having a time-horizon longer than one cycle" (post:1862898721383444540).
- Think in years, not weeks. "all my big wins have been buying distressed assets, working to improve them, and thinking in terms of years vs. weeks" (post:2057807312056701409).
- Hold, chill, and do nothing. "do nothing, chill, research comically deep, take very few swings, size well, and then do nothing & chill again" (post:2096260236021817530).
- "99 of the time, you are better off holding a coin with a strong thesis for a very long time horizon" (post:1974843290865238200).

## Risk management
- Use ledger, exchange, or multisig; avoid custodial risk. "use a ledger, exchange, or multisig" (post:1554879738480656385).
- Avoid protocols without frozen or multisig authorities. "going full goblin mode on protocols without frozen or multisig authorities from now on" (post:1603784814418599938).
- Don't time the market. "you will never be able to reliably \"time\" a market" (post:1749622892776538489).
- Don't use leverage. "ok can you guys like stop using leverage lol" (post:1995308693844566221).
- Gamble only with what you can afford to lose. "gamble all you want and have fun (with what you can afford to lose) — just remember gravity and don't click weird links" (post:1764190945120080070).
- Never use single-signer setup; use multisigs. "never use a single-signer setup for your money. just do not do this. it does not matter if it's a hardware wallet." (post:2087897699341525260).
- Audit opsec: MFA, hardware wallets, hot/cold wallets, don't reuse passwords, never SMS auth. "make sure your self custody setups are well thought out. MFA, hardware wallets, hot/cold wallet setups." (post:2034640944893415639; post:2087897699341525260).
- Don't panic-move funds during fear. "no, do not move your funds no matter what chain youre on" (post:2107996849919701390).
- Don't be a panican. "iv) don't be a panican" (post:1989047146302394844).
