- Buy mispriced assets when the market is asleep at the wheel. "Didn't realize it was as easy as just buying mispriced assets" (post:1716592275466113277)

- Have a default 5–10 year portfolio anchor: SOL, BTC, and one or two other alternatives. "i) You buy SOL, BTC, and 1-2 other alternatives on a 5-10 year time horizon" (post:1766954734919757941)
- DCA into a small number of high-conviction bets until you no longer think they are undervalued. "just estimate what you think something should be valued at and keep DCAing until you believe it is no longer undervalued" (post:1977185927601553460)
- Buy SOL aggressively below a price you believe is a gift. "SOL under 500 is a free gift" (post:1985629614786728332)

## Exit and taking profit
- Don't sell when price is lower; sell when it is higher. "why wouldn't you just sell when the price is up and buy when the price is down" (post:1960309128649134570)
- Do not obsess over micro-timing tops and bottoms while missing the large move. "ct traders … would rather obsess about micro-timing an imaginary top/bottom to squeeze out a marginal 2.7%, while missing on the 1000%" (post:2101137641601450093)
- Never sell SOL; the thesis is long-term. "Ive not sold any SOL and will not sell any. I am serious about trillions" (post:1985622469169000570)

## Sizing
- Diversification is dumb. "diversification is dumb" (post:1784298467768070302)
- Hold mostly SOL or angel cheques, roughly 80/20. "I don't like holding anything but SOL or angel cheques in an 80/20" (post:1892638784329519504)
- Concentrate on about three assets max and do extreme research on them. "iii) concentrate on ~3 assets max at once and do extreme research" (post:1989047146302394844)
- Keep a small cash sleeve for high-risk fun only. "use 5-20% of your cash for high risk fun shit if you want" (post:1974843290865238200, post:1974032793303986655)

## Averaging down
- If you sell when an asset goes down instead of buying more, you should not have bought it. "if you buy an asset and sell it when it goes down instead of buying more, why did you buy it? are you stupid?" (post:2017055162171355423, post:2059967120042021070)
- When a coin you are long falls, treat it as an opportunity to buy more. "oh no an opportunity to buy more of the coins that I said I was bullish on for the past 5 years how terrible grow up" (post:2021679862738772268)
- The right coin is one you are happy to see lower. "the way to know if you bought the right coin is whether you're happy when price goes down" (post:2059575428373791138)

## Hold time
- Expand your time horizon during turbulence. "If you're worried, expand your time horizon" (post:1533128448893403138)
- A horizon under 5–10 years makes you a tourist. "if your time horizon for this space isn't at least 5-10 years — you're a tourist" (post:1709629712211829198)
- Ignore the price chart and focus on qualitative long-term metrics. "thesis: you probably have a massive edge over anyone else in the space if you literally ignore the price chart and focus only on qualitative metrics" (post:1724818966827159849)
- The biggest edge is a time horizon longer than one cycle. "the biggest edge in crypto is having a time-horizon longer than one cycle" (post:1862898721383444540)
- Corrections transfer money from short-term to long-term holders. "corrections are a transfer from the short-term minded to the long-term minded … unstoppable private money is not a trade" (post:1995448446988263439)

## Risk management
- Use a ledger, exchange, or multisig; avoid custodial risk. "use a ledger, exchange, or multisig" (post:1554879738480656385)
- Avoid protocols without frozen or multisig authorities. "going full goblin mode on protocols without frozen or multisig authorities from now on" (post:1603784814418599938)
- Add priority fees dynamically and beat the block median. "you almost certainly have to add priority fees … you need to beat the block median" (post:1747007572266094677, post:1786490530504822968)
- Gamble only with what you can afford to lose, and do not click weird links. "gamble all you want and have fun (with what you can afford to lose) — just remember gravity and don't click weird links" (post:1764190945120080070)
- Stake SOL, because unstaked SOL melts from inflation. "if you're not staking your SOL, it's literally melting in your wallet from inflation" (post:1784426738577010800)
- Cap leverage at max 2x after systemic blowups. "after seeing aster do well … reduced all leverage to max 2x" (post:1976838108663722481)
- Never use a single-signer setup, even a hardware wallet. "never use a single-signer setup for your money. just do not do this. it does not matter if it's a hardware wallet." (post:2083230604217508015)
- Audit opsec: no clicking links, hardware MFA, revoke connected apps. "good time to audit your opsec … use MFA (no SMS, use Yubikey or Authenticator apps), revoke connected apps" (post:2045582183767916789)
- Only risk what you can lose on privacy trades. "do not allocate what you're not willing to lose, this is risky" (post:1973335855977680957)
