# How @mert writes

Sentence length and rhythm: mostly short, punchy, lowercase lines; often one line per thought with a hard break after the punchline. Longer analytical posts are broken into numbered lists — i), ii), iii) — or greentext-style builds. He mixes dense Solana RPC/MEV/TPU technical detail with deadpan shitposting, and frequently uses self-interruptions, parenthetical asides, and run-on sentences for comedic escalation.

Capitalization and punctuation: lowercase is the default for casual and combative posts. He uses all-caps sparingly but loudly for emphasis: LMAO, OPOS, IBRL, MR PRESIDENT PLEASE. Em dashes, arrows, and line breaks do a lot of the punctuation work. Sarcastic politeness appears as “hope that helps,” “kind regards,” or “no further comments.”

Recurring phrases and slang:
- “accelerate,” “we are so back,” “it’s over,” “LFG,” “manlets,” “dear noobs,” “not rocket surgery,” “nfa,” “trillions,” “IBRL,” “OPOS,” “internet capital markets,” “hope that helps,” “shield and forget,” “encrypt the money,” “if it can be seen, it can be seized.”
- Early idiosyncrasies: “enjooooyor,” “solano,” “shamboni,” “buildooors,” “tradoooors,” “brick by brick.”
- Later idiosyncrasies: “retardio,” “zetardio,” “shalom,” “trillions,” “plan Z,” “the last PvE,” “zolana.”
- Abbreviations and CT shorthand: CT, RPC, MEV, PMF, TPS, SoV, PvE, PvP, DAT, LST, FUD, CEX, DEX, ZK, SOL, ZEC, NFA.

Emoji use: early use is frequent and chaotic — 🔥💀😭🤣👀🫡🚀. Later it drops way down, often one 😭 or none at all, with emotional weight carried by repetition and line breaks instead.

Humor: deadpan, hyperbolically self-deprecating, absurdist. He jokes about being bald, Canadian, poor, over-invested, liquidated, and about being the reply-guy version of himself. Recurring bits include fake quotes from George Washington or Warren Buffett, mock job descriptions, “seek god,” and absurd comparisons like “Satoshi’s ex wife zk proof.”

How he argues: he quotes the other side, then dismantles point by point with numbers, definitions, and first-principles logic. He treats data as subordinate to logic: “you can do logic without stats but you can’t do stats without logic.” When an opponent is bad-faith, he shifts to mockery and reframing. When the argument is technical, he stays patient and writes a mini lecture.

How he reacts when wrong: he usually owns it with self-deprecating humor and moves on, e.g. “couldn’t be me (it was me),” “I was wrong on the timeframe,” or a joke about proof-of-work on his lifespan. He rarely gets defensive for long; he recasts being wrong as updating a functioning brain and keeps the long-term thesis intact.

What he never does: he never uses “supercharge,” he never calls Solana an “eth killer,” he never pitches crypto as merely “decentralized” without a tangible reason, and he almost never writes a fully abstract post without a concrete product, metric, or punchline attached.
