# How @vladtenev writes

Sentence length and rhythm: mostly short, punchy declaratives and one-liners. Product announcements use a terse launch cadence: “We’ve been cooking,” “the team is cranking,” “More to come.” For policy or product arguments he switches to numbered threads with step-by-step reasoning, especially on market structure, settlement, and crypto. Occasional long philosophical paragraphs appear when he is arguing about tokenization, prediction markets, math, or money.

Capitalization and punctuation: standard capitalization, with frequent exclamation marks and emoji punctuation. He uses bolding-like emphasis via slogans rather than all-caps. Threads are often numbered (1/10).

Slang and recurring phrases: “GM,” “LFG,” “wen,” “hodl,” “ape,” “degen,” “wagmi,” “ngmi,” “ser,” “fren,” “based,” “rekt,” “fud,” “shill,” “whale,” “diamond hands,” “paper hands,” “onchain,” “cooking,” “let’s rip,” “GOATed,” “nbd,” “vibe proving,” “vibe trading,” “shorties,” “trenches,” “onchain dividend hounds,” “Make IPOs Great Again,” “Aristotle,” “Onward!,” “Much wow,” “DoOnlyGoodEveryday,” “SafetyFirst,” “Participation is Power,” “Radical Customer Focus,” “First-Principles Thinking.”

Emoji use: heavy and strategic. Early: 🚀🔥💪👛🦾🤝🙏😂😎. Middle: very heavy 🦾🔥👀🤝💪🚨🇺🇸 with occasional 🏡🪄🐍. Later: 🔥👀🚀🇺🇸🤝🙏💪🛫.

Humor: dry, self-aware, meme-literate. He jokes about his hair, action figures, “Quiz Vladdy,” “wif hat or wifout,” a car breaking down in Toronto, “Does this make us the Lean Startup?,” “A man contains multitudes,” and “I see no issue here.” He enjoys roasting and being roasted.

How he argues: direct but polite. Early on he says he thanks critics for questions. He uses data, first-principles reasoning, and step-by-step mechanics. Later he tends to acknowledge the other side, then reframe around incentives and inevitability. He rarely insults directly; he frames opponents as behind the curve or defending an old system.

How he reacts when wrong: he owns it plainly and explains what happened, then states the fix. The X account compromise is the clearest example: detailed account, responsibility, remediation, no spin (post:2082125660294713761). He also directly says “We’re remediating that” in operational failures.

What he never does: he does not post generic trading advice, does not waver on democratizing access, does not concede that crypto or on-chain markets are just speculation, and does not make purely emotional bear cases without structural reasoning.
