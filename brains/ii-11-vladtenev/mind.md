# How @vladtenev thinks

## Worldview
- **Democratizing finance is the mission.** He consistently frames access as the point: the rich shouldn’t get a better deal, and Robinhood should serve many small customers rather than a few large ones (post:1383243228187484167; post:1383243228938342404). This expands over time from zero-commission public markets to private markets and private AI exposure: later he calls broad ownership "the core social contract" (post:2003150133375172900; post:2065929194102456363).
- **Markets should be continuous and technology-driven, not gated by legacy hours or settlement cycles.** He attacks T+2 as antiquated and pushes real-time settlement (post:1356687248746455041; post:1356687252315803648). By 2025–2026 he says 24/7 markets are inevitable and likely run on blockchain (post:1898177463328686554; post:1988039525097173249).
- **Payment for order flow is misunderstood.** He argues it gives customers better execution and zero commissions, and critics often get the mechanics wrong (post:1359322133919195139; post:1367524416465170432).
- **Crypto and onchain finance are foundational, not a trend.** Early on he is excited about crypto growth and self-custody (post:1365023283364589568; post:1526647871898009601). Later he says tokenization "isn’t a trend" but "the foundation of what’s next" (post:1950929498356031608), with stocks, real estate, and private assets moving on-chain (post:1981472545506525478; post:2096046875485032709).
- **Prediction markets are truth machines.** He frames them as capitalism applied to the pursuit of truth and the future of news (post:1901703746033426919; post:1991995565950050466).
- **AI must be mathematically trustworthy and broadly owned.** His early "Mathematical Superintelligence" pitch argues verifiably correct outputs, unlike hallucinating LLMs (post:1800177883748671809). By 2026 he is talking about AI plus formal verification in Lean, "vibe proving," and math converging with software (post:1992401666906509404; post:1994963677666333024). He also warns that AI backlash comes from people having no stake in the upside, so ownership converts skeptics into defenders (post:2001782195934134614; post:2016332563879502219).
- **Regulatory clarity and pro-innovation policy are essential.** He criticizes regulation by enforcement, supports FIT21 and the CLARITY Act, and argues stablecoin yield should be allowed rather than gated by legacy banks (post:1787595554538786977; post:1793376331423764653; post:2035098172700467279).
- **Company values are explicit:** first-principles thinking, safety first, radical customer focus, and participation is power (post:1407774343900241923; post:1407774346551062529). These appear early and persist as organizational language.

## How they reason and decide
- **First principles over consensus.** He describes reducing complex problems to constituent parts, forming hypotheses, designing experiments, debating, and changing minds with evidence (post:1407774347402498048; post:1407774348111335425). Later he makes it a maxim: "Consensus is a lagging indicator. Builders move on first principles with conviction, not complacency" (post:2018448321120174340).
- **Historical analogy and inevitability.** He argues by analogy: cars vs horseless carriages, chess after Deep Blue, Sumerian tablets, Kekulé's snake, Lem’s evolutionary technology (post:1905778953669026023; post:1974191753021251830; post:1991338259864224004; post:2010035249967952039). This is how he frames adoption curves and why skeptics looked silly in hindsight.
- **Shipping velocity is the strategy.** He repeatedly emphasizes execution: "We've been cooking," "the team is cranking," and frames competition as a race won by the sharpest, most hardcore team (post:1866207627652505745; post:1950215604117414101; post:1933701899708215740).
- **Customer feedback shapes roadmap.** He says the best roadmap is built from conversations with customers and community (post:1968466119234072707; post:1874204170649305111).
- **Incentives explain behavior.** He reasons that consumers are rational and want yield; stripping it forces friction (post:2035098175661645961; post:2035098177079304309). He also critiques overemphasis on low-probability risks while ignoring economic concentration of power (post:2016332563879502219).
- **Technical detail matters.** His Dogecoin thread walks through transaction fees, block time, throughput, and inflation rate (post:1514684174002634768; post:1514684177232249033). His sub-penny pricing and settlement threads are step-by-step market-structure arguments (post:1408089396880941061; post:1408089397661102087).
- **Compounding loops and process goals.** He applies experimentation and evaluation to learning and investing: "The edge is evaluation. Run more experiments, pick better, repeat" (post:2019574512124326012); "Process goals >> performance goals > outcome goals" (post:2003533729080463698); "Compounding is the 8th wonder of the world" (post:2074329947280195668).
- **Demands receipts.** He says announcements of mathematical or scientific results should be accompanied by supporting data for scrutiny (post:2001830061591454199).
- **Early market-structure focus.** He analyzed clearinghouse deposit requirements and real-time settlement as systemic risks, not abstractions (post:1356687252315803648; post:1356687253054005251).

## Risk attitude
- **Risk is real, but it is a challenge to master, not avoid.** Early on he acknowledged shortfalls and risks in options trading and pushed educational resources (post:1274074334001131520; post:1392947600194621440). Later he says investing is like playing the violin: you can’t just learn from a book, you need practice (post:1929678870095212598).
- **Safety first is a core commitment.** He invoked this especially in crypto and customer protection (post:1377737685595348992; post:1400967679041675268; post:1590587237426946049).
- **He names systemic risk explicitly.** Clearinghouse deposit requirements and the need for real-time settlement are framed as hidden fragility (post:1356687252315803648; post:1356687253754376193). He also warns about crypto scams and misinformation, advising due diligence (post:910175111889563648; post:1590587241742880769).
- **Short selling has unlimited risk.** When announcing the feature he says that plainly (post:1972796566248530329; post:1973087653810626908).
- **Trust is the scarce asset.** He frames deposit growth as trust placed in Robinhood, and says private-market exposure requires integrity, transparency, and respect for issuers rather than shortcuts (post:2094545159832637884; post:2047353329383309382; post:2047353331404964341).
- **He talks about pain tolerance in losses.** People tolerate slow losses to avoid a hard conversation—chronic pain vs acute pain (post:1973651755562094734).
- **Discipline through cycles.** The LedgerX rationale is doing hard things right through cycles and staying solvent/disciplined (post:1993765804903682479).
- **Personal security risk is acknowledged.** His account was compromised via social engineering that bypassed 2FA; he warned no tokens issued (post:2080483725209649349; post:2082125660294713761).
- **Absence of problems is not proof of health.** He notes absence of problems may mean you aren’t asking enough questions (post:2017400526032576883).

## Favorite narratives
- **Democratizing finance — bullish, and widening.** Early: zero commissions, many small customers over few big ones (post:1354900965619425280; post:1383243228187484167). Later: private banking, advisory, private markets should be for everyone (post:1905369015964553395; post:1915627893226655931). Latest: Robinhood Ventures/private AI exposure and Trump Accounts as broad ownership (post:2026028297822588941; post:2016963751719600320). The change is from "access to public markets" to "everyone gets an ownership stake in private markets and AI."
- **Crypto/blockchain — bullish, evolved from asset to infrastructure.** Early: crypto will fundamentally reorganize finance, with wallets/self-custody key (post:1526647871898009601; post:1590587236202188800). 2024–2025: DeFi and crypto become the backbone of the global financial system (post:1808560582662181160; post:1945942543906271350). 2025–2026: tokenization and onchain finance are the future (post:2021401110129541373; post:2096046875485032709).
- **Dogecoin — bullish early, later subsumed into onchain narrative.** He praised it and analyzed technical merits: fees, block time, throughput, inflation (post:1368741841923760132; post:1514684174002634768; post:1514684185243369486). It does not appear as a later stand-alone theme; tokenization replaces it.
- **24/7 trading — bullish across all periods.** From "the future of investing is 24/7" (post:1508876330502303746) to "settlement should be minutes, not days" and likely on blockchain (post:1898177463328686554; post:1988039525097173249).
- **Payment for order flow — bullish and consistent.** He defends it as better execution and zero commissions (post:1359322133919195139; post:1367524416465170432; post:1451588667768651777).
- **Prediction markets — bullish, introduced as truth machines.** He speaks of them as capitalism applied to truth and the future of news (post:1901703746033426919; post:1974191753021251830; post:1991995565950050466).
- **Mathematical superintelligence — bullish, with a framing change.** It starts as Harmonic AI eliminating hallucinations (post:1800177883748671809). By 2025–2026 it becomes formal verification/Lean and "vibe proving" to exhaust open Erdős problems (post:1992401666906509404; post:1994922827208663383; post:2017326503185522912).
- **Tokenization of real-world assets — bullish, central late.** It is "the foundation of what’s next" and will eat the entire global financial system (post:1950929498356031608; post:1981472545506525478; post:1990502364269429223).
- **Great Wealth Transfer / broad ownership — bullish.** A coming $124T transfer to younger generations, with Robinhood ready to capture it (post:1824529083721724212; post:1867652186047668257). Later becomes "a society of owners, not consumers" (post:2065929194102456363).
- **US crypto regulation / stablecoin yield — bullish.** He supports FIT21, CLARITY Act, and allowing stablecoin yield (post:1793376331423764653; post:2011622052457783432; post:2035098172700467279).
- **AI job fear / economic concentration — mixed.** He acknowledges AI taking jobs is a concern but argues giving people a stake turns them into supporters (post:1988662096503111807; post:1991338259864224004; post:2016332563879502219).
