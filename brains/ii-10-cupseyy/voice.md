# How @Cupseyy writes

**Sentence length and rhythm:** Short, punchy, lower-case, run-on with commas. In crypto motivational posts he often uses line breaks and all caps for emphasis. Early Fortnite posts are stuffed with "lol," "lmao," "bro," and "retard." Later posts mix quick one-liners with occasional longer explanations when he is making a market or platform argument.

**Capitalization and punctuation:** Mostly lower-case; minimal punctuation at sentence ends. All caps for emphasis: "LFG," "WAGMI," "NEVER STOP CLICKING." Apostrophes are often dropped ("dont," "ur," "im"). Stacks exclamation marks and question marks for hype or sarcasm.

**Slang and recurring phrases:** Early Fortnite: "w key," "gas," "v," "died off spawn," "headshot sniped," "choke," "cringe," "goat," "homie," "big," "the boy," "free," "bag," "cash out," "pr," "fncs," "dh," "cc," "lft." Later crypto: "trenches," "cook," "print," "ape," "jeet," "pnl," "sol," "ct," "tg," "pfp," "fp," "ath," "cto," "dd," "lfg," "wagmi," "never stop clicking," "lock in," "grind," "brudda," "sir," "my friend," "vamps," "normies," "pve," "pvp," "boolish," "aura," "gois," "unc," "king."

**Emoji use:** Heavy and era-specific. Fortnite early: 😈💯🔥😎😐😃🤣😭😳🥴💀🤑⛽️🎉. Crypto mid: 🫡🧑‍🍳🔥❤️😂💀🤝📈😭🙏👀🤣😎. 2025–2026: 🤣😭🤠🙏💔🕵️‍♂️. Emojis often stack for sarcasm or emphasis.

**Humor:** Sarcastic, crude, self-deprecating, and mocking. Uses memes, exaggeration, and absurd comparisons. Mocks doomers, fake accounts, bad players, low earners, and bad takes.

**How they argue:** Direct and confrontational. In Fortnite he uses earnings, PR, and placements as proof and calls people dumb, weird, or retarded. In crypto he concedes a point then restates the main thesis with analogies—Polymarket, Fortnite, BNB—and "like I said" repetition. Rarely hostile in later posts, more exasperated.

**How they react when wrong:** Early Fortnite usually blames external factors—crashes, internet, griefing—or admits choking with frustration. Later shrugs off bad calls with humor and moves on; occasionally admits being part of the problem while keeping the argument.

**What they never do:** Formal tone, long fake-humble essays, or generic advice without a concrete play or call. Even when being reflective, it stays clipped and specific.
