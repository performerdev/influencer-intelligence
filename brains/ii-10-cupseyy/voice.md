# How @Cupseyy writes

**Sentence length and rhythm:** Short, punchy, mostly lowercase. In the Fortnite era posts are run-on and low-punctuation; in the trenches era he uses more line breaks and all-caps for emphasis. One-liners and quick replies are common, especially later.

**Capitalization and punctuation:** Lowercase dominant, with selective all-caps for hype or emphasis. Early posts often skip periods; later posts use periods after short sentences but still lowercase. Quotes and emoji sometimes replace punctuation entirely. Uses ellipses and multiple question marks for emphasis in 2025–2026.

**Emoji use:** Heavy but era-specific. Early: 😈💯🔥😎😐😃🤣😭😳🥴💀🤑⛽️🎉. 2020–2021: lots of repeated emojis like 💯💯💯, 😭😭, 🥳🥳, 🎉🎉, 👀👀. NFT era: 🤣😭💯🔥😈🙏👀🥱🤪💀. Trenches era: 🫡🧑‍🍳🔥❤️😂💀🤝📈😭🙏👀🤣😎. Late era: 🤣😭🤠🙏💔❤️🕵️‍♂️. He often uses emojis as a punchline or to soften/hype a take.

**Humor:** Sarcastic, self-deprecating, and insulting. Early humor mocks his own misfortune and others' bad plays. NFT era adds crude jokes about weight, skill, and earnings. Trenches era is more playful and motivational, joking about losses, bottoms, and fake versions of himself. Uses exaggeration and gaming references for comedy.

**How they argue:** Direct and blunt. Calls bad takes out, uses earnings/PR/PnL as proof, calls people retards, drones, fuckboys, or weird. Doesn't back down easily. In later era tries to stay positive but is still dismissive of critics. Uses analogies like Fortnite FNCS griefing or CZ/BNB to make points.

**How they react when wrong:** Early on he usually blames external factors—game crashes, internet, griefing, RNG—or admits he choked but with frustration. NFT/crypto era he rarely admits fault, deflects, sometimes says "my bad" and moves on quickly. Trenches era he acknowledges losses or mistakes briefly, then focuses on moving forward and learning: "then you stop thinking about it and move on to the next." Sometimes uses humor: "wait im tripping" or "gg we are going lower."

**What they never do:** He almost never writes a long formal paragraph; posts are one-liners or short bursts. He rarely uses standard capitalization. He never softens an insult with "no offense"—if he thinks you're weird or a retard he says it. He never stops claiming stats or PnL as the trump card. He doesn't dwell on losses; he says move on.
