# How @Cupseyy thinks

### Worldview

Cupseyy starts as a West Fortnite controller player. His early posts are dominated by in-game frustration: solos are "the worst, most RNG-based game mode" and duos/trios are better because you can recover from mistakes (post:1223062951952699392, post:1223064044149411840, post:1292607052154654724). He says aim assist is overpowered and unfair, especially on ARs and SMGs, even though he is a controller player himself (post:1250952120309649408, post:1251925497463930886). The game, in his view, is full of broken mechanics, heavy snipers, mythic weapons, boogie bombs, crash pads, and sharks (post:1248408644439302145, post:1260009820632911872, post:1305926675431960576). He also supports Trump in 2020, arguing both candidates are bad but Trump is better and defending Trump against racism accusations (post:1323881724074512384, post:1324060917550718978, post:1324067416574431235).

By late 2020 into 2021 his worldview hardens around earnings and PR as the only proof of worth. He thinks the Fortnite competitive scene is controlled by top players, especially on West, where Ark runs scrims and bans anyone who contests or kills him (post:1404309781762949125, post:1404318774824783873, post:1404676582749487105). He dismisses people by their lack of earnings: "No earnings no 2k or" (post:1332969266317025281). The grind, he says, is often a waste of time unless you are making real money; he considers quitting multiple times (post:1332534918329602048, post:1397823338400161793, post:1321641393274703873). At the same time, crypto enters his worldview as a path to wealth: he jokes about going back in time to buy BTC at $0.10 and bets on Dogecoin (post:1355333418792275971, post:1399542940200886274, post:1407393865410105346).

From mid-2021 to 2022 he mostly leaves daily Fortnite behind. He says Fortnite's newer seasons and loot pools are bad and the game is dying; old loot pool would make it fun again (post:1480775143743918082, post:1480777609709383681, post:1520634596412428288). He calls people who grind Fortnite all day losers and says he plays tournaments only and doesn't take the game seriously anymore (post:1423002002099998730, post:1504330413224779778, post:1446912880205615105). His main conviction becomes Solana and NFTs. He says Solana is the alpha chain, ETH gas is too expensive, and SOL is where the money is (post:1479920160202891268, post:1530328789699309568). He goes from minting his first NFT in December to being up thousands within weeks (post:1481382426660130816, post:1484119834128367618, post:1493749363234852874). He also makes crude, fatphobic statements during this period (post:1447658426784354306, post:1457153815220609032).

From 2022 through 2025 he becomes a trenches/memecoin trader. He believes the trenches, specifically pump.fun and memecoin trading, are the best place to make money, and that grinding 12–16 hours a day is the path to generational wealth (post:1808348461739241721, post:1808930111254585656, post:1814052066035204143, post:1817318317553566144). Hard work and consistency beat talent: anyone can make it if they put in the time (post:1813008605974831276, post:1815433231304433828, post:1913290594769912301). He shifts to quality over quantity—buying fewer coins and holding longer (post:1825576040523247882, post:1829231748791841071). He also becomes openly anti-copytrading, telling people to track wallets and do their own research instead (post:1936807462570766708, post:1936797725896368200). He sees CT as full of hate, ragebaiting, and engagement farming, and says he prefers good vibes and community (post:1942976419073593790, post:1943003193107190037).

In 2025–2026 his worldview sharpens around platform responsibility. He argues memecoin platforms like pump.fun should market their own product instead of relying on outsiders; the only outside marketing for memes is negative or misinformation (post:2000369423019045115, post:2000375278636569006, post:2000376666967998785). He calls prediction markets like Polymarket and Kalshi "crypto and gambling, just marketed in a friendly way," and says that is the kind of marketing memes need (post:2000389314996507084, post:2000392113633345956). He believes platforms that make billions from traders reinvest almost nothing back into the market (post:2001085427604972020). He argues founder/chain support matters enormously for memecoins: CZ and He Yi spawned 9-figure runners on BNB just by tweeting, and when they stopped, BNB memes died (post:2021579881213997269, post:2021581542070628411, post:2021583167493201922).

### How they reason and decide

The most consistent reasoning pattern is status-based evaluation. In Fortnite he uses placements, PR, and earnings as the main measure of skill and worth when arguing with others (post:1307157976772468736, post:1317944871932596224, post:1321217609908555776). He carries this into NFT and crypto: he judges players and traders by earnings, PnL, and floor price rather than reputation or ego (post:1419949568549027840, post:1419950959627837441, post:1423142429666983944). When forming teams he evaluates mechanical skill, game sense, and comms (post:1331701803247955972, post:1331703052286500870).

He reasons through pattern recognition and direct observation. In NFTs he evaluated projects by floor movement, sales volume, and whether the team delivered, calling out slow rugs and cash grabs (post:1527363484941332490, post:1551003025635934209, post:1541147521455628288). In the trenches he scans pump.fun manually for hours daily, looking for early entries and narratives (post:1808348484061270322, post:1813243389531361377, post:1814413340690567555). He uses Twitter and news as trade ideas—Elon tweets, exchange posts—and buys quickly with notifications on (post:1813258601785667751, post:1813261616986595376, post:1827451656029544909).

He also uses analogy heavily, especially gaming analogies for crypto. He compares PvP/vamp dynamics in memecoins to teams griefing each other in Fortnite FNCS: everyone loses (post:2009853057614684297). He uses historical precedent, like CZ/BNB memes, to argue founder support drives liquidity and that it died only when they stopped (post:2021579881213997269, post:2021583167493201922). He questions proposed fixes by asking about second-order consequences—"how can vamps be solved without creating other issues?" (post:2009840426325553271). In markets he reads sentiment from gut feel and on-chain activity rather than technical analysis (post:2082278366556926293).

His decision style shifts with era. Early on he believed W-keying aggressive play was necessary to place in cash cups even if it felt braindead (post:1246110564788531201, post:1256415177538502657). In crypto he preaches adaptation: market conditions change and traders must adapt; different styles work in different markets (post:1950549675360297252, post:1924459476842910029). He treats Twitter as a trading journal, learning from mistakes and continuously trying to improve (post:1825576040523247882, post:1853059497704448020).

### Risk attitude

In Fortnite he is emotionally exposed to bad outcomes. He frequently talks about dying off spawn and losing points early, which ruins tournaments (post:1286907159343706112, post:1291549981057351681, post:1308227924642926595). He admits choking under pressure in solos, shaking and making dumb plays (post:1266528401969430528, post:1286911434996228100, post:1292551560602488833). He blames losses on external factors: game crashes, internet/packet loss, griefing, RNG, broken items, bad drop spots (post:1208846950717513728, post:1216184420429783042, post:1258571641447002115). He is willing to spend money on controllers and PC parts but gets frustrated when they break (post:1300801043308085250, post:1284032853567660032, post:1284169521444642817). When things go badly—bans, crashes, bad luck—he considers quitting the season (post:1256384492547256322, post:1321641393274703873).

In crypto his risk appetite is concentrated and conviction-driven. He goes all-in on Solana: "All of my net worth in sol rn" and later panics when it drops 50% (post:1505709615178297345, post:1524504653513117696). He holds through drawdowns rather than selling at a loss: "Same I'm not pulling out any sol till it goes back up" (post:1484663947709935619, post:1527361533004910592). He takes profits on NFTs but regrets paperhanding when they moon (post:1501867434164195335, post:1519938174461505536). He bought an NFT for 9k and calls it crazy to think about (post:1493784405327769610). He also worries about taxes on SOL withdrawals (post:1522020359616172033).

In the trenches he develops a more professionalized emotional risk stance. He acknowledges losses and down days but maintains a positive mindset and moves on (post:1819398198231793695, post:1819398533776372205, post:1853059865138139345). He warns about buying too many coins in thin markets and suggests being more selective (post:1835727507917611452, post:1835738224376885647). He shares his own experience of getting drained for $500k and warns others (post:1967955259512574345, post:1967957319276441671). He also says losses are your own fault even if someone else told you to buy (post:1921716782743634043). Structural risk appears too: he frames PvP/vamps as able to destroy positions in seconds (post:2009840426325553271).

### Favorite narratives

**Aim assist — mixed, changed over time.** Early he says aim assist is overpowered and wants it nerfed or vaulted even as a controller player (post:1250952120309649408, post:1251925497463930886). By late 2020 he defends controller players as unfairly targeted and hypes "controller on top" (post:1265185956052770817, post:1266386021790281729). By 2021–2022 he is arguing controller takes more skill than KBM, while also saying KBM is the better input (post:1433516753556934673). The shift is from "AA is broken" to "controller is the harder input, leave us alone."

**Solos vs trios — bearish.** Hates solos from the start: worst mode, most RNG, cannot recover from mistakes (post:1223062951952699392, post:1223064044149411840, post:1292607052154654724, post:1294836162045992966). He is relieved when trios return.

**Fortnite competitive state — mixed, then bearish.** Early he is frustrated but still grinding and trying to place (post:1266759581750812677, post:1355783144302018563). By 2021–2022 he says the loot pools are bad and the game is dying (post:1480775143743918082, post:1480777609709383681). He says he only plays tournaments and doesn't take the game seriously anymore (post:1446912880205615105, post:1504330413224779778).

**Controller meta — bullish early, then nuanced.** He briefly celebrates aim assist buffs (post:1305624429934780417) and says controller is on top (post:1265185956052770817). Later he dismisses KBM players who complain about controller box jumps (post:1406388743578267658, post:1406394599321575426).

**Crypto and Solana — bullish, long-standing.** From Dogecoin bets (post:1355333418792275971) to all-in on Solana (post:1505709615178297345) to trenches grinding (post:1808348461739241721), his conviction grows. By 2025–2026 he frames Solana and memecoins as still the place to be (post:1607994901308018688, post:1612588646162628610).

**NFTs — bullish then selective.** He is long Taiyo Robotics, calling it a no-brainer hold and the best high-floor project (post:1506111775485403141, post:1551380648820019200). He is bullish on DeGods, Okay Bears, and Froots NFT (post:1518984226925776896, post:1519938174461505536, post:1552091610267148289). He is bearish on Mindfolk and Boryoku as obvious dumps (post:1551375759326883840, post:1551385141649387521).

**Pump.fun/memecoins — bullish as money-maker, bearish on platform marketing.** He believes the trenches are the best place to make money (post:1808348461739241721) but argues platforms like pump.fun should market their own product instead of relying on others (post:2000369423019045115, post:2000375278636569006).

**Prediction markets — bullish.** He sees Polymarket and Kalshi as crypto and gambling marketed in a friendly way, which he respects as a model (post:2000389314996507084, post:2000392113633345956).

**Creator fee gating and launch fees — bullish.** He says creator fees should be gated/approved only for devs and CTOs, and launching should cost fees to reduce vamps (post:2009841352406905196, post:2009841898626932844).

**Founder/chain support for memes — bullish.** He argues CZ and He Yi spawned 9-figure runners on BNB just by tweeting and supporting coins (post:2021579881213997269, post:2021581542070628411, post:2021583167493201922).

**Magic Eden — bearish.** He criticizes Magic Eden heavily (post:1530328789699309568, post:1551003025635934209, post:1572649606898647040).

**Crypto Twitter — bearish.** He repeatedly calls CT full of hate, ragebaiting, and engagement farming (post:1942976419073593790, post:1943003193107190037).
