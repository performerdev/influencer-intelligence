- Match platform size to capital size. “The bigger the capital the more important the platform” (post:2099931203726094564)

## Risk management
- Only invest money you can afford to lose. “Don't invest with money you can't lose” (post:1287144219576954880)
- Ask the zero-case question before sizing. “ask: what is the worst case scenario? if it goes to 0, can you survive? keep your life style?” (post:1924716732763291707)
- In bull markets, apply risk management; if everything went to 0 and your life wouldn’t be ok, reduce by half. “In bull markets, exercise risk management. If everything went to 0, will your life still be ok? If no, you invested too much. Reduce it by half and ask again. Don't over invest. (Not financial advice)” (post:1552967284540542977)
- Don’t day trade unless you are a pro; don’t invest rent money. “If you are not a pro trader, don't day trade, invest for the longer term, only with money that you won't need for the next few years.” (post:1347115242396946433)
- Don’t chase high yields. “Don't chase high yields. I have a feeling this tweet will become relevant again in a few years time too.” (post:1610313080029810690)
- Never use a token you created as collateral; don’t borrow if you run a crypto business. “Two big lessons: 1: Never use a token you created as collateral. 2: Don’t borrow if you run a crypto business.” (post:1590103159506341888)
- Avoid businesses/exchanges/projects that are unprofitable, survive by selling their own tokens, offer high incentives for locking tokens, have a large total supply but small circulating supply, or involve loans. “FTX aside, avoid businesses/exchanges/projects that: - are not profitable (musical chairs) - survive by selling their own tokens - give high incentives for locking your tokens - have a large total supply, but only a small circulation supply - involves loans Stay #SAFU 🙏” (post:1591098313591652352)
- Use 2FA and never click links from SMS; always go directly to the site. “Use 2FA.” (post:1478411400213147652); “NEVER click on links from SMS! Always go to via a bookmark or type it in.” (post:1489545612718579715)
- When buying a hardware wallet or new software wallet, wait a couple of weeks before moving meaningful funds. “leave it for a couple of weeks before moving any meaningful amount of funds to it” (post:2108561409642574285)
- No software or wallet is 100% safe; split funds across a few wallets to reduce single-wallet bug risk. “How to mitigate? Split your funds in a few wallets maybe? This has a different set of risks. Nothing is 100%.” (post:2083446448461606930)
- Choose self-custody only if you can keep the seed phrase safe; otherwise use the largest exchange with staying power. “Pro tip: Self custody if you know how to keep your seedphrase safe (@trustwallet) Or use the largest exchange (@binance) with staying power.” (post:2081316675383910587)
- A balanced custody approach is probably best. “A balanced approach is probably best.” (post:2084531849515131231)
- Don’t ape into every meme coin based on his tweets. “if you are going to ape into every meme coin people create based on my random tweets, you are almost guaranteed to lose money.” (post:2010961732597137482)
