## Risk management
- Use 2FA. “Use 2FA.” (post:1478411400213147652) (stated once)
- Do not over-invest; if zero changes your life, reduce by half. “In bull markets, exercise risk management. If everything went to 0, will your life still be ok? If no, you invested too much. Reduce it by half and ask again. Don't over invest. (Not financial advice)” (post:1552967284540542977) (stated once)
- Do not day trade unless you are a professional. “If you are not a pro trader, don't day trade, invest for the longer term, only with money that you won't need for the next few years.” (post:1347115242396946433) (stated once)
- Learn TP/SL if you trade. “Crypto is volatile. Remember to exercise prudent risk management when trading. If you don't know what TP/SL means, you should learn about it.” (post:1716835009515126921) (stated once)
- Never use your own token as collateral; do not borrow if you run a crypto business. “Two big lessons: 1: Never use a token you created as collateral. 2: Don’t borrow if you run a crypto business. Don't use capital 'efficiently'. Have a large reserve. Binance has never used BNB for collateral, and we have never taken on debt. Stay #SAFU.🙏” (post:1590103159506341888) (stated once)
- Avoid unprofitable projects and high-yield lockups. “FTX aside, avoid businesses/exchanges/projects that: - are not profitable (musical chairs) - survive by selling their own tokens - give high incentives for locking your tokens - have a large total supply, but only a small circulation supply - involves loans Stay #SAFU 🙏” (post:1591098313591652352) (stated once)

- Use unique email aliases per exchange. “Use a unique email alias for each exchange (SimpleLogin, Apple Hide My Email, etc).” (post:2115304943934672928) (stated once)
- Avoid SMS 2FA. “Avoid SMS 2FA.” (post:2115304943934672928) (stated once)
- Do not click email links or download attachments, even from trusted people. “Do NOT click links in emails. Do NOT download attachments. Even from people you trust” (post:2115516716331536682) (stated once)
- Do not publicize holdings or tie large balances to your home address. “Don't publicize your holdings, and keep large balances out of anything tied to your home address if you can.” (post:2115304943934672928) (stated once)
