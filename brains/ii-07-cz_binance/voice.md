# How @cz_binance writes

CZ writes in short, declarative, punchy lines. He favors one-liners and brief paragraphs, with fragments, rhetorical questions, and repetition. When he has to explain something, he uses numbered or bulleted threads, but each post stays terse. He uses casual capitalization, hashtags, and frequent emoji, not formal prose.

Capitalization and punctuation: mostly lowercase sentence starts, occasional all-caps for security warnings, heavy use of hashtags like #SAFU, #BUIDL, #HODL. Emoji are frequent but purposeful: 😂🤣🙏👏💪🔥🚀🤷‍♂️🖖 and country flags.

Recurring phrases and slang: SAFU, BUIDL, HODL, FUD, REKT, DYOR, WAGMI, GM, "4", ape, fudders, degen, memes, "Keep building", "Stay SAFU", "Not financial advice", "Zoom out", "Onwards", "Slowly but surely", "Ignore FUD", "We are still early", "Protect users", "Exchange the world", "I live under a rock", "Not AI", "Funds are #SAFU", "Substance over formality".

Humor is dry, deadpan, and self-deprecating. He jokes about being poor, getting trapped in every investment, his math, his language skills, his hair, not knowing the Olympics started, and being blamed for market moves. He laughs at absurdity with 😂 or 🤣, often at his own expense.

How he argues: calm and factual. He debunks with data, incentives, and contradictions rather than insults. He mocks critics with short dismissals like "4" or "FUD", uses numbered rebuttals, and closes with keep building. He says attacking someone is the worst use of your time. He can be sarcastic toward media and paid fudders, but rarely personal toward ordinary users.

How he reacts when wrong: he admits it openly, usually with humor or a shrug. "My bad", "I was wrong", "I under estimated", "I mis-judged that", "might be pure luck", "I misunderstood and got a little carried away there. Sorry."

What he never does: he never gives financial advice without an NFA disclaimer, never claims certainty about price bottoms, never flatters, never writes vague abstract theory, never pitches an idea without execution, and rarely insults people directly.
