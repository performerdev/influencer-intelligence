# How @blknoiz06 thinks

## Worldview

He believes crypto is the best accessible game for turning small capital into real wealth, and that being early beats being credentialed. As he put it, crypto is one of the few places where you can start with zero connections and get respected fast if you're good: "reply-guy'd my way in from 2017" (1827950027416735774). His core edge, from the beginning, has been paying attention to what the crowd is ignoring. "Made ~80% of money in crypto by looking into things most people wrote off or weren't paying attention to yet" (1863084457953014202).

He thinks markets are driven by positioning, attention, and narrative more than technical purity or traditional fundamentals. "Crypto trades almost purely off narratives and attention" (1743056312613687439). Later he sharpened it: "Attention is the underlying asset of every market; trading attention is the game" (2095591087104487619). That is why memecoins are not a joke to him. He sees them as a 0-to-1 way to financialize internet culture: "Memecoins are a paradigm shift in social coordination and crypto's ability to financialize anything easily" (1849423881305506112). He extends this to social trading: "Social trading is a megatrend and a multi-trillion dollar category that will flip Instagram/TikTok; traders become the next generation of content creators" (2088475976158282171).

He also believes speculation is not a bug. "Speculation is necessary for crypto innovation; without it, the industry would have died" (1755095020288221599). He frames crypto as a survival tool in a broken economy: "Crypto is a screaming long because the economy forces people to hypergamble through markets to leapfrog socioeconomic tiers" (1751670007774277732). Late in the notes he says the fundamental value is economic freedom: "Crypto gives everyone access to non-sovereign money and capital markets; economic freedom is the fundamental value prop" (2081473504629690512).

His view on chains has changed. In the 2017-2018 era he was bullish NEO and bearish ETH: "Ethereum is overrated ('garbo'); NEO is the king in China" (925906034274832384, 926444132251455488). In 2020-2021 he was very bullish on ETH and DeFi, at one point saying "ETH is the gauge for the bull market" (1394741084371234823). By 2023 that flipped hard toward Solana: "Solana is the most mispriced asset in crypto and will outperform Ethereum" (1677358208829071368). He now names Solana "the people's chain" (1786124144519561691) and argues ETH's value accrual is broken: "Ethereum has no real moat as a smart contract blockchain; winners of the L1 competition will be $1T+ protocols" (1862706131585388544). But he is not a permanent ETH hater: in 2025 he can be tactically long ETH when the chart and catalysts line up (1953945870077952219).

## How they reason and decide

His decisions start with price action, not price. He has repeated "trade price action, not price" for years (1371224638865473538). He uses simple high-timeframe trend tools: "only real trend indicators i use on high timeframes are 200D EMA & ichimoku cloud" (1885799696960045214). For alts specifically, he waits for momentum shifts: "easiest way to trade alts is go to the H6 timeframe & wait for the moku cloud to flip from bear -> bull" (1360960453900447749). He also marks levels in advance and sets alerts rather than staring at intraday candles: "thats why i like setting alerts and marking important levels before the market reaches them" (2116070096443429308).

He trades in scenarios, with invalidation before entry. He wants a plan for both bullish and bearish cases: "when looking at a chart, always look at a bullish & a bearish scenario regardless of your position" (958173438748909570). He refuses to assign certainty: "one of the worst things you can do as a trader is to assign a 0% probability to something happening" (1505295528766156806). He journals thesis and invalidation: "JOURNAL every trade. thesis + invalidations, written down" (2080414456136212591).

He is aggressively contrarian, but not just to be different. His most quoted signal is social: "bid when ppl are calling me a scammer and start taking profit when ppl are calling me the black jesus christ" (1782983518659752073). He thinks consensus is late money: "your best trades will always be when the majority disagrees with you when you first take the position" (1509950754752286726). But he distinguishes that from using CT sentiment as a primary system: "trying to use ct sentiment to trade is a one way ticket to poverty" (1789923905961906312).

He uses both technicals and fundamentals, but gives price action the final vote. In 2018 he said "technical analysis beats fundamental analysis: 'TA > FA'" (980497999699824640). By 2024 he had split fundamentals into two kinds: "technical merit and the likelihood something pumps (meme-ability, community, narrative)" (1763709541261484302). He will not let a good story override a bad chart: "when i use fundamentals as a reason i shouldnt take a trade based off technicals i miss like 90% of the time" (1509955164156928001). At the same time, he researches on-chain data, revenue, and revenue multiples when the market demands it: "Judge protocols by revenue and fundamentals versus narrative discount" (2083204167271596324, 2079269195997401522).

He front-runs attention and capital flows. He made his best money being early to under-researched areas: "best time to b actively researching in crypto is when everyone else is being lazy, that's when you'll make the most bread" (1515980576049741830). He tracks rotations and which alts hold up during selloffs: "watch side by side assets price action during liquidation events, bid strength when you see it in pa" (1809617104691134823). He prefers finding the trade before consensus: "find the trade before it's consensus; first-mover/time value is highest at peak doubt" (2083584335085519172).

## Risk attitude

His risk rule is mostly defensive: stay in spot, avoid liquidation, keep the long-term stack untouchable. He has repeated some version of "you can't get liquidated if you're sitting in spot" for years (1734045147531272265). He warns most people away from leverage: "using leverage in crypto makes no sense for ~90% of ppl every cycle coins go up ~10x+" (1731675741949985214). His basic plan is boring on purpose: "buy the bottom heavy in spot coins, do nothing, wait, sell slowly over time in 12-18 months, profit" (1742626447448027251).

But he is not anti-leverage in all contexts. He has said "All great investing records employed leverage" (1913122044645958083), and he acknowledged that leverage longs at the covid bottom made people rich (1977128567638012200). The real problem is the user and the timing: "up only is a trap, strong trends let you get into bad habits of lev longing in the middle of trends over & over & getting away with it then one flash crash can wipe you out" (1768262078186365001). His own major losses came from exactly that: "every one of my major fuck ups has been bc of being over confident w/ leverage and it is not even close" (1462475458432352260).

He protects upside while still taking risk via segmenting the portfolio. His framework is persistent: "70% high conviction plays 30% degen risky plays very important to segment your portfolio this way" (2084287163282465070). He also keeps a separate ape stack so he doesn't sell core bags to chase the new meta (2084287163282465070). He sizes for survival: "if you're hunting 100x to 1000x returns, you need the conviction and sizing to survive aggressive drawdowns without getting shaken out of the entire trend" (2098462838721589509).

He is comfortable being wrong and looking stupid in public. He has said "I am wrong a fuckton bros" (1776670703422525659). He does not delete losing tweets; he owns them with humor and repositions. He was brutally honest about shorting LUNA too early: "being right but blown out" is his worst trade (2080448877388238622).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

**Memecoins.** He shifted from early dismissal to full supercycle belief. In 2021 he called meme coins a permanent casino: "Meme coins are a permanent market segment, like casinos; betting on them dying is like betting on casinos going out of business" (1395566411813117952). By 2024 he was fully evangelized: "Memecoins are a legitimate, serious asset class and the core trade of this cycle" (1762542376067752331, 1763394466876965008). He became the loudest WIF bull: "The dog has a hat" and "hat stays on" became his signature. He frames memecoins as financialized attention and a retail onboarding mechanism (1749968625363005719).

**Solana vs Ethereum.** Early on he was an ETH/DeFi bull, but after 2022 he became the cycle's biggest Solana proponent. He called Solana at $26 the generational bottom and said it was hilariously mispriced: "Solana is the most mispriced asset in crypto and will outperform Ethereum" (1677358208829071368). He later upgraded it to "the people's chain" and argued it beats ETH on real metrics (1786124144519561691). But he is not a pure maxi; he says "never become a maximalist when you are a trader" (2101894967710937558).

**Bitcoin.** He is long-term bullish but often tactically bearish on BTC when equities/risk look weak. Early on he called BTC's PoW flawed (917639747924889600), then evolved to treating it as the only hard money: "Bitcoin is the hardest money, reflexive to debasement talk; the denominator is worthless, buy bitcoin" (2092342098238550328). He has said he sees no reason not to have 100% of long-term savings in bitcoin (1824494087367434661). At the same time, in risk-off macro he derisks BTC because it trades like a risk asset (2115913121185529917).

**Hyperliquid.** This is his later-cycle favorite platform. He is convex on HYPE: "Hyperliquid is the standout: exchange + L1 + native stablecoin trifecta, flipping Nasdaq profitability with a tiny team" (1964568832565227714). He calls it crypto's best 24/7 exchange product (2105721654546424087, 2115931791446220896).

**AI x crypto.** He believes it is a dominant meta, but he separates vaporware from real distribution: "AI x crypto is the breakout narrative for 2025, comparable to DeFi in 2019-2020; many vaporware coins will go to zero but cornerstones will emerge" (1873824504696488411). He also warns that AI stocks are in a bubble while AI itself is real (2115947804170617056).

**Privacy/Zcash.** By 2025-2026 he is loudly bullish on privacy: "Privacy is a core tenet of crypto that never had its bull market; Zcash is the revenge trade against surveillance states" (1976637248675217633). He trades ZEC with specific levels and calls it the next bitcoin narrative (2095916659542200783).
