# How @blknoiz06 thinks

## Worldview
He sees crypto as the most open wealth-generation game there is: asymmetric, early, and one of the few places where time and research beat credentials. He says “crypto is one of the few asymmetric opportunities where you are still early” and that you can start with nothing and get respected fast if you’re good (post:1399104902056579075, post:1827950027416735774). He believes the richest returns come from being early to trends others dismiss. “Made ~80% of money in crypto by looking into things most people wrote off or weren't paying attention to yet” (post:1863084457953014202). In his view, markets trade on attention and narrative as much as fundamentals: “crypto trades almost purely off narratives and attention” (post:1743056312613687439).

He is a hard Solana bull and long-time Ethereum bear. Early on he pushed NEO/Skycoin, but by the 2020s he settled into “Solana is the most mispriced asset in crypto” (post:1677358208829071368), and by 2024-2025 he repeated it as “SOL/ETH new ATHs are a generational trade” (post:1849198819923341648). He treats ETH as structurally weak: high fees, fragmented L2 value, broken ultrasound-money thesis. “ETH is not outperforming other L1s right now; the EVM wasn't designed for a multichain world” (post:1447998821586378759). He also respects Bitcoin as hard money and says “ideal to have 100% of long-term savings in Bitcoin” (post:1859719937373478978). Later he adds Hyperliquid as core infrastructure: “the best exchange: flawless during volatility, best economics, most catalysts” (post:1886427509874417715).

He thinks speculation is not a bug; it is the product. Memecoins are a legitimate asset class because they financialize attention and internet culture. “Memecoins are a paradigm shift in social coordination and crypto's ability to financialize anything easily” (post:1849423881305506112). He believes crypto’s speculative games are proof it is permanent, not a fad (post:1822316054757912744). He also believes the economic system forces people to gamble: “Gambling is a supercycle: wages are suppressed, so people hypergamble through markets to leapfrog socioeconomic tiers” (post:1825291207045791894).

Politically and socially, he is direct about race and class. He says crypto enables financial sovereignty for Black people who face discrimination in traditional finance (post:1476687726980251658). He criticizes the wealth gap, police violence, and government overreach, but is not anti-capitalist in a performative way; he says “making money is not inherently evil; critiquing capitalism as a system shouldn't mean lambasting those using it” (post:1305903031683346433). He voted red in 2024 because Republicans in Congress were better on crypto legislation (post:1829226729434689665).

## How they reason and decide
Price action first, fundamentals second. He says “when i use fundamentals as a reason i shouldn't take a trade based off technicals i miss like 90% of the time” (post:1509955164156928001). His toolkit is simple: structure, volume, higher-timeframe trend, Ichimoku, 200D EMA, S/R levels, and relative strength. “Price and volume are the only things you need to trade any market” (post:1621647641837813760). He often reads charts as fractals and looks for origin-of-breakout entries rather than chasing (post:1827029427940945927).

He is contrarian and sentiment-driven. His most repeated rule is “bid when ppl are calling me a scammer and start taking profit when ppl are calling me the black jesus christ” (post:1782983518659752073). He fades consensus: “your best trades will always be when the majority disagrees with you when you first take the position” (post:1509950754752286726). He also uses his own ego as a signal: “you only need two sell signals 1) when i tell the entire timeline to suck my dick 2) when i go on a 5 day bender” (post:1749977385296855542).

He plans for multiple scenarios instead of predicting one path. “Never assign 0% probability to something happening” (post:1505295528766156806). He journals every trade with thesis and invalidation: “JOURNAL every trade. thesis + invalidations, written down. it's how i got good in 2020-2021” (post:2080414456136212591). He stresses having a process for wrongness and re-entry: “it is also important to have some process for evaluating when you are wrong” (post:2095188608005120411).

He flips bias quickly and does not marry bags. “never become a maximalist when you are a trader” (post:2101894967710937558). He says the ability to realize when wrong and pivot aggressively is one of the most important parts of being a good trader (post:1921662836566110717). He is willing to admit over-rotation: “i mean ive been wrong about a lot ive just been able to change bias @ the right times recently” (post:1462499846368157703). He also stresses that being early is not the same as being wrong: “Being too early is a difficult place: the thesis can be right for a long time before late capital and price action confirm it” (post:1962213802167968230).

## Risk attitude
His risk attitude is aggressive but disciplined around one thing: do not get liquidated. He repeats “all you gotta do for the next two years is not get liq'd bros” (post:1679568340979707905). He prefers spot: “you can't get liquidated if you're sitting in spot” (post:1734045147531272265). He says leverage makes no sense for 90% of people because coins already go 10x+ (post:1731675741949985214). He uses a 70/30 framework: 70% high-conviction spot bags and 30% degen/rotations/onchain punts (post:1776671011196264929). Later he refines it: “70% long-term diversified spot bags, 30% actively trading rotations & narratives” (post:1883587685711024558).

He is comfortable with discomfort: “the road to a 100x is littered with many -30% pullbacks” (post:1726999021288653013). He holds through deep drawdowns when conviction is high: “we were bullposting $WIF through multiple 70-80% drawdowns for several months straight” (post:1790819196617556392). But he also warns against holding loser bags out of honor: “longterm conviction on underperformers after the trade has topped out is not honorable it is retarded” (post:1842646825741771127).

He admits losses and mistakes openly, often with humor. He says “i am wrong a fuckton bros” (post:1776670703422525659) and describes his worst trade as shorting LUNA late 2021 with max size, being right eventually but blown out first (post:2080448877388238226). He says the key is limiting downside when wrong and sizing up when high conviction (post:1776670703422525659). He also warns against tailing people without a process: “do not blind copy trade anyone on X you will eventually lose money” (post:1784219491141841196).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)
- **Solana**: Bullish since he pivoted from NEO/Skycoin. He called it “the kingmaker trade of this cycle” and “most mispriced asset” (post:1683915979485503498, post:1677358208829071368). He kept adding through FTX collapse and 2023 bear: “buying $SOL here is a generational opportunity” (post:1665408154224545792). By 2024-2025 he frames it as “the people's chain” and predicts flip of ETH (post:1786124144519561691).
- **Memecoins**: He was initially skeptical/reluctant in 2021 but flipped bullish by late 2023-2024. He says “i dont buy derivative memes, nearly ever” but also “Memecoins are the highest-beta assets in a bull market” (post:1773553923845914647, post:1768285870367314104). He became a leading voice of the “memecoin supercycle” and said “the memecoin supercycle is real and accelerating” (post:1795155457650167884). By 2025 he still says they are “the best beta every time the market goes up” (post:1921276327891501414).
- **Ethereum**: Bearish for most of the timeline. Early he said “Ethereum is overrated ('garbo'); NEO is the king in China” (post:925906034274832384). Later the bearish view hardened: “ETH dominance has topped; modular architecture spreads value across rollups and DA layers” (post:1788161996711399831). In 2025-2026 he still shorted or avoided ETH: “ETH is not outperforming other L1s; it has none of the setup SOL had” (post:1786124144519561691).
- **Bitcoin**: Mixed early, increasingly bullish as hard money. In 2020 he was bearish short term during COVID but long-term bullish (post:1241879109132865537). By 2024 he said “Bitcoin flipping gold is the real trade of our generation” (post:1856105571759309085). In 2025 he called it the only 10-year hold: “only thing id be comfortable holding for 10 years is BTC” (post:1797555245381456305). But he also traded around it and sometimes called short-term bearish BTC/ETH.
- **Hyperliquid / HYPE**: Bullish from launch. He called it “the best exchange: flawless during volatility, best economics, most catalysts” (post:1886427509874417715). He accumulated HYPE and said “the only high-probability bet is to hold spot $HYPE and have the ability not to care about the volatility” (post:1939511030666678584).
- **AI x crypto / social trading / prediction markets**: Bullish from 2024-2025. He says “AI x crypto is the breakout narrative for 2025” (post:1873824504696488411) and “social trading is a megatrend and a multi-trillion dollar category” (post:2093118390185431348). He believes prediction markets are early innings (post:1970568291648180584).
- **Privacy / Zcash**: Relatively late addition, bullish by 2025: “Privacy is a core tenet of crypto that never had its bull market; Zcash is the revenge trade” (post:1976637248675217633).
