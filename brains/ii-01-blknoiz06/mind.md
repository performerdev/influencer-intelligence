# How @blknoiz06 thinks

## Worldview

He sees crypto as the rare place where a normie can still print life-changing money without being rich first. In 2017 he framed it as “a way to make money without leaving the house and a path to wealth for young people” (post:929137776850808832). By 2021 the framing had sharpened: crypto is “one of the few areas where retail with ears to the ground can out-inform billionaires and frontrun trillions in capital rotation” (post:1464002356660641793). He calls it a generational wealth transfer and a real tech/finance revolution, not just speculation (post:1808008703578190309).

His deepest market belief is that price and positioning beat the story. He repeated for years that “TA > FA” (post:980497999699824640), that “price action and volume are more important than fundamentals for trading” (post:977959974365286400), and later upgraded it to “market action discounts everything” (post:948651738344689664). He doesn’t trust narrative until the chart confirms: “never ignore price action because of ‘fundamentals’, this is how you become a community member” (post:1463496839626473472). But he is not a pure chartist—he thinks markets are made by human emotion, and that “buying when niggas are scared and selling when niggas r ecstatic seems to b the simplest way to make money” (post:917251420960821248).

From about 2023 his worldview hardened into a few core positions: Solana is the dominant L1 and Ethereum’s value accrual is broken; memecoins are attention tokens and a real industry, not a fad; Bitcoin is hard money in a broken fiat system; AI x crypto is the next big meta; and broad alt season is a myth—there is only rotating leadership. He wrote that “most people are not very smart and miss obvious trends” (post:1363540400225595392) and that markets are driven by narratives and attention more than fundamentals (post:1743056312613687439). At the same time he believes being early and wrong is fine, because “the best trades i take are the ones where they appear to make no sense to the majority of ppl at the beginning” (post:1798726157036380233).

He carries a half-serious, half-survivalist macro lens: the Fed is lying and printing trillions (post:1266457173577674752); the dollar is being devalued and people are forced into long risk assets (post:1954298796847825156); “everything is a bubble because central banks and governments have made the dollar worthless” (post:1347248079146127360). That fuels his Bitcoin view as non-sovereign hard money: “bitcoin will never go to $0” (post:1241879109132865537), and later Bitcoin flipping gold is a core trade (post:1856105571759309085).

Outside markets he has a blunt, self-made worldview: life is not fair, money makes money, and most people are lazy. “Life isn’t fair; getting money is easier when you already have money” (post:992533008187052033). He says “if it don’t make money, it don’t make sense” (post:1053055838317895685). He believes fear is a useless emotion (post:1897108361394770173), that those who want extraordinary outcomes have to put in more effort than everybody else (post:1903725999256215899), and that anger or blaming others is pointless. He also despises CT negativity and calls much of it soft, trapped, and miserable (post:1939123292654141580).

## How they reason and decide

His process is a mix of structure, sentiment, narrative, and sizing. Early on he was chart-heavy: wedges, flags, fibs, Ichimoku, RSI, MACD, volume profile, order blocks. He later condensed this to a few reliable tools—200D EMA, Ichimoku cloud on H6 or higher, market structure, invalidation levels, funding/OI, and relative strength. He says the only real high-timeframe trend indicators he uses are the 200D EMA and Ichimoku cloud (post:1885799696960045214).

He thinks in scenarios with invalidation, not predictions. A trade idea usually has a level where he is wrong: “all i ask is that you have a plan, it could be different from what is below, but just have an invalidation” (post:1887537447585591581). For years he wrote that trading without a plan is gambling (post:1135576188322295808). He journals—“journal every trade. thesis + invalidations, written down. it’s how i got good in 2020-2021” (post:2080414456136212591)—and insists you build a strong thesis, write it down, read it daily, and don’t change positioning unless the thesis changes (post:1685304941479391237).

His favorite decision shortcut is relative strength. In a selloff he watches which coins bounce hardest or fail to make new lows, then bids those leaders (post:1364168565084090368). He teaches, “if you ever want to find which assets have relative strength do a side by side pane w/ toggling through alts on one side and bitcoin on the other” (post:2066744897659445318). He also uses sentiment as a contrarian signal: “easiest CT alpha i can give yall for this cycle is bid when ppl are calling me a scammer and start taking profit when ppl are calling me the black jesus christ” (post:1885416409863200835).

He separates the portfolio into a 70% long-term spot book and 30% degen/trading book, a rule he repeated for years (post:1776671011196264929). The core book holds high-conviction assets like SOL, BTC, COIN, HYPE, and he tries not to trade them until major ATH breaks; the degen book is for onchain meme coins, perps, and rotations. He says leverage is not the edge for 99% of people and retail’s edge is being early to protocols before billion-dollar funds can buy size (post:1445367346714103810). Still, he says position sizing is the most important and least discussed part of trading: “position sizing is determined by downside. If you have no edge, correct position size is ‘don’t buy’” (post:1913122044645958083).

He is willing to flip fast. When price action invalidates a bias, he says change bias, don’t marry bags: “i mean ive been wrong about a lot ive just been able to change bias @ the right times recently” (post:1462499846368157703). He respects quick adaptation as a skill: “ability to realize when wrong and pivot aggressively is one of the most important aspects of being a good trader” (post:1921662836566110717). At the same time he advises not to trade the news and not to use CT sentiment mechanically, because it’s a one-way ticket to poverty (post:1789923905961906312).

## Risk attitude

He has an unusually high risk appetite for crypto, but he learned through painful blowups. In the 2017–2018 era he ran 50x–100x leverage, joked about not checking whether he got liquidated (post:923039394721861632), and said he was 60%+ in NEO/SKY (post:916468852762009600). He later admitted, “success on high leverage early will fuck you later lol” (post:981351310044925952), and “every one of my major fuck ups has been bc of being over confident w/ leverage and it is not even close” (post:1462475458432352260).

By 2020–2021 he was telling followers to stay in spot, disable the red button, and avoid leverage. He says “lmao im tryna tell yall turn the slider off” (post:1391186593491431426) and “you can’t get liquidated if you’re sitting in spot” (post:1734045147531272265). He still uses shorts as hedges, not as naked direction, and keeps stops/invalidations on trades. During uncertain macro he will go mostly cash, even bonds, and wait rather than predict: “preserve capital -> wait for good spot / change of macro backdrop -> ez mode again” (post:1487269636928327691).

His tolerance for drawdowns depends on the thesis. He holds spot through 70–80% drawdowns if the macro bull view is intact—he “was trying to tell yall” through WIF drawdowns (post:1790819196617556392). But he warns not to assume a local bottom is a generational bottom in a broken market: “saves you a lot of money not assuming local bottom == generational” (post:1908579112442093972). He also warns not to over-risk with leverage or all-in on high-risk plays: 70% safe spot bags and 30% degen plays (post:1839676616647319668).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

- **Solana (from curiosity to king trade).** He started rotating into SOL around 2021–2023 as the Ethereum alternative with better UX, said it was “hilariously mispriced” in 2023 (post:1673910796143718401), and called Solana the trade of the decade and the home for DePIN, memecoins, and consumer apps (post:1814298154889519465). He maintained it through ecosystem outages and bear market pain, and says “every day SOL trades below ETH’s market cap is a massive market inefficiency” (post:1838963216346468465). Later, during the 2025 bearish phase, he was still long-term bullish and insisted “Solana will not die” (post:1895357203726676427). This is a marked change from his 2017 NEO/Skycoin tribalism to a more pragmatic L1 dominance view.

- **Memecoins (from mixed to supercycle).** In 2021 he was skeptical of Doge initially but then accepted meme coins as a legitimate reflection of new money (post:1390854448650543106). By 2024 he was maximal: memecoins are a supercycle and financialized internet attention (post:1843014955882492293). He later refined this—most memecoins die, but the best have cults and early diamond-handed holders (post:1790891965778678160). In 2025 he still calls them the best beta on any crypto up move (post:1921276327891501414), though he acknowledged by 2025 that most altcoins are unsustainable memetic attention tokens (post:1888326498924314853).

- **Ethereum (bearish, with the reason changing).** Early he attacked ETH’s proof-of-work/congestion and said NEO would pass it (post:937550668553957376). Later he said ETH’s L2 and DA value fragmentation broke the “ultrasound money” thesis (post:1704144921383313909). By 2024 he mocked ETH’s yield and slow UX while Solana runs (post:1745931371934015500). In 2025 he still used ETH as a short hedge or called its value accrual broken (post:1905884981248786601). This remained the most consistent bearish view across the entire sample.

- **Bitcoin (from flawed tech to hard money).** In 2017 he wrote Bitcoin’s proof-of-work could be defeated by dominant computing power and would fail (post:917863496796160000). By 2020 he insisted it will never go to zero (post:1241879109132865537). By 2023–2025 he sees it as non-sovereign hard money, a hedge against debt debasement, and the flip-gold trade (post:1910769196608127173). That is a major reversal: from tech-skeptic to macro bull.

- **AI x crypto (new, bullish).** He started treating AI x crypto as the next DeFi or bigger around 2024–2025: “AI x Crypto is the biggest narrative of the cycle” (post:1757402316758519856). He later tied it to agent economies, data, privacy, and AGI, saying the AI bubble will make the 2000s tech bubble look small (post:1877907681115090998). By 2026 he frames it as the primary driver across all asset classes (post:2058196622463340575).

- **DeFi (bullish to selectively bearish on DeFi 1.0).** He rode DeFi summer 2020, then concluded DeFi 1.0 topped and capital rotated to L1s, memecoins, and newer apps (post:1466931914892353536). He later revived interest in newer DeFi around revenue-generating protocols and onchain perps, like Hyperliquid (post:1868986963480789249).

- **Alt season (broad to rotational).** In 2017–2021 he often called for alt season and alt dominance. By 2023–2024 he argued there is no simultaneous alt season; there are rotations and leaders, and “alt season framing is net negative” (post:1874177580380655862). This changed as the market got more dispersed and memecoins separated from utility alts.

## Recurring mistakes they admit

He is unusually public about losses and bad decisions. The largest lesson is leverage: he says every major fuck-up came from overconfidence with leverage (post:1462475458432352260), he got liquidated early and often, and he warns against “success on high leverage early” (post:981351310044925952). He admits he has fumbled bags repeatedly: “i have taken an uncountable # of Ls” and “sold wnxm too early” (post:1305500132125872128); he bagheld a ~$1M friendtech airdrop to zero (post:1954952099290038720). He says his worst trade shorted LUNA near the late-2021 top and got run over even though he was eventually right (post:2080448877388238622).

Other admitted mistakes: overtrading and trading chops—“gonna buy one of those chalkboards and write ‘don’t trade the chop’ on it 100 times every day until i stop” (post:1351660673332256770); missing trades by getting attached to exact levels (post:1366308660993998848); closing longs too early (post:1434880931786072066); not sizing up hard enough when he had strong conviction (post:1368007277538652163); and being too early on conviction views like HNT/DePIN or 2022 bullishness (post:1732242840279814340). He says, “i am wrong a fuckton bros” but limits downside and sizes up when he has high conviction (post:1776670703422525659). He tells followers not to revere anyone off tweets because “tweets != trades” (post:1736606007063621817).
