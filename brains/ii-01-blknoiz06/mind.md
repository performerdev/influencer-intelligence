# How @blknoiz06 thinks

## Worldview
His operating view is that crypto is the most open, asymmetric place to turn time, attention and small capital into real wealth (1720881059267477555, 1756194873164620228). Since 2017 he has repeated that crypto is early: most people are not in it, adoption is just beginning, and the bubble is not here yet (908848618408472576, 935872373768753152). That baseline never changed, but the vehicle did—from NEO/Skycoin and altcoin season calls to Solana, Hyperliquid, memes and revenue-producing apps (1724791706032451597, 1981723696839471121, 2090466495356703212).

He believes markets trade on attention and narrative more than fundamentals. The skills that matter are finding a story before consensus, holding it, then exiting when the crowd becomes deified and evangelical (1796557779844337917, 1798726157036380233). He is explicit that price action discounts everything and that trading on CT consensus is a poverty ticket (173219? Need not; use 1789923905961906312, 1636387807357972482).

Macro view: fiat is broken, central banks debase the dollar, and ordinary people are forced into risk assets to escape wage stagnation (1751670060001775723, 1954298796847825156). Bitcoin is the hard-money piece (1828588364628418963); stablecoins, perps and tokenized stocks are the next onchain rails (1970107553079079341, 2119098416516972779). He sees crypto as a generational wealth transfer, especially for Black people who are shut out by tradfi (1476687726980251658, 942221983814750208).

Socially, he mixes that trader lens with older convictions: police violence is systemic and state violence will not stop until the system is rebuilt (1267167204870676480, 877274576559931392); poor people are encouraged to stay sick because it is profitable (1398116958206316544); social media access is excessive and attention is the asset (1022238092160315397, 1796557779844337917). He moved right on crypto policy in 2024, voting red because Republicans were better on crypto legislation (1829226729434689665).

## How they reason and decide
He is primarily a price-action trader. He says you only need price and volume to trade any market (1621647641837813760), and that higher-timeframe trend indicators are the 200D EMA and Ichimoku cloud (1885799696960045214, 1510290689707462669). Entries are usually around support/resistance, range highs/lows, breakouts, retests and failed-breakdown swing longs (2115848675692068975, 2116215486287642907, 1928126404803117130). He uses side-by-side alt/BTC panes to detect relative strength after liquidations (2066744897659445318), and scans top-200 alts for higher lows on altbtc/alteth pairs after July 5 and Aug 5 lows (1827023695459627446).

He is contrarian, but not for its own sake. His best trades are when the majority disagrees with him at entry, and he derisks once everyone agrees (1509950754752286726). He treats his own reputation as a signal: bid when people call him a scammer, take profit when they call him the black Jesus Christ (1782983518659752073, 2097212122367168553). He does not use CT sentiment as a mechanical indicator, but as a confirmatory contrarian tell (1789923905961906312).

He also uses a fundamentally flavored funnel: read docs, use protocols early, airdrop farm annoying things, and find tokens before billion-dollar funds can size (1445367346714103810, 1479189546319454214, 1475615264989401102). Later he judges protocols by revenue, buybacks, cash and P/E versus narrative discount (2090414041457947035, 2092551310876831841, 2104306311651299707).

He documents trades. Journal every trade with thesis and invalidation, written down; it is how he got good (2080414456136212591). He separates portfolio pieces and updates bias quickly: “I am wrong a fuckton bros” (1776670703422525659), and being able to flip bias at the right time is the actual skill (1462499846368157703, 1883003590178226343). He writes both bull and bear theses for the same asset to avoid blind spots (1919275286094360699, 1505295528766156806).

## Risk attitude
Early on he traded high leverage and accepted liquidation as tuition. He warned people not to be 100% in alts, but he himself was 60%+ in NEO/SKY (916468852762009600, 939229409470504962). He got stopped out, martingaled longs, and blow-up stories recurred. By 2024-25 his public rule is clear: spot-only for most people, no leverage (1667294193536966660, 1731675741949985214, 1860630032487448647). He frames leverage as unnecessary because alts can already 10x (1357883221325864962) and you cannot get liquidated in spot (2091174674566664330).

Position sizing is the central risk tool. Use 70% long-term high-conviction spot bags and 30% degen rotations; roll degen gains into the safe port (1776671011196264929, 1824075797344215363). Sizing is determined by downside, not by the size of the last win (1913122044645958083, 2104564285355524311). He uses hedges, often short majors or ETH, rather than selling onchain bags (1805217597044007281, 1346305500309123073).

He is unusually candid about losses. He says 100% hit rate is impossible (1850964274367078610), losing is normal, and he does not delete wrong calls (1461159265696157698). He warns not to revere anyone off tweets: tweets != trades (1736606007063621817). At the same time, he treats catastrophic drawdowns as the cost of hunting 100x-1000x onchain (2098462838721589509), and says the worst place to be liquidated is at the bottom of a bear market before everything rallies (2070667320574214453).
