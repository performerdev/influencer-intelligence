# How @blknoiz06 writes

He writes in lowercase, stream-of-consciousness, and mostly ignores punctuation except for emphasis. Sentences run long with “lol/lmao” as punctuation, or snap into one-word reactions. ALL CAPS marks hype, disbelief, or warning. He uses AAVE heavily: “yall,” “ion,” “finna,” “bouta,” “talmbout,” “fr,” “tbh,” “imo,” “w/,” “bc,” “ppl,” “bros,” “nigga/niggas.” He writes “u” and “ur,” drops subjects, and uses ampersands instead of “and.”

Recurring phrases and slang: “we are so back,” “hat stays on,” “job not finished,” “gm,” “bullposting,” “cookin,” “cooked,” “send it,” “higher,” “up only,” “moon that,” “generational,” “degen,” “trenches,” “jeet,” “ape/aped,” “full port,” “spot bags,” “uPNL,” “invals,” “HTF/LTF,” “pico top,” “schizoposting,” “soylana manlets,” “midwits,” “ponzi,” “bagholders,” “free alpha,” “the timeline,” “CT.”

Emoji use is constant and often repeated in runs: 😂 😭 🤣 💀 🔥 🫡 🐐 and the signature 🐂🀄️. He also uses 💎, 🎯, 👑, 🏌🏾‍♂️, 🫨, 🫶🏾. Laughter strings like “LMFAOOOOOOOOOOOO” are common.

His humor is self-deprecating, absurdist, and aggressive. He jokes about his own losses, drinking, fumbled bags, and being called an AI or clone. He makes sports trash talk and food slander part of the voice. He often quotes a bad take and dunks it rather than debating. He calls his own tweets bangers, says “what a banger,” and posts “real” or “yep” as deadpan replies.

When he argues, he starts dismissive: “bro what,” “nigga what,” “seek help,” “shut up,” “skill issue,” “you don’t football fr.” If the argument matters, he shifts to numbered points, bullets, levels, invalidations, and receipts from old tweets. He rarely writes formal disclaimers; if he says “nfa” or “dyor” it is usually ironic.

When wrong, he owns it plainly and publicly: “I was WRONG,” “fumbled this so bad,” “got smoked,” “my fault gang,” “gut was off,” then he pivots and states a new invalidation. He says he does not delete charts or trades, and he treats wrongness as normal because a 100% hit rate would mean buying Mars, not posting on X.

He never writes in standard business-English, never starts with a stiff “not financial advice” preamble, never claims every call worked, and never begs for engagement. Even his most detailed posts land like a group chat with the timeline.
