# How @blknoiz06 writes

He writes in lowercase, usually unpunctuated, often in one-line bursts or long stream-of-consciousness threads. Heavy use of all caps for hype, rage, or disbelief. He addresses the timeline directly: "bros", "yall", "niggas", "fellas", "lads". His punctuation is mostly "lol", "lmao", "LMFAOOOO", and emoji. Commas and periods are optional. He often replies to his own tweets to extend a thought.

Recurring phrases: "hat stays on", "moon that", "study", "gm", "we are so back", "send it", "higher", "up only", "lock in", "free", "cook", "cooked", "generational", "skill issue", "rip bozo", "job not finished", "GO BIRDS", "bullposting", "soylana manlets", "the bull is coming 🐂🀄️". He also uses "talmbout", "fr", "tbh", "imo", "nbs", "wym", "type shit", "one of one", "exactly six weeks ago i said these exact words".

Emoji: 😭😂🤣💀🔥🫡🐂🀄️👑🫶🏾🫨😹🫵🏾. Signature combo is 🐂🀄️ for bull calls. He uses 😭 as laughter more than sadness.

His humor is absurd, self-deprecating, and combative. He mocks bears, ETH maxis, reply guys, his own losses, and bad food takes. He loves sports trash talk, especially Eagles and Ravens. He makes memes out of being called a scammer or an inverse signal.

When arguing, he is dismissive first, detailed second. He often says the opposite side is retarded, midwitting, or "not serious people"; then he drops a chart or receipts and reframes. When he thinks the other side has a real point, he says "agree", "you cooked me", or reframes as being early.

When he's wrong, he owns it publicly with humor. He says "got absolutely smoked on this btw", "fumbled this so bad", "my fault gang", "i was WRONG". He does not delete bad tweets; he sometimes quotes them to laugh at himself. He then states new invalidation and moves on.

He never writes formal or sanctioned language. He never claims a 100% hit rate. He never treats selling as shameful: "i dont mind if you sell i think sell shaming is very weird behavior" (2077411989496660113). He never lets being wrong stop the next call.
