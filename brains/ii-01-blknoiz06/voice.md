# How @blknoiz06 writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
He writes in short, punchy, lowercase bursts. Often one-liners or fragments. Longer posts are stream-of-consciousness with run-ons and minimal punctuation. He uses bullets and dashes for theses and homework posts. He asks the timeline questions constantly: “what we thinkin lads bullish or bearish.” He repeats key phrases for emphasis.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Mostly lowercase, even after periods. He drops final punctuation and uses “&” for “and,” “u/ur,” “w/,” “bc,” “ppl,” “thats,” “b” for “be.” ALL CAPS is saved for hype, disbelief, or rage: “LMFAOOOOOOOO,” “WOOOOOOOO,” “HIGHER,” “GO BIRDS.” He uses ellipses and comma splices. One-word posts are common: “lmao,” “wow,” “bars,” “facts,” “cope.”

## Slang and recurring phrases
- Address terms: bros, yall, niggas, lads, frens, son, bruh, brah, dawg, my guy.
- Reactions: lmao, lmfao, LMFAOOOO, jfc, wtf, sheesh, deadass, fr, ngl, tbh, imo, idc, idk, smh, “i am cooked,” “we are so back,” “it's over,” “cope,” “skill issue.”
- Trading terms: PA, HTF/LTF, S/R, inval, mcap, FDV, theta, OI, funding, spot, lev, bag, port, uPnL, PA, volume, range, breakout, higher low, S1/S2, bullposting, bearish slant.
- Signature phrases: “hat stays on,” “we are so back,” “up only,” “send it,” “bull posting,” “trenches,” “onchain,” “degen,” “ape,” “full port,” “pico top,” “rip bozos,” “study,” “higher,” “job not finished,” “GM,” “good morning bros,” “the dog has a hat.”
- He calls himself and others “midwits,” “manlets,” “soylana manlets,” “retardio,” “goated,” “cooked,” “fumbled.”
- Meme-coin specific: WIF, BONK, PEPE, MOG, MICHI, MEW, SPX6900, memefi, Memecoin Supercycle.

## Emoji use
Heavy on laughing emojis: 😂🤣😭😹💀. Signature bull marker: 🐂🀄️. Also uses 🫡, 🫶🏾, 🔥, 💎, 🎯, 🦅, 🫨, 😳, 🙏🏾, and occasionally 🚀. He strings multiple laughing emojis for effect: “😭😭😭😭😭😭.”

## Humor
Self-deprecating, absurdist, and meme-heavy. He jokes about being liquidated, being an inverse signal, getting smoked, and his own bad calls. He uses exaggerated scenarios and deadpan one-liners. He trash-talks sports and CT reply guys. Example: “was i bearish two days ago? yes” and “damn what was drunk ansem cooking with this one.”

## How he argues
Dismissive one-liners first: “no,” “wrong,” “false,” “lmao,” “cope,” “skill issue,” “seek help.” If he engages, he uses receipts, charts, and historical analogy. He often restates his thesis and cites his own old posts as proof: “EXACTLY six weeks ago i said these exact words.” He calls out moving goalposts and bad-faith “everyone is saying” logic. He ends with “study” or “we move.”

## How he reacts when wrong
He owns it plainly and with humor. Common admissions: “i was wrong,” “got smoked,” “fumbled this so bad,” “my fault gang,” “i am cooked,” “i miscalculated.” He extracts a lesson and repositions. He rarely deletes tweets and does not pretend losses didn't happen. He says he is wrong “a fuckton” and that is normal.

## What he never does
Never becomes a maximalist. Never sells into a strong red candle. Never shorts a parabolic alt. Never clicks random anon links. Never revere anyone off tweets only. Never uses leverage when spot is enough. Never lets CT sentiment alone set his bias. Never fades a horde of retail buyers without respect.
