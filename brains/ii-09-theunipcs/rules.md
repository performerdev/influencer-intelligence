# @theunipcs's rules

## Entry

- **Buy the dip when the timeline is in extreme fear.** “from a sentiments perspective, the extreme fear on the TL rn tells me it’s time to bid” (post:1768525471912772093).
- **Don’t FOMO after big runs; use a small ‘suicide stack’ then DCA dips.** “buy a ‘sucide stack’ that’s usually a small amount in case it keeps going so u don’t feel sidelined and hurt then wait for dips and DCA in the rest of your main buys” (post:1794955013770645951).
- **Scale in now and DCA on dips rather than waiting for a perfect 10-20% dip.** “it’s safer and more efficient to scale in now and DCA on dips” (post:1836851954393153802).
- **Buy extremely oversold assets (down 80-90%) with strong fundamentals during a bull run.** “in a proven bull run, buying extremely oversold assets with strong fundamentals is the easiest way to make generational wealth” (post:1920516286733250857).
- **Buy when no one is talking about an asset and sell when everyone is euphoric.** “buy when no one is talking about an asset and sell when everyone is euphoric” (post:1840515686378143782).
- **If you believe in the narrative and lack exposure, DCA rather than FOMO buying a local top or waiting for a dip that may never come.** “if you believe in the narrative and don’t have exposure, the best way to enter is to DCA, rather than FOMO buy what you think might be a local top or wait for a dip that may never come” (post:1938277686528409994).
- **Buy dips on high-conviction plays into Q4, don’t chase new pairs.** “any dip we get into Q4 is for buying IMO don’t let them shake you out before the REAL move” (post:2097772686181068806, post:2097977503616729250).
- **Bet on leaders and outperformers when a new trend emerges.** “the way to win when a new trend emerges is to simply bet on the leaders and outperformers” (post:2007515223532462304).
- **Position before the crowd realizes the trend has changed; ask where attention goes next.** “don’t ask: ‘what is pumping right now?’ ask: ‘where is attention going next, and what becomes the biggest beneficiary if i’m right?’” (post:2095974106969588053).

## Exit and taking profit

- **Don’t take profit early in a bull run.** “‘take profit’ these are the two most dangerous words anyone will ever hear at the beginning of a new bull run” (post:1764351140685115830).
- **Set portfolio/individual asset targets and take profit when reached, but don’t be too hasty calling a top.** “set portfolio/individual asset targets for yourself and take profit when these targets are reached, but don’t be too hasty in calling ‘top’” (post:1863844351639498915).
- **Take profits routinely; don’t be blindly loyal to bags or communities.** “you should be routinely taking profits on any and every coin as long as you’re not doing anything illegal or unethical” (post:2084617347621351700).
- **Won’t sell a token until it hits a specific market cap target.** “i won’t be selling a single token until it hits $100 million market cap” (post:1917166319709155634); “i will not be selling a single CAT token before $1 billion market cap” (post:1854891532970868841).
- **Sell during strong momentum so one person’s sells don’t materially affect the chart.** “whenever i sell, it would be during period of strong momentum where the actions of one man would not affect the charts” (post:1953891250366820704).

## Sizing

- **Only invest what you can afford to lose.** “only invest what i can afford to lose” (post:1851048358036615590).
- **Put less than 1% of portfolio into most trades.** “generally tho, i put less than 1% of my portfolio into most trades” (post:1953518789795909983).
- **Make a handful of high-conviction, concentrated bets.** “every cycle, i make a handful of high-conviction, concentrated bets on the memecoins i believe can massively outperform” (post:2095145010832257102).

## Averaging down

- **Keep buying dips in high-conviction bags as long as CT ignores them.** “i’ll probably keep buying for as long as CT continues to ignore it” (post:1777256583723966837).
- **Buy more when it dips further, and even more until it stops dipping.** “when it dips after you buy YOU BUY MORE when it dips even further YOU BUY EVEN MORE until it stops dipping eventually” (post:1985353414381879527).
- **Bid every dip hard on a memecoin with strong fundamentals.** “when you get a memecoin with strong fundamentals you bid every dip opportunity you get hard” (post:1865504709613879457).

## Hold time

- **Hold through drawdowns; the hardest part of a 100x is simply holding.** “the hardest part of hitting 100x, 1,000x, or some magic number is simply holding” (post:1768038976277880927).
- **Hold with high conviction for long periods; big gains take months or years, not days.** “almost every glamorous story of people who made insane amounts from memecoins came from people who had CONVICTION often, this means they had to hold for years or at least a lot of months” (post:1855354040173130024).
- **Hold until the thesis plays out; winning is about patience, not being right immediately.** “sometimes, winning isn’t determined by whether your thesis is right it’s determined by whether you’re willing to hold until it plays out” (post:2078528934103208354).
- **Don’t let temporary price action shake you out before the real run.** “don’t let temporary price action shake you out before the real fireworks begin” (post:2109723023502930302).

## Risk management

- **Tone down leverage; most biggest wins are spot.** “make sure to tone down the leverage everyone 🙏” (post:1763741393799786847); “just spot and chill if you can. easy to hold that way, too” (post:1776025077085073649).
- **Don’t get liquidated; survive drawdowns.** “you have only one job here: SURVIVE that’s it don’t get liquidated. don’t panic-close high-conviction trades.” (post:1909117312709390574).
- **Never invest more than you can afford to lose.** “i simply do not invest more than i can afford to lose, no matter how alluring the trade is” (post:1953518789795909983).
- **Avoid leverage completely if trying to recover from a loss.** “if you just lost everything to leverage liquidation and are trying to make it back, avoid leverage at all costs” (post:1886572123256402179).
- **Use isolated margin, avoid cross.** “i exclusively use isolated trades and generally avoid using cross margin” (post:1953518789795909983).
