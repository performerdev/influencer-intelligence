- Scale in now and DCA on dips instead of waiting for a perfect 10–20% dip: “it's safer and more efficient to scale in now and DCA on dips” (post:1836851954393153802).

## Exit and taking profit
- Do not take profit early in a new bull run: “'take profit' these are the two most dangerous words anyone will ever hear at the beginning of a new bull run” (post:1764351140685115830).
- Hold high-conviction memecoins until retail mania comes unless a very significant target is hit earlier: “my focus with most of my memecoin plays is to accumulate and hold until retail mania comes which is when i sell, unless they hit a very significant target prior to then” (post:1872913119997309323).
- Sell when the thesis fully plays out: “i will sell when my thesis fully plays out” (post:1953430747517210919).
- Take profits routinely; do not be blindly loyal to bags or communities: “you should be routinely taking profits on any and every coin as long as you're not doing anything illegal or unethical” (post:2084617347621351700).
- Sell during strong momentum so one person’s sells do not move the chart: “whenever i sell, it would be during period of strong momentum where the actions of one man would not affect the charts” (post:1953891250366820704).
- Buy coins when it feels scary and sell when it feels retarded: “buy coins when it feels scary to do so sell coins when it feels retarded to do so” (post:1936108815403073948).

- Diversify across multiple memecoins rather than being a maxi: “having a diversified portfolio is ALWAYS better than being a maxi” (post:1857219677740532207).
