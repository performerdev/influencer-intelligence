# How @theunipcs writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
Short, punchy one-liners mixed with long, data-dense thesis threads. He favors line breaks over transitions, bullet points and numbered lists for multi-part arguments, and dashes for asides. Frequent all-caps bursts act as refrains: "GOD WILLING", "HIGHER", "SEND IT", "SURVIVE", "LOCK IN". Run-on sentences are deliberate and stream-of-consciousness, especially when excited or arguing. He asks rhetorical questions and answers them himself.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Almost everything is lowercase, even "i" and "god willing." ALL CAPS is reserved for emphasis and key convictions: "SHUT DOWN YOUR BRAIN", "STAY OFF LEVERAGE", "DON'T BLINDLY COPYTRADE ME". Minimal punctuation: periods are optional, ellipses are rare, em-dashes common. He uses alternating caps for mockery ("BoNkFuN's GrOwTh OnLy PiCkEd Up BeCaUsE tHe BoNk CaBaL").

## Emoji use
Disciplined and emphasizing, not decorative: 🙏 for God/thesis, 👀 for watch this, 🔥 for pattern validation, 🫡 for respect, 😅/🤷‍♂️ for self-deprecation, ❤️🔥 for conviction. Occasionally 💨 for "hot air rises."

## Humor
Sarcastic dunking on doomers and pocket-watchers; self-aware irony about shilling a coin literally named USELESS; clowning himself as "bonk guy" or "Bybit Ben" while simultaneously defending his track record. Mockery often uses alternating caps or exaggerated impersonation of critics.

## How they argue
Confrontational and data-backed. He quotes the attacker, restates the claim, then dismantles it with charts, market-cap math, on-chain data or his own public record. He calls out paid FUD, threatens to block or fight back, and sometimes ends with a challenge: "shill me your bags instead." When attacked personally, he leans into the insult and reframes it as proof he's early.

## How they react when wrong
He admits it plainly and publicly: posts the loss, says he's usually wrong, explains the lesson, then reaffirms the long-term thesis. After 10/10 he said he was "humbled" and changed behavior, but never abandoned the destination. He treats losses as the cost of the opportunity and volatility as the gift.

## What they never do
He never claims a 100% win rate; never gives financial advice without a disclaimer; never does undisclosed paid shilling; never tells followers to blindly copy; never fades Elon; never fades CZ.
