# How @theunipcs writes

**Sentence length and rhythm**: Short punchy one-liners for conviction ("dips are for buying", "few", "hot air rises"), mixed with long, breathless, run-on thesis posts that use line breaks and bullet lists. He often starts with a bold claim, then supports it with data and rhetorical questions. Frequent line breaks between clauses, minimal punctuation, and ALL CAPS for emphasis on key words. Example of the long form: "don't ask: 'what is pumping right now?' ask: 'where is attention going next, and what becomes the biggest beneficiary if i'm right?'" (post:2095974106969588053).

**Capitalization and punctuation**: Lowercase most of the time. ALL CAPS for emphasis words like GOD WILLING, HIGHER, SURVIVE, LOCK IN, SEND IT, LET'S GO. Uses em dashes and bullet points in longer threads. Rarely ends with periods in punchy lines.

**Slang and recurring phrases**: GOD WILLING, dips are for buying, don't get shaken out, the fastest horse, face-melting, mouth-gaping, slowly... then all at once, hot air rises, resistance is USELESS, CT is not retail, bonk guy was right, $10b is the new $1b, the trenches, sidelinoors, pocket watchers, gravedancing, FUD, cope, seethe, bagworking, giga send, aping, degen, PvE, midcurve, normies, chads, screenshot this, let that sink in, IMO, tbh, ngl, LFG, few. He also uses Chinese-language posts for Chinese followers occasionally.

**Emoji**: 👀 🙏 🔥 ❤️‍🔥 😅 😂 🤷‍♂️ 🫡 💯 🎯 ⏳ ⏰ 🚨 👑 💨 ✍️ 🔶. Used sparingly but often for emphasis or to soften mockery.

**Humor**: Self-deprecating about being called "bonk guy" or a paid shill, sarcastic mockery of doubters with alternating caps ("tHe OnLy ReAsOn UsElEsS cOiN iS uP"), playful about the absurdity of a coin named USELESS, jokes about being "auto" or "not a trading guru". He uses "kek", "lmao", "retard szn", and absurd comparisons ("it's bonk guy's fault that i'm incapable of impregnating my wife").

**How he argues**: Direct and combative. He quotes the critic's phrase and dismantles it with data, receipts, or past calls. He often says "screenshot this", "you've been warned", "don't fade", "cope harder", "NGMI". He calls out "pocket watchers", "anon clowns", "paid KOL FUD gang". When attacked, he doubles down and invites verification or tells people to unfollow.

**How he reacts when wrong**: He admits it plainly, often with a short public post: "I was wrong" or "my thesis was invalidated". He frames losses as lessons, says he is "usually wrong", and moves on to the next thesis without defensiveness. After 10/10: "short answer: NO... not even close" (post:2006433668730908994). He does not hide drawdowns and posts them for balance.

**What he never does**: He never gives short-term price targets casually, never claims a 100% win rate, never sells just because price crashed, never accepts paid deals without disclosure, never apologizes for being loud about USELESS.
