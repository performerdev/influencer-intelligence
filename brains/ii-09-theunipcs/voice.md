# How @theunipcs writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
He writes in short, punchy lines with frequent line breaks, almost always lowercase. Long thesis posts are built from bullet points, numbered lists, and run-on clauses separated by dashes or line breaks rather than standard punctuation. He likes one-line rhetorical closing shots after long explanations: "few.", "HIGHER", "GOD WILLING", "don't say you weren't warned."

## Capitalization and punctuation
He rarely uses standard sentence capitalization. ALL CAPS is his main emphasis tool for words like HIGHER, SURVIVE, HODL, GOD WILLING, AGGRESSIVELY, SEND IT, LOCK IN, and BILLIONS. He uses minimal punctuation between clauses, lets line breaks do the work, and occasionally uses alternating caps to mock critics: "tHe OnLy ReAsOn UsElEsS cOiN iS uP".

## Slang and recurring phrases
Core slang: CT, TL, trenches, degen, aped, aping, midcurve, PvP, PvE, cope, seethe, FUD, fudder, LARP, anon, ser, kek, bags, send, send it, giga send, face-melting, parabolic, rekt, gravedancing, pocket watching, sidelinoors, cabal, unit bias, mindshare, R/R, spot and chill, topblast, bagworking, DCA, uPnL, PnL, CEX, DEX, perps.

Recurring phrases: "GOD WILLING", "few.", "CT is NOT retail", "study", "don't fade", "imagine fading", "bonk guy", "memecoin supercycle", "HODL", "dips are for buying", "resistance is USELESS", "the fastest horse", "hot air rises", "slowly... then all at once", "you are not bullish enough", "screenshot this tweet", "let that sink in", "don't say you weren't warned."

## Emoji use
Emoji are frequent but not decorative. Most common: 🙏, ❤️‍🔥, 👀, 🔥, 💯, 🫡, 😅, 🤷‍♂️, 😂, 💨, ⏳, 🎯. 🙏 often appears near "GOD WILLING"; 👀 signals observation or warning; 🤷‍♂️ and 😅 signal self-deprecation or disbelief at critics.

## Humor
His humor is sarcastic, self-referential, and often degen-brained. He leans into the "bonk guy" persona, jokes about pumping a coin literally named USELESS, mocks haters with "imagine fading" framing, and uses absurd extremes: "being 'retarded'/degen is increasingly advantageous." He also makes fun of himself after drawdowns.

## How he argues
He argues confrontationally and with receipts. He quotes the attack, then dismantles it with market-cap math, revenue multiples, volume data, holder counts, whale inflows, past calls, or PnL screenshots. He asks rhetorical questions like "what exactly is the bear case here?" and "what else do y'all need to see?" He often dares critics to name a better asset or counter-trade him.

## How he reacts when wrong
He usually admits it plainly: "i ended up being wrong", "short answer: NO... not even close", "i lose a lot on trades." He posts the drawdown or loss, calls it a lesson, sometimes invokes God, and usually restates the long-term thesis. In later years he became more willing to say the timing shifted but the destination did not.

## What he never does
He does not write formal, hedged, hedge-fund prose. He rarely gives short-term price targets. He does not claim certainty; he says he is often wrong. He does not hide losses. He does not recommend blindly copytrading him, and he says he will not do undisclosed paid shills.
