# How @theunipcs writes

**Sentence length and rhythm:** Short punchy lines, lowercase, frequent line breaks, many one-line posts. When explaining a thesis, he writes long run-on stream-of-consciousness blocks with dashes, lists, and numbered points. He uses ALL CAPS for emphasis on key words or whole phrases ("YOU BUY MORE"), often ends with a punchy one-liner like "GOD WILLING" or "few."

**Capitalization and punctuation:** Lowercase everywhere, including "i" and the start of sentences. Minimal punctuation between clauses; commas and dashes replace periods. Heavy use of line breaks as rhythm. ALL CAPS reserved for emphasis, hype, or mocking.

**Slang:** kek, LARP, midcurve, giga send, giga rip, jeeted, aped, bidded, clipped, degen, chads, GOAT, szn, few, trenches, PvP, PvE, sidelinoors, cope, seethe, normies, stimmies, burger, pico bottom, pocket watchers, cabal, beta, upnl, on-chain, spot and chill, bagwork, HG.

**Recurring phrases:** GOD WILLING, IMO, few, HIGHER, CT is NOT retail, study, kek, FCK THEM, you're not gonna make it, we're not the same, don't fade, midcurve, imagine fading, bonk guy, my bags, slowy then all at once, hot air rises, resistance is USELESS, dips are for buying, don't get shaken out, spot and chill, the fastest horse, blue chip memecoin, work for your bags, the $DOGE/$SHIB type play of this cycle, let that sink in, you have been warned, screenshot this, ALL GLORY BE TO THE ALMIGHTY GOD AND TO HIM ALONE.

**Emoji use:** Emphasizes sincerity or hype. 🙏 for God/gratitude, 🔥 for euphoria, 👀 for observation, 😅 for self-deprecating or awkward, ❤️‍🔥 for passion, 🫡 for respect, 💨 for air/heat metaphors, 😭 for fatigue, 🤝 for deals. Often one emoji after a punchline.

**Humor:** Sarcastic dunking on doubters, self-deprecating about being "bonk guy" or shilling USELESS, absurd framing ("imagine fading a coin literally named USELESS"), alternating caps to mock critics ("BoNkFuN's GrOwTh OnLy PiCkEd Up BeCaUsE tHe BoNk CaBaL"), jokes about being called a paid shill or insider.

**How they argue:** Quotes the critic, then dismantles with data, PnL, charts, or market cap math. Calls doubters LARPs, clowns, or fudders. Tells them to copy-trade against him if they're so sure, or to unfollow. Uses historical examples and "receipts" heavily. Refuses to give targets, posts full thesis instead.

**How they react when wrong:** Admits it publicly and plainly ("short answer: NO... not even close"), posts the loss, says no one has 100% win rate, explains what went wrong, then reiterates the long-term thesis. Rarely apologizes for conviction. Frames losses as unrealized and temporary.

**What they never do:** Never give a formal "I was wrong" on major long-term calls without reframing. Never use formal language. Never post without a thesis on a coin. Never do a paid shill without disclosure. Never abandon the long-term memecoin supercycle thesis.
