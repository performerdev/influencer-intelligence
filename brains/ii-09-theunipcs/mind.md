# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview
The throughline is that memecoins are the main event. In early 2024 he was already arguing CT is not retail, CT consensus is usually wrong, and memecoins are the leading narrative of the cycle (post:1763656001532637440, post:1765247411704684806). He repeated that $10 billion was the new $1 billion for memecoins, with several $100 billion memecoins possible (post:1764298459387953229, post:1767120072177234134, post:1862914241260359783). By late 2024 and 2025 he had hardened this into "the $BONK revolution will be televised," unit bias is real, and retail has barely arrived (post:1860985054605738086, post:1857324439223832665, post:1857171533224948135). He believed memecoins would top with the whole market, not before (post:1792242037959311754). By 2025-2026 the thesis widened further: almost all crypto is useless, so a coin that says so is the purest expression of the sector—he called USELESS the cycle's DOGE/SHIB/PEPE-type runner (post:1933530942158623219, post:1997041392938352999, post:2091256125803151722).

His macro view is relentlessly bullish unless structurally invalidated. Early he leaned on ETF flows, rate cuts, election-year liquidity, equities ATHs, and "don't fight the Fed" (post:1768525475922477271, post:1833235694170554687, post:1836543913902969024). After Trump won, he said the Republican sweep was giga bullish because it opened listings and institutional flows (post:1850588342875746771, post:1852458622993011057). In 2025-2026 he argued crypto was lagging stocks and gold and would catch up explosively; the 4-year cycle was dead, and macro conditions were the best ever (post:1978167073617617281, post:1987541408165548466, post:1997041392938352999). He later admitted he was wrong about the 4-year cycle being dead and re-anchored to a later Q4/Q1 move (post:2072782315093205259, post:2087270834557595890).

He is not a one-chain maxi. He was heavily Solana/BONK in 2024-2025 (post:1880646081500918020, post:1881078225125720166). By mid-2025 he championed BonkFun as the trenches and attacked Pumpfun's TGE as "the great extraction" (post:1941835070127456289, post:1942522044584710448). In 2026 he broadened to Robinhood Chain as THE chain of the cycle and PONS as its launchpad proxy (post:2075525662920642599, post:2092549016659636544, post:2106021394966540608). His Solana view turned mixed/critical: the tech was never the problem, the extraction culture was (post:2089277573729566779, post:2097652983567700107).

Faith is central. He frames wins as God's will, repeats "GOD WILLING," and by 2026 explicitly credits God alone and recommends church on Sundays (post:1859001983413236195, post:2093266527029961167, post:2104028251417125363).

## How they reason and decide
His first move is usually sentiment contrarianism. When the timeline is in extreme fear or CT is sure something is dead, he bids (post:1768525471912772093, post:1767994756083105881). He treats hate and coordinated FUD as confirmation: sidelined capital that fuds a missed coin is demand waiting to pounce on dips or green candles (post:1934343748357648524, post:1939610058653859899, post:2063660234116104639). He says the loudest CT consensus is often the best countertrade (post:1944672596366082227, post:1967943234396512582).

He reasons through historical analogs: SHIB 2021, DOGE betas, GME/AMC mania, PEPE 2023, BONK 2023, FARTCOIN 2025. He uses those to argue that 80-99% drawdowns are normal before new ATHs (post:1804235987666768209, post:1986711441819210084, post:2019785568792526961, post:2061823586914971821). He also reads structural and technical patterns—cup and handle, falling wedge, breakout/retest—but says he does not rely solely on TA (post:1848156366319284479, post:1982835775512117307, post:2001027211256848832).

He constantly compares market caps, volumes, and revenue multiples across peers to call something undervalued. Early examples: CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, BTC memes vs ETH/SOL memes, BONK vs WIF (post:1836530239754633613, post:1842522339415846995, post:1862624760846258548). Later: USELESS doing DOGE-level CEX volume at a fraction of the valuation, and PONS trading at roughly 0.66x annualized revenue versus PUMP at 4.35x (post:2109569775919841300, post:2116087930213036136, post:2091618376137187518).

On-chain data is core evidence. He tracks holder growth during sell-offs, smart money/whale netflows, volume/OI, CEX listing signals, Wintermute transfers, and Robinhood account creation (post:1862266553728708611, post:1950178237151105299, post:1950435042372591726, post:2090539059475046783). For memecoins he distilled fundamentals into narrative, community, liquidity/accessibility, and volume (post:1953798167931027690, post:1882859338244440567). For launchpads he reasons from revenue, market share, buy/burn flywheels, and whether a project survives its biggest competitor (post:2097341438917030215, post:2087152884026679792, post:2115280521215332818).

He makes narrative-plus-catalyst bets rather than chasing every new coin: ETFs, CEX listings, D.O.G.E, meme-stock events, China/Korea adoption, Robinhood distribution. He asks where attention goes next and positions before consensus (post:2095974106969588053, post:2098943739905359983).

## Risk attitude
He is a high-conviction, high-drawdown trader. He publicly reports six-, seven-, and eight-figure drops and says he conditions himself to treat the numbers as imaginary so losses do not faze him (post:1776021430884315193, post:1929575099608281509, post:2096885768014733719). He held BONK through 80% drawdowns and down-eight-figure periods (post:1796102767066403127, post:1876674463648940411). He says 60-90% dips are the price of 100x, and if you cannot stomach the dips you do not deserve the gains (post:1775914030638477650, post:1776026551219368281, post:2019785568792526961).

He repeatedly warns others against leverage even though he has used it heavily and sometimes blown up. Early he told people to tone down leverage and said most of his biggest wins are spot (post:1763741393799786847, post:1776025077085073649). He later admitted losing seven figures from greed and overtrading after getting into money (post:1813159285204894130). The climax was 10/10: all perps wiped out, an eight-figure loss, peak positions worth $30M+ gone (post:1977047126015459602, post:2095391343996539096). After that he had zero perps on core positions, preferred spot and chill, and kept advising others to stay away from leverage (post:2082807481210753042, post:1985303490999030123).

Sizing is deliberately asymmetric. He assumes every memecoin could go to zero and says only invest what you can afford to lose (post:1845063147171807694, post:1851048358036615590, post:1933608647973646633). He puts less than 1% into most trades, but will buy 1-3% of a token's supply on the highest-conviction plays (post:1953518789795909983, post:2107123654836506953). He accepts being wrong often and says one massive winner can erase months of losses (post:1932235802576085328, post:2079900400275083636).

## Recurring mistakes they admit
He is unusually public about losses. He posted a mid-six-figure L on overleveraged weekend longs including DOGE in 2024 (post:1769406925618192763, post:1769406931335094474). He admitted NEIRO thesis invalidation and said he lost seven figures from greed/overtrading after getting into money (post:1824535673740956009, post:1813159285204894130). He said he got carried out on smaller higher-leverage positions in the Feb 2025 crash (post:1886572123256402179). The largest was 10/10: all perps wiped out, including BONK and a 7-figure FARTCOIN position, peak positions over $30M (post:1977047126015459602, post:2095391343996539096). He later named overtrading after 10/10 as his biggest mistake, saying he should have stepped away instead of trying to make it back (post:2114047078116782093). He admitted being bad at taking profits historically and round-tripping eight figures chasing social clout or community loyalty, only later learning to be loyal to his P&L (post:2084617347621351700, post:2052788553893990515). He took a roughly $100k rug loss from borrowed conviction and said most losses cluster around borrowed conviction, not self-built theses (post:2108216523907006964). He admitted he was wrong about the 4-year cycle being dead (post:2072782315093205259, post:2087270834557595890). He also regrets not sizing in more aggressively at lows while waiting for a bigger dip (post:1996693006913564674, post:1997964238875234508).
