# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview

He starts from a simple structural belief: Crypto Twitter is not retail. CT is bag-biased, often wrong, and fades its own opportunity; the real retail bid comes later from Robinhood, Coinbase, Binance, and DINO muscle memory (post:1763656001532637440, post:1846649755910525119, post:1857324439223832665). That makes CT consensus a contrarian signal, not a source of alpha. He has repeated some version of this for two years: counter-trade CT when it is loud, hateful, or unanimous (post:1944672596366082227, post:1936155087530873157).

His second fixed belief is that memecoins are the purest expression of crypto attention. Early on he argued memecoins could have fundamentals: narrative, community, liquidity, accessibility, CEX listings, and volume decide winners (post:1770585064029474896, post:1818407937133408352, post:1953798167931027690). By 2024 he was calling $10b "the new $1b" and saying the memecoin top was not in (post:1764298459387953229, post:1792227571167281199, post:1862914241260359783). Later he sharpened this into the USELESS thesis: almost all of crypto is useless, so the coin that says so is the honest apex narrative (post:1943987507843965252, post:1954524844910919880, post:2004830435977646238).

He sees markets as repetitive and emotional, not fundamentally rational. Retail arrives late, chases familiar coins first, then trickles down into memecoins (post:1860251917122764996, post:1860282826345591274). Cycles follow a playbook: BTC leads, then ETH, then large caps, then low-cap memes (post:1953871238868218139). Human greed and psychology guarantee another mania phase because they have been consistent for centuries (post:1953890272602886191). At the same time, he believes attention and positioning matter more than discounted cash-flow reasoning (post:2008659295605235944). The winning positions sit at the extremes: giga-brain utility like $HYPE or pure degen uselessness like $USELESS, not the midcurve middle (post:2061720934231273503).

His macro view is aggressively bullish, but it has softened and shifted as the market hurt him. In 2024–2025 he argued rate cuts, Trump, the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, and crypto lagging stocks/gold made the bull case so strong that bears were "mentally ill" (post:1833235694170554687, post:1850588342875746771, post:1987541408165548466). After the October 10 liquidation event he admitted the structural damage was worse than FTX and that CEX opacity made the extent of pain hard to assess (post:2017674208688398809, post:1990124918231781627). He once declared the 4-year cycle dead; later he said he was wrong and expected the real move in Q4 (post:1997041392938352999, post:2072782315093205259, post:2087270834557595890).

Faith is not decorative for him. He attributes outcomes to God, says God is the biggest factor in his success, and treats losses as God's will (post:1859001983413236195, post:2093266527029961167, post:2095391343996539096). He also frames stepping away, going to church, and being with family as part of trading discipline (post:2104028251417125363, post:2114205311611318323).

## How they reason and decide

He uses four filters for a memecoin: narrative strength, community strength, accessibility/liquidity, and volume (post:1818407937133408352, post:1953798167931027690). Price going down is not automatically invalidation; what invalidates a thesis is the narrative dying, a rug, a dead community, or a better PvP project taking its attention (post:1862246244518645910, post:1872913119997309323). This is why he can hold through 80–90% dumps while others panic.

On-chain data is his main evidence. He tracks holder growth, whale accumulation, smart-money netflows, volume-to-market-cap, and OI-to-market-cap as leading indicators before price catches up (post:1857742424946196649, post:1950178237151105299, post:2066298266120077776, post:2109569775919841300). He often says volume precedes price (post:1942572531967267237, post:1973785196353769689). If new holders keep absorbing sells during a sell-off, he wants to buy the dip; if holders are shrinking, he treats that as worse than price (post:1950178237151105299).

He thinks in relative valuations. He compares market caps of similar coins and revenue multiples of comparable protocols: CAT versus POPCAT/MEW/MOG, FLOKI as a BNB/DOGE beta, PONS versus PUMP at a fraction of annualized revenue (post:1862624760846258548, post:1846143316821201189, post:2091618376137187518). He also compares CEX volume against market cap: if $USELESS is trading with volume close to $DOGE but at a tiny market cap, he treats that as mispricing (post:2109569775919841300).

He leans heavily on historical analogies: SHIB's 2021 rally, pre-explosion PEPE, 2023 PEPE's consolidation before $5B, FARTCOIN's early chart, GME 2021, CAKE last cycle, and the DOGE/SHIB/FLOKI rotation (post:1804235987666768209, post:2061823586914971821, post:1951230193080713283, post:1944874028805840971). He uses charts—falling wedges, cup-and-handles, breakouts, retests, long consolidations—but says he does not rely on TA alone (post:1848156366319284479, post:1855615921001259247, post:1984231678923952161).

Sentiment is a trading signal. Extreme fear, CT hate, coordinated FUD, sidelined capital, and "this is dead" energy are bullish to him (post:1768525471912772093, post:1819821500293382306, post:1934343748357648524). He buys when no one wants to talk about an asset and sells into euphoria (post:1840515686378143782). The same logic made him say the most hated rallies pump hardest (post:1920967516949803141, post:1923771042989842669).

He is catalyst-driven. CEX listings, ETF/ETP filings, buybacks and burns, CZ's return, the election, Roaring Kitty/GME news, and revenue protocol upgrades all enter his timing (post:1828530428271313032, post:1846514933216731535, post:1890356174832197770, post:1834636411309375903). But he says being early is about studying data, not about the first price seen; "too late" is cope if the data is still early (post:2079488793258643771).

For risk/reward he asks what the bear case actually is and whether the asymmetry favors longs. If an asset is down 90–98% and likely to survive, the downside is limited and the upside is multiples (post:2071627996784390415, post:2110392689417990409). In a new trend he prefers betting leaders and outperformers over the lowest cap, arguing reflexivity matters more than market cap alone (post:2007515223532462304). He also tries to anticipate attention flow: not "what is pumping right now?" but "where is attention going next and what is the biggest beneficiary?" (post:2095974106969588053).

## Risk attitude

He is openly a high-conviction, high-drawdown trader. He says he conditioned himself so no crypto loss fazes him and treats the numbers as imaginary (post:1776021430884315193). He accepts 60–90% dumps as the price of 100x, and says if you cannot stomach the dips you do not deserve the gains (post:1775914030638477650, post:1845063147171807694). He publicized watching BONK drop through several 80% drawdowns and staying in (post:1796102767066403127, post:1876674463648940411).

He loses publicly. He posted a mid-six-figure L on overleveraged weekend longs including DOGE, later admitted losing seven figures in days/weeks from greed and overtrading, and ultimately admitted the October 10 wipeout of eight figures on perps positions, including BONK and FARTCOIN (post:1769406925618192763, post:1813159285204894130, post:1977047126015459602, post:2095391343996539096). He continues to post large drawdowns: down $3.6M in 24 hours, down around $9M from peak, and mocked by the timeline (post:2096885768014733719, post:2097248578880098436, post:2110301178852519991).

His leverage view changed materially. In 2024 he repeatedly warned others to tone down leverage and said his biggest wins were spot (post:1763741393799786847, post:1776025077085073649). But he kept using large perp positions and paying huge funding, sometimes over $1M on BONK (post:1776024138068422717, post:1820184762293567989). After 10/10 he became much stricter: avoid leverage, use isolated margin, prefer spot, and do not trade perps off his targets because funding can liquidate you even if the thesis is right (post:1886572123256402179, post:1944522334947410272, post:1953408691182448794, post:1985303490999030123, post:2106108708069445861). By late 2026 he said he had zero perps exposure on core positions so he could stomach volatility (post:2095391343996539096).

His position-sizing approach is a mix of extreme concentration and small discipline. He often says never invest more than you can afford to lose, and generally puts less than 1% of portfolio into most trades (post:1953518789795909983, post:1933608647973646633). On highest-conviction plays he is willing to be aggressive—buying size, sometimes 1–3% of a token's supply, or accumulating $500k+ over several days (post:2107123654836506953, post:2096855993636290982). The goal is sizing wins so they greatly overwhelm losses, since he is often wrong (post:1932235802576085328).

His profit-taking philosophy changed. Early he called "take profit" the two most dangerous words at the start of a bull run (post:1764351140685115830). After round-tripping huge wins he became explicit: take some profits to secure a comfortable life, and take profits routinely rather than being blindly loyal to bags or communities (post:1953541799743758689, post:2084617347621351700). He has said he was historically bad at taking profits and had to grow out of it (post:2052788553893990515).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

**Memecoin supercycle — bullish, unchanged in core.** He believed from 2024 onward that memecoins are the sector of this cycle, that retail had not arrived, and that memecoins would top only when the whole market topped (post:1763560840458813831, post:1792227571167281199, post:1848152070899327181). After brutal drawdowns he still rejected "memecoins are dead" (post:1986711441819210084, post:2003453355692081220). The supercycle framing later narrowed into "one $DOGE/$SHIB-type runner emerges each cycle" and the argument that $USELESS is that runner (post:2091256125803151722, post:2112936955919151539).

**BONK — bullish from the first batch to the end.** He fought BONK FUD constantly, held through 80% drawdowns, called a deca-billion play on buybacks, unit bias, CEX listings and ETF filings, and attacked WIF fans and BONK bears (post:1764652264998715839, post:1796102767066403127, post:1855354040173130024, post:1944696030504644669). It remained a core Solana beta and "fastest horse" pick for him (post:1880288626585596313, post:2007230780385186190).

**FLOKI — bullish as levered beta.** He framed it as a DOGE/BNB/PEPE beta: if those pumped, long FLOKI (post:1814697389820649973). He kept this stance through 2026, citing BNB chain season and Elon catalysts (post:1973718270818615671, post:1980272209819308506).

**Dogecoin and the D.O.G.E narrative — bullish, then more selective.** He fused Dogecoin with Elon Musk's "Department of Government Efficiency" memecoin as the two doges and a dominant Trump-era narrative (post:1846234409910804626, post:1856533063678771437, post:1862246244518645910). He later admitted round-tripping tens of millions on DOGEGOV, MAGA, and GME meta coins, and said he preferred timeless narratives over event-based ones (post:1953553490845806702).

**Cat memecoins / Simon's Cat — bullish and stubborn.** He called cats underrated versus dogs and repeatedly committed not to sell a single CAT token before $1B (post:1772245492849594760, post:1845128468104687968, post:1875152881432567924).

**BTC memecoins / Runes — early bullish, later faded from center.** In 2024 he called them the easiest 50–100x and the most asymmetric bet (post:1842522339415846995, post:1851231246463967505). They never became his main 2025–2026 trade; USELESS and ecosystem trades took over.

**RWA and AI — bullish but secondary.** He kept a clear hierarchy: memecoins first, RWA second, AI third (post:1812962237554041212, post:1915733774945362124). RWA was a small-category underrated play; AI was a huge wealth-creation narrative but mostly a rotation trade, not his core (post:1770793300548583816, post:1860363821908373536).

**BonkFun / LetsBONKfun — bullish, became a crusade.** He promoted it as a Pumpfun disruptor with better economics, then declared it had flipped Pumpfun and was "the trenches" (post:1921699995926900819, post:1941835070127456289, post:1952961808987275592). He opposed the PUMP TGE as "the great extraction" (post:1942522044584710448). Later he apologized for past aggression toward Pumpfun (post:2094346586771829123).

**Pumpfun / PUMP — bearish to mixed.** He turned hard against Pumpfun during the BonkFun war, calling it extractive and claiming its growth came from the BONK cabal (post:1940338468846268895, post:1952961808987275592). His later stance softened enough to disclose respect and apology (post:2094346586771829123).

**USELESS — his final boss narrative.** Starting in 2025, he made USELESS the centerpiece: all memecoins are useless, so the one that admits it is the honest blue-chip; he held through 90%+ drawdowns and argued it would be the cycle's DOGE/SHIB/PEPE runner (post:1923498440073330892, post:1933530942158623219, post:2019785568792526961, post:2095145010832257102).

**Solana — bullish to mixed.** He believed SOL won the cycle and could flip ETH, with BONK as its main meme (post:1880646081500918020, post:1880288626585596313). By late 2026 he criticized Solana's extraction culture and rotators (post:2089277573729566779), though he acknowledged the cultural shift when leadership engaged the trenches (post:2097652983567700107).

**ETH — critical to bullish.** He called high gas fees elitist and said marketing would not fix it (post:1791139374756745297). Later he turned tactically bullish on a hated rally to $10K+ and ETH as the vehicle for big money to fight back against Solana (post:1945492912009015345, post:1954310223130235160, post:2094794638741623125).

**Utility/revenue protocols — bearish to selectively bullish.** He mocked the "institutions only want revenue protocols" narrative with data showing top revenue tokens down while memecoins led attention (post:2003787973045756085, post:2008659295605235944). Later he argued utility tokens are ridiculously cheap at the lows of a new chain ecosystem and built the $HYPE x $USELESS barbell thesis (post:2067975447967654386, post:2061720934231273503). This became very visible in his PONS and Robinhood-chain work (post:2091551249485238638, post:2092549016659636544).

**Robinhood Chain and PONS — bullish, late-cycle centerpiece.** He called Robinhood Chain the chain of the new cycle, praised Vlad Tenev for embracing memecoins, and argued PONS was undervalued on revenue multiples versus PUMP (post:2075525662920642599, post:2086069162682839134, post:2091551249485238638).

**Privacy / Nullmask — bullish new theme.** Near the end of the notes he argued privacy was the narrative of the cycle and $MASK sat at its center with a working product and real revenue (post:2111110757735600482, post:2112917849152831795).
