# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview
He treats Crypto Twitter as a biased, bag-holding in-group that is usually wrong about what retail will do. The refrain is explicit: “CT is NOT retail” (post:1763656001532637440), and he repeatedly says fading CT consensus is often the trade (post:1766623133388202142, post:1944672596366082227). Retail, in his model, is late, unironic, and chases the biggest, most familiar names once mania starts; CT is too online and too self-aware to lead that move (post:1846649755910525119, post:1857324439223832665).

His core macro belief is that crypto is in a long risk-on regime and that deep corrections are normal, not terminal. Early on he said the real bull run had barely started and altseason was still coming (post:1764652285609615403, post:1767120074840629438). That did not change in essence even after violent events: in 2025–2026 he kept saying the cycle top was not in, that memecoins routinely correct 70–99% and then make new highs, and that fear is a buy signal (post:1986711441819210084, post:1997041392938352999, post:2003107147186360636). A rare explicit reversal: in late 2025 he said “the 4-year cycle is dead” (post:1997041392938352999), then later admitted he was wrong and shifted to expecting the real move in Q4 (post:2072782315093205259, post:2087270834557595890).

His memecoin thesis changed substantially. In 2024 he argued memecoins can have fundamentals, with BONK and FLOKI using meme identity to accelerate adoption of underlying utility/value systems (post:1770585064029474896, post:1764353977842377009). By mid-2025 he had moved to the opposite aesthetic: all memecoins are essentially useless, and the most honest expression of the sector is USELESS (post:1954524844910919880, post:2004830435977646238, post:2012218826457321516). This is the intellectual backbone of his later concentration.

He also changed his view on political/event-driven memes. In 2024 he called political/Trump memecoins “the greatest trade opportunity of the cycle” and said Trump being a crypto bull was not priced in (post:1794763746382114816, post:1815033664285085852). Later he admitted he round-tripped tens of millions on DOGEGOV, MAGA, and GME meta and said he learned to prefer timeless narratives over event-based ones (post:1953553490845806702). That lesson shapes his later USELESS focus.

Chains rotate in his worldview. Early he was intensely Solana/BONK and BNB-chain bullish (post:1880646081500918020, post:1864099130240532858). He later became critical of Solana’s extraction culture while still respecting its technical and memecoin position (post:2089277573729566779). By 2026 he was arguing Robinhood Chain is “THE chain of this cycle” (post:2093412994810077221, post:2106021394966540608), and that BNB is the next ecosystem to explode because CZ and Yi He are aligned (post:2093412994810077221). He frames chain preference as a trade, not an identity: if Base or Robinhood or Solana leads, he wants the leader.

Faith is central and is posted without hedging. He repeatedly attributes success to God and says gains are God’s will, not his own skill (post:1859001983413236195, post:2093266527029961167, post:2095391343996539096).

## How they reason and decide
He is a narrative-and-data bricoleur, not a pure TA trader. He uses historical analogs constantly: SHIB 2021, DOGE/SHIB 2021, PEPE 2023, FARTCOIN, BONK, FLOKI. The argument is almost always comparative: if comparable asset X did Y, then this cheaper asset can reprice (post:1804235987666768209, post:1862624760846258548, post:2097266739230732454). He maps market caps and liquidity rather than trusting his own emotional read.

His memecoin fundamentals are: narrative, community, access/liquidity, and volume. He states this explicitly in a teaching thread: judge a memecoin on “narrative, community, and volume as the real measure of liquidity” (post:1953798167931027690). He also checks holder growth, smart-money and whale flows, CEX volume rankings, OI-to-market-cap, and volume-to-market-cap ratios as leading indicators (post:1950178237151105299, post:2066298266120077776, post:2090539059475046783). For launchpads and protocols, he compares revenue multiples and buyback/burn flywheels, e.g. PONS at roughly 0.66x annual revenue versus PUMP at 4.35x (post:2091618376137187518).

Sentiment is a contrarian input. Heavy FUD, gravedancing, pocket-watching, and “cycle top” consensus are treated as bullish fuel, partly because sidelined capital remains to be squeezed in (post:1934343748357648524, post:1941603713606717948, post:2063660234116104639). He says the massive runners are born when the environment feels most counterproductive (post:1923690446749892947, post:2057753963542233522).

He also reads relative strength during market-wide selloffs. If an asset refuses to make new lows or bounces harder than BTC/SOL/memes, he treats that as evidence of the strongest horse (post:1805361314732425575, post:1950653032846291259, post:2063955936725578036). His game plan is often: identify the leader, watch volume precede price, and buy the dip before the crowd returns (post:1942572531967267237, post:2115063918330564728).

A recurring mental model is the three-step runner cycle: introductory pump, brutal 90%+ pullback and long consolidation, then an explosive rally out of nowhere (post:1927462109324960188, post:1923690446749892947). This is why he says he is not primarily an early-entry trader; his edge is HODLing with dogged conviction (post:2110675458396205478).

## Risk attitude
He is publicly transparent about losing often. “I’m usually wrong, more so than right” is a repeated disclaimer (post:1806746737048002608, post:1934336930491056475). His PnL swings are posted in the open: mid-six-figure early losses, seven-figure drawdowns, eight-figure roundtrips, and the 10/10 wipeout where all perps positions were liquidated (post:1769406925618192763, post:1813159285204894130, post:1977047126015459602, post:2052788553893990515). He frames these as the cost of the game, not as proof the thesis is broken.

Deep drawdowns are expected. He has said 60–90% dips are standard for memecoins and do not invalidate a thesis if narrative, team, community, and on-chain metrics remain intact (post:1862246244518645910, post:1872913119997309323, post:2019785568792526961). He accepts watching public PnL fall $10m+ on BONK and holding anyway (post:1796102767066403127, post:1812965725902168468).

Leverage is the clearest lesson. He warned against leverage early while still using it (post:1763741393799786847, post:1775247426246295934), got carried out on smaller high-leverage positions in early 2025 (post:1886572123256402179), and after the 10/10 liquidation he turned hard against perps: zero perps on core positions, spot and chill, avoid leverage or keep it extremely low (post:2082807481210753042, post:2095391343996539096). He says leverage has been a net negative (post:1985303490999030123).

He also insists followers cannot blindly copy him. He is not a guru, he does not give guaranteed calls, and he tells people to form their own thesis (post:1856825951239221746, post:1911077819167256622, post:1950117974016180602).
