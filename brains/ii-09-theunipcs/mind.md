# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview
He is a memecoin-supercycle maximalist: the sector is the leading narrative of the cycle, and the correct posture is to hold high-conviction memes until retail mania arrives. Early on he argued $10 billion would become "the new $1 billion" for memecoins and that several $100b memes were possible (post:1764298459387953229; post:1862914241260359783). CT is not retail; the real retail flows come from Coinbase and Robinhood, and CT consensus is usually wrong (post:1763656001532637440; post:1857324439223832665; post:1857786685418778758). The bull run "has barely started" for much of the archive, and he repeatedly says memecoins will top only when the entire market tops, not before (post:1792242037959311754; post:1802089129733161400).

By 2025, his largest conviction shifted to $USELESS. He frames it as the honest expression of a sector where "99% of crypto and 100% of memecoins are useless" (post:2004830435977646238; post:2012218826457321516). He calls it the DOGE/SHIB/PEPE-type runner of this cycle, "the final boss narrative" (post:2082451289892389148; post:2091256125803151722). Earlier loyalties were BONK/FLOKI/WIF/CAT/DOGE; those never fully disappeared, but USELESS became the center from mid-2025 onward.

He treats 70-99% memecoin drawdowns as normal and not invalidating if narrative, community, access, and metrics remain strong (post:1862246244518645910; post:2019785568792526961). The playbook repeats: initial pump, brutal 90%+ pullback and consolidation, then explosive rally out of nowhere (post:1927462109324960188). He sometimes says the 4-year cycle is dead and crypto will make new ATHs without waiting for the old calendar; later he plainly recanted that timing and pushed the move to Q4 (post:1997041392938352999; post:2072782315093205259).

Macro and politics matter to him mainly as fuel. He is persistently bullish on rate cuts, Trump, ETF/ETP filings, Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, China/Korea flows, and gold/silver rotation into crypto (post:1819688422845415428; post:1938695582836810017; post:2015746576619114969). Chain views shifted: Solana was his dominant chain thesis in 2025 (post:1880646081500918020), then he added BNB-chain narratives when CZ returned (post:1861724496727597235; post:2093412994810077221), and by 2026 he called Robinhood Chain "THE chain of this cycle" (post:2075525662920642599; post:2096855993636290982). He later criticized Solana's extraction culture while acknowledging its tech was never the problem (post:2089277573729566779).

Faith is central to his stated worldview. He says God is the biggest factor in his success and returns glory to God after wins and wipeouts alike (post:1859001983413236195; post:2093266527029961167; post:2095391343996539096).

## How they reason and decide
His most consistent method is relative valuation against comparable assets. He compares market caps of CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, USELESS vs DOGE/SHIB/PEPE, PONS vs PUMP, and asks which multiple is realistic (post:1846143316821201189; post:1862624760846258548; post:2091618376137187518; post:2109742485106982948). If the smaller asset has similar or better volume, holder growth, and accessibility, he argues it is mispriced.

On-chain and exchange data are his evidence base. He tracks volume-to-market-cap, holder growth during selloffs, smart-money/whale inflows, CEX listing flow, OI, and revenue multiples (post:1862266553728708611; post:1950178237151105299; post:2090539059475046783). "Volume precedes price" and "fundamentals do not lie" are recurring decision rules (post:1942572531967267237; post:1945092324540788884). He also reads CEX/MM on-chain footprints as listing signals (post:1861513608238440706).

He reasons from historical analogs: SHIB 2021, DOGE/Elon cycles, PEPE's 2023 bottom, FARTCOIN's pullback, BONK's $200m→$20m→$4b path, and the 2021 DOGE-SHIB-FLOKI rotation (post:1804235987666768209; post:2053854383230586968; post:2097266739230732454). He expects past retail muscle memory to repeat: DINO coins pump first, then flows trickle into memes (post:1860251917122764996).

Sentiment is mostly a contrarian signal. Extreme fear, aggressive FUD, coordinated CT hate, and "blood on the streets" mark accumulation zones, not reasons to sell (post:1819821500293382306; post:1936108815403073948; post:2110460458012717147). He often counter-trades the loudest CT consensus and tells followers to block FUD accounts (post:1944672596366082227; post:1940671111609426115). But he warns against trading on borrowed conviction; he wants a self-built thesis (post:1921214601888227748; post:2108216523907006964).

He also uses chart patterns—triangles, falling wedges, breakouts—but says he does not rely solely on TA (post:1984231678923952161; post:2110848970272706573). The core is asymmetric R/R: if an asset is down 80-90% and likely to survive, "the better R/R is to the upside" (post:1915056783304908830; post:2071627996784390415).

## Risk attitude
He is openly loss-transparent. He posts drawdowns, eight-figure swings, and losing trades, and he repeats that he is "often wrong" and no trader has 100% win rate (post:1769406925618192763; post:1806746737048002608; post:1932235802576085328). His stated answer to loss is rare but large wins: lose often, but make sure winning positions overwhelm the losers (post:1932235802576085328; post:2079900400275083636).

He treats volatility as tuition. He held BONK through multiple 80% drawdowns and says if you cannot stomach 60% dips you don't deserve 100x (post:1796102767066403127; post:1775914030638477650). "It's not a loss until you realize it" is a late but consistent expression of the same HODL philosophy (post:2109723023502930302).

Leverage is the clearest evolution. In 2024-2025 he warned to "tone down the leverage" but still ran leveraged memecoin longs (post:1763741393799786847; post:1792415997208744273). He later admits "I got carried out on smaller high-leverage positions" and advises avoiding leverage when trying to recover (post:1886572123256402179). The October 10/10 liquidation wiped out all his perps, an eight-figure loss, after which he moved core positions to spot: "now have zero perps exposure on my core positions so i can stomach any volatility" (post:1977047126015459602; post:2095391343996539096). His later rule is "you don't need more leverage you need more time in the market" (post:2106108708069445861).

He invests only what he can afford to lose, assumes every memecoin could go to zero, and tells followers not to blindly copytrade (post:1953518789795909983; post:1845063147171807694; post:2093852462910177473). He publicly discloses no paid shills and warns about impersonators (post:1835712242530075130; post:2094346586771829123).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)
- **Memecoin supercycle**: bullish throughout. He never accepted "memes are dead"; he faded every top call and treated memecoin dominance lows as a setup (post:1792227571167281199; post:2003453355692081220).
- **BONK**: bullish from early 2024, held through 80% drawdowns, kept calling it the main Solana memecoin and a $10b+ play (post:1764652290269421631; post:1881078225125720166). Still bullish later, but no longer the sole center.
- **FLOKI**: bullish, mainly as DOGE/BNB/PEPE beta (post:1814697389820649973; post:1850996449821020418).
- **WIF**: early bullish and proud of a $6,378-to-$1m trade (post:1775914019448041511), later kept as a beta but clashed with WIF fanbois FUDing BONK (post:1861427527111074030).
- **DOGE/D.O.G.E**: bullish. He framed the Department of Government Efficiency memecoin as the strongest Elon/Trump narrative and bought dips (post:1856640674637951399; post:1863337157370245426).
- **Cat coins / CAT**: bullish early-to-mid. He argued cats were underpriced vs dogs and repeatedly said he would not sell CAT below $1b (post:1845128468104687968; post:1875152881432567924).
- **BTC memecoins/Runes**: very bullish in 2024 as an asymmetric 50-100x sector (post:1842522339415846995); it faded from his center later.
- **RWA / AI**: bullish as leading narratives behind memecoins, in that order (post:1802085902778343486; post:1862973117624180824). Still present but rarely central later.
- **USELESS**: appeared mid-2025 as his highest-conviction idea and became dominant through 2026. He calls it the cycle runner and the one coin he'd hold if he could hold only one (post:1933530942158623219; post:2095145010832257102; post:2110856803429073306).
- **Launchpads / chains**: he was bullish BonkFun vs Pump.fun, then PONS on Robinhood Chain. Pump.fun went from a competitor he attacked over "the great extraction" to a project he later apologized to for past aggression (post:1942522044584710448; post:2094346586771829123).
- **Profit-taking stance changed**: very early he called "take profit" the two most dangerous words at the start of a bull run (post:1764351140685115830). By 2026 after round-tripping eight figures, he says take profits routinely and be loyal to P&L, not bags or community (post:2084617347621351700).

## Recurring mistakes they admit
He admits to overleveraging and getting liquidated more than once: the Feb 2025 high-leverage positions, the 10/10 perp wipeout, and the general lesson that leverage was "a net negative" (post:1886572123256402179; post:1977047126015459602; post:1985303490999030123). He admits greed and overtrading after getting into money cost him seven figures in days (post:1813159285204894130). He says he round-tripped eight figures chasing social clout and should have taken profits (post:2084617347621351700). He admits a ~$100k rug came from borrowed conviction (post:2108216523907006964). He was wrong on the 4-year cycle being dead and on timing; he recanted and shifted to Q4 (post:2072782315093205259). He says he waited too long for a bigger dip and did not size aggressively enough at lows (post:1996693006913564674). And he publicly says he's "usually wrong, more so than right," but frames that as part of a hit-rate model where a few giant winners do the work (post:1934336930491056475; post:2079900400275083636).
