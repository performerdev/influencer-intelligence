# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview

He is a memecoin degen who became famous as "bonk guy" after riding BONK from small caps to multi-billions. His core belief is that CT (Crypto Twitter) is not retail, and CT consensus is usually wrong (post:1763656001532637440, post:1857324439223832665). He repeated this for years: CT is biased toward its own bags, misses real retail flows, and fades the narratives that actually rip. His edge is counter-trading the most vocal CT takes, especially when they call tops (post:1790826316729565301, post:1936155087530873157).

His strongest repeated thesis is that memecoins are the leading narrative of the cycle and the "memecoin supercycle" is not over. He argued this from April 2024 through 2026, even after brutal drawdowns (post:1763560840458813831, post:1792227571167281199, post:1857541280823406881, post:1997041392938352999). He pushed the idea that "$10 billion is the new $1 billion for memecoins" (post:1764298459387953229, post:1862914241260359783). He argued memecoins can have fundamentals: narrative strength, community strength, liquidity/accessibility, and CEX listings determine winners (post:1770585064029474896, post:1818407937133408352, post:1953798167931027690). Later he refined this into "fundamentals do not lie" (post:1945092324540788884, post:1945196489799545307).

His view of market structure: retail had barely arrived, altseason was yet to come, and the real bull run had barely started (post:1764652285609615403, post:1798735836265263417, post:1855354040173130024). He believed institutions and tradfi would pile in, and stocks/gold making ATHs while crypto lagged meant crypto would follow aggressively (post:1819688422845415428, post:1819698451443036279, post:1970199967181787260).

By late 2025 he sharpened his view: almost all of crypto is useless, and memecoins are the purest expression of that. So USELESS became his ultimate narrative — the one coin honest about being a useless coin (post:1933530942158623219, post:1954524844910919880, post:2004830435977646238, post:2095145010832257102). He framed USELESS as "the DOGE/SHIB/PEPE-type runner of this cycle" (post:2095128612378685824, post:2109569775919841300). He also argued Holder growth during sell-offs is the single most important signal, and smart money/whale accumulation confirms a thesis (post:1950178237151105299, post:2109569775919841300).

On chains, he early called Solana the cycle winner and said it could flip ETH (post:1875464863859847448, post:1880646081500918020, post:1959613425035686234). He argued ETH gas fees were elitist and would hurt it (post:1791139374756745297, post:1791140047837635010). Later he flipped to Robinhood Chain as THE chain of this cycle, while also saying BNB Chain season would continue and "never fade CZ and Yi He" (post:2093412994810077221, post:2094884614699397255, post:2095974106969588053). He also argued BonkFun had flipped Pumpfun as the launchpad of the trenches (post:1941835070127456289, post:1952961808987275592).

Faith is central to his late voice. He attributes gains to God, says "GOD WILLING" constantly, and framed his 10/10 wipeout as "God's will" (post:1859001983413236195, post:2093266527029961167, post:2095391343996539096, post:2095974106969588053). He says faith gives emotional detachment from portfolio swings, which improves trading decisions (post:2093266527029961167).

## How they reason and decide

He rarely uses pure TA. He says he studies historical patterns, on-chain flows, catalysts, and sentiment extremes (post:1768525475922477271, post:1859187653586886964, post:1941603713606717948). His go-to methods:

- **Market cap comparisons** to argue a coin is undervalued versus peers: CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, BTC memes vs ETH/SOL memes, USELESS vs DOGE (post:1846143316821201189, post:1920596665108472230, post:2109742485106982948, post:2091618376137187518).
- **On-chain metrics** — holder growth, volume, whale inflows, OI — as evidence before price catches up (post:1857742424946196649, post:1950178237151105299, post:2109569775919841300).
- **Historical analogues** — SHIB 2021, PEPE 2023, SAFEMOON 2021, GME 2021 — to project memecoin moves (post:1804235987666768209, post:1944874028805840971, post:2061823586914971821, post:2095416964852158565).
- **Relative strength** — bid what holds up or pumps against BTC/ETH bloodbaths (post:1805361314732425575, post:1933487381950124299, post:2063955936725578036).
- **Sentiment as contrarian signal** — extreme fear, FUD, grave dancing, and "the timeline is dead" mark bottoms (post:1768525471912772093, post:1957901813270909436, post:2063660234116104639). He says "FUD is bullish" (post:2006810407537881302, post:2008209045543375037).
- **Catalysts** — CEX listings, ETF filings, political events, buy/burn mechanisms, CZ release, Roaring Kitty (post:1828530428271313032, post:1846514933216731535, post:1797527616616054899, post:1855354040173130024).
- **Volume precedes price** — consistent on-chain and CEX volume always precedes multi-billion-dollar memecoin runs (post:1942572531967267237, post:1973785196353769689).

He describes his thesis on memecoin selection in three or four parts: narrative strength, community strength, and access/liquidity (volume), later adding accessibility/CEX listings (post:1818407937133408352, post:1953798167931027690, post:2011445418362642617). He warns never to take a trade on borrowed conviction — losses cluster around that (post:1921214601888227748, post:2108216523907006964).

He also says he doesn't rely on 1-minute charts, thinks lining up with a plan and DCA beats FOMO, and says the smarter move is often to position immediately at 80-90% drawdowns rather than wait for a slightly better entry (post:1797681493768286283, post:1915056783304908830, post:1920516286733250857).

## Risk attitude

He treats invested capital as disposable and says losses don't faze him (post:1776021430884315193, post:1953741443371000275). He accepts 60-90% dips as standard for memecoins and says if you can't stomach the dips you don't deserve the gains (post:1775914030638477650, post:1776026551219368281). He held BONK through 80% drawdowns and periods of being down 8 figures (post:1796102767066403127, post:1876674463648940411). He publicly shares drawdowns — down $3.6M in 24h, down ~$9M from peak (post:2096885768014733719, post:2097248578880098436).

His relationship with leverage changed. Early on he used it heavily and defended paying $1M+ funding on BONK (post:1776024138068422717, post:1820184762293567989). He warned often against high leverage (post:1763741393799786847, post:1852061323137552573). After the 10/10 liquidation, when he got wiped out of eight figures on perps including BONK and a 7-figure FARTCOIN position, he became much more explicit: avoid leverage, buy spot and chill, you need time in the market not more leverage (post:1977047126015459602, post:2006433668730908994, post:1985303490999030123, post:2106108708069445861). He now holds zero perps on core positions to stomach volatility (post:2095391343996539096).

He sizes wins to overwhelm losses, acknowledges being wrong often, and says most of his biggest trades were spot (post:1776025077085073649, post:1932235802576085328, post:1910687854272942413). He assumes 100% loss on every memecoin buy (post:1845063147171807694, post:1953518789795909983).
