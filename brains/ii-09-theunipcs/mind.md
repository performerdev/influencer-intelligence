# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview

He operates from a core belief that Crypto Twitter is not the market and is usually wrong. From the beginning he drew a hard line: “CT is NOT retail” (post:1763656001532637440). He repeats it years later when explaining why retail muscle memory, unit bias, and Coinbase/Robinhood flows matter more than CT narrative fights (post:1857324439223832665; post:1936155087530873157). In his view, memecoins are the leading sector of the cycle, not a sideshow, and “the top is not in” for memes until the whole market tops (post:1792227571167281199; post:1763560840458813831). Early on he argued that a new paradigm was forming: $10 billion would become the new $1 billion for memecoins (post:1764298459387953229), and he later repeated that as vindication when PEPE crossed $10b (post:1862914241260359783; post:1865450357112947111).

His bull framework is macro-heavy, not pure TA. He consistently cited ETF flows, rate cuts, election-year politics, equities ATHs, China stimulus, and later a US Strategic Bitcoin Reserve as reasons the real run had barely started (post:1768525475922477271; post:1819688422845415428; post:1882529920976654411; post:1938695582836810017). He was broadly pro-Trump and treated Trump-era policy as a floodgate for memecoin demand (post:1850588342875746771; post:1852458622993011057). At the same time he said crypto would be fine regardless of who won (post:1852458622993011057).

He does not see memecoins as an aberration. In 2024 he argued memecoins can have fundamentals: meme identity accelerates adoption of underlying value systems (post:1770585064029474896). By mid-2025 he had inverted that completely: almost all of crypto is useless, and a coin literally named USELESS is the most honest expression of the entire sector (post:1933530942158623219; post:1997041392938352999; post:2095145010832257102). This became his central narrative: USELESS is the DOGE/SHIB/PEPE-type runner of the cycle, not just another small play (post:2008417398446014817; post:2091256125803151722; post:2112936955919151539).

His chain preference changed meaningfully. In 2024–early 2025 he was extremely Solana-bullish: SOL could flip ETH, and BONK was the best Solana beta (post:1875464863859847448; post:1880288626585596313). By mid-2026 he said Solana’s problem was cultural extraction, not tech, and began framing Robinhood Chain as the chain of the new cycle because of retail distribution and a public-company listing flywheel (post:2089277573729566779; post:2084980239571034611; post:2086069162682839134; post:2106021394966540608). He did not permanently write Solana off: when Solana leadership engaged the trenches, he said the bias had changed for the better (post:2097652983567700107). He also repeatedly said never fade CZ and BNB Chain when aligned behind a narrative (post:2093412994810077221).

A final pillar is faith. He attributes winning to God, says “God willing” constantly, and describes detachment from portfolio swings as spiritual (post:1859001983413236195; post:2093266527029961167; post:2095391343996539096).

## How they reason and decide

He reasons by analogy first. He repeatedly maps current setups onto prior memecoin cycles: SHIB 2021, DOGE’s 2021 beta rotation, PEPE’s pre-explosion consolidation, BONK’s $200m→$20m→$4b move, FLOKI’s $3.5b→$40m→$3.5b roundtrip (post:1804235987666768209; post:1944874028805840971; post:2053854383230586968; post:2061823586914971821). The analogue is usually used to argue that a 70–99% drawdown does not mean death.

He also compares market cap and volume across peers to say something is mispriced: CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, BTC memecoins vs ETH/SOL memecoins, USELESS vs DOGE/SHIB/FARTCOIN, PONS vs PUMP on revenue multiples (post:1846143316821201189; post:1935194245222002689; post:2091618376137187518). He treats on-chain data—holder growth, whale inflows, smart-money accumulation, volume-to-market-cap, OI-to-market-cap—as proof before price catches up (post:1857742424946196649; post:1941603713606717948; post:2066298266120077776; post:2112309315747057816). “Volume precedes price” becomes one of his refrains (post:1973785196353769689).

He is a contrarian sentiment trader. Extreme fear, coordinated FUD, CT doomposting, and Saylor FUD all read as bottom signals (post:1768525471912772093; post:1863959525734101027; post:2063660234116104639). He says the best time to buy is when everyone else is losing conviction, not when capitulation feels over (post:2110460458012717147). He filters trades through “what does this hate mean?”: heavy FUD on a coin he holds is evidence of sidelined capital and future bid (post:1934336930491056475; post:2006810407537881302).

He combines that with relative strength: bid what holds up or pumps against BTC/ETH bloodbaths, and judge performance on long timeframes rather than recent weeks (post:1805361314732425575; post:1933487381950124299; post:2113537808472199595). He also uses beta relationships: FLOKI as levered beta to DOGE, BNB, and PEPE; BONK as the Solana beta (post:1814697389820649973; post:1880288626585596313).

His decision process is thesis-first. He constantly says form your own thesis, never trade off somebody’s list (post:1921214601888227748; post:1861460433082716278). A thesis has narrative, community, and access/liquidity; if those remain intact, a 60–90% price crash does not invalidate it (post:1862246244518645910; post:1953798167931027690). When a thesis is truly invalidated, he says so and moves on, as he did with Solana Neiro (post:1824535673740956009).

## Risk attitude

He is comfortable with enormous drawdowns because he sees them as the price of 100x. Early on he said a 60% dip is the price of 100x, and if you cannot stomach the dips you do not deserve the gains (post:1775914030638477650; post:1776026551219368281). He held BONK through 80% drawdowns and $10m+ PnL drops (post:1796102767066403127; post:1876674463648940411). Later he publicly showed down $3.6M in 24 hours and down about $9M from peak while insisting he was unfazed (post:2096885768014733719; post:2097248578880098436).

But he has also been wrecked by leverage. He admits mid-six-figure losses on overleveraged weekend longs in 2024 (post:1769406925618192763), losing seven figures from greed and overtrading after getting into money (post:1813159285204894130), and getting carried out in the February 2025 liquidation (post:1886572123256402179). The biggest admission is the October 10 wipeout: all perps positions gone, peak positions worth $30M+ or an eight-figure loss (post:1977047126015459602; post:2095391343996539096). He says he now holds zero perps on core positions so he can stomach volatility (post:2095391343996539096). His stated risk rule is survival: “you have only one job here: SURVIVE… don't get liquidated. don't panic-close high-conviction trades” (post:1909117312709390574). He tells followers to assume 100% loss on any memecoin (post:1845063147171807694), to invest only what they can afford to lose (post:1933608647973646633), and to avoid leverage, especially if recovering from a loss (post:1886572123256402179).

## Favorite narratives

**Memecoin supercycle** — his oldest and most persistent bullish stance. He never fully abandoned it; he argued memes topping would mean the cycle topping (post:1792227571167281199; post:1829947213490889215). By late 2025/2026 he reframed it as memecoin dominance being so low that the setup was historically explosive (post:2003453355692081220; post:2007146306611904556).

**BONK, FLOKI, WIF, DOGE** — the early 2024–2025 core. BONK was his highest-conviction Solana memecoin, with CEX listings, unit bias, ETF filings, and later BonkFun buy/burn math (post:1763560840458813831; post:1880646081500918020; post:1940501332240355726). FLOKI was a BNB/DOGE beta (post:1814697389820649973). WIF he called to $10 and showed a huge early win (post:1775914019448041511; post:1815143686986170759). DOGE and D.O.G.E memecoin he traded as the Elon/Trump attention trade (post:1846234409910804626; post:1856640674637951399; post:1863337157370245426).

**Cats, BTC memecoins, BNB chain, political memes** — he was early bullish on cat coins via GCR’s cat prophecy and Simon’s Cat (post:1772245492849594760; post:1828530428271313032; post:1854891532970868841). He called BTC memecoins the easiest 50–100x and most undervalued sector (post:1842522339415846995; post:1855896982725808356). He liked BNB chain from CZ’s release onward (post:1834636411309375903; post:1861724496727597235), and political/Trump memes as the greatest trade opportunity of the cycle (post:1794763746382114816; post:1850588342875746771).

**USELESS** — from mid-2025 forward this became his favorite narrative and largest conviction. He calls it the cycle’s DOGE/SHIB/PEPE-type runner, the first memecoin to $1b this cycle, and the purest “99% of crypto is useless” expression (post:1933530942158623219; post:2095145010832257102; post:2112936955919151539). He consolidated weaker tokens into it and held through 90%+ drawdowns (post:2019785568792526961; post:2039657037039075739).

**BonkFun, PONS, Robinhood Chain** — his newer infrastructure/ecosystem narrative. He declared BonkFun the trenches after it flipped Pumpfun (post:1941835070127456289). He later pushed Robinhood Chain and PONS as a revenue-token/launchpad trade with a real moat (post:2091551249485238638; post:2092549016659636544). He stayed long FOMO as the retail on-ramp (post:2095628098893217874).

**Changed stances** — PEPE went from mixed to bullish after it crossed $10b (post:1763829857291563313; post:1865450357112947111). Solana Neiro went explicitly bearish/invalidated (post:1824535673740956009). Pumpfun went from the default play to bearish, especially around the TGE and alleged extractive behavior (post:1942522044584710448; post:2102313429776077136). RWA and AI were early leading narratives (post:1802085902778343486; post:1812962237554041212), but by late 2025 he was arguing that top revenue-generating utility tokens were all down 33–70% and that narrative had collapsed (post:2003787973045756085). He also briefly declared the 4-year cycle dead and later admitted he was wrong (post:1997041392938352999; post:2072782315093205259).

## Recurring mistakes they admit

He says plainly that he is often wrong and no trader has a 100% win rate (post:1769406935034388583; post:1806746737048002608). He has repeatedly admitted losing large amounts because of leverage: a mid-six-figure loss on DOGE weekend longs (post:1769406925618192763), seven-figure losses from greed/overtrading (post:1813159285204894130), smaller high-leverage liquidation in early 2025 (post:1886572123256402179), and the October 10 eight-figure perps wipeout (post:1977047126015459602). He says the biggest error after that was overtrading instead of stepping away (post:2114047078116782093). He admits Solana Neiro thesis invalidated (post:1824535673740956009). He says he regretted not sizing in more aggressively at lows because he waited for a bigger dip (post:1996693006913564674; post:1997964238875234508). He admitted round-tripping eight figures chasing social clout and said loyalty should be to P&L, not bags or communities (post:2084617347621351700). He admitted a rug loss came from borrowed conviction, not his own thesis (post:2108216523907006964). He also said he was wrong that the 4-year cycle was dead (post:2072782315093205259).
