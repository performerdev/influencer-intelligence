# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview

He believes the most important truth about Crypto Twitter is that **CT is not retail**. The timeline is biased toward its own bags, misses the real flows, and is usually wrong at extremes. He said it early: “CT is NOT retail” (post:1763656001532637440), and he has never stopped. That belief drives his entire contrarian method: if CT is screaming bearish, he is bidding. If CT is euphoric, he is suspicious. He calls CT a “mirror of the market, not a source of alpha” and says “consensus trades rarely win” (post:2104558745292300337). The corollary is that retail investors — the actual buyers on Coinbase, Robinhood, Upbit — chase the biggest, most familiar memes, not what CT thinks is cool. That is why he keeps hammering unit bias, DINO coins, and exchange listings: those are where the real money enters.

He believes **memecoins are the main event of this cycle**. From the very first batch he was saying “memecoins are the leading narrative of this cycle and will keep outperforming” (post:1763560840458813831). By 2025 he was calling it a “memecoin supercycle” and insisting “$10b is the new $1b for memecoins” (post:1862914241260359783). He does not think this is froth to be dismissed; he thinks degeneracy is the point. He said retail will “fuel further degeneracy in memes, not utility alts” (post:1798735836265263417). Later, after watching utility tokens collapse, he doubled down: the “institutions only want revenue-generating protocols” narrative collapses on data because the top revenue protocol tokens were all down 33–70% (post:2003787973045756085). The honest framing became his favorite: **99% of crypto is useless, and USELESS is the one honest expression of that reality** (post:1933530942158623219, post:1940179531194904655).

His macro view is perma-bullish but not blindly so. He uses ETF flows, rate cuts, stimulus, election outcomes, and stock/gold strength as the actual bull/bear gauge rather than short-term TA (post:1768525475922477271, post:1770031107284586531). He believes crypto lags other risk assets and has a lot of catching up to do; the true run starts when that gap closes (post:1812850383213007109, post:1978167073617617281). He called the Trump win “giga bullish” and said it opens the floodgates for listings and institutional flows (post:1850588342875746771). After the Oct 10 liquidation event, he developed a specific view: it was caused by system failures on major CEXs and likely left a major entity as a forced seller with zero transparency (post:1990124918231781627, post:1991796853277360387). That view made him even more anti-perps and pro-spot.

A major change over time: he initially believed the 4-year cycle was dead and that crypto would rip to new ATHs regardless (post:1997041392938352999). Later he admitted he was wrong about that and said **“I ended up being wrong”**; he now expects the real move in Q4 (post:2087270834557595890). That is a rare full reversal, and he has not hidden it.

## How they reason and decide

His core reasoning loop is: find a narrative that is hated, ignored, or misunderstood; verify it with on-chain data and market-cap math; then size in with conviction and hold through the inevitable drawdown.

He is obsessed with **historical analogs**. He studies the 2021 SHIB rally, the DOGE playbook, PEPE 2023, BONK’s $200m→$20m→$4b arc, and FLOKI’s $3.5b→$40m→$3.5b round trip (post:1804235987666768209, post:2042285080396054871, post:2053854383230586968). He uses those to project current setups: “if this is like SHIB 2021, then X is priced wrong.” That is why he kept telling people to “study the 2021 memecoin cycle” (post:1833111132619317732).

He compares market caps and volume across comparable assets. For CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, he argued the cat sector was tiny relative to dogs (post:1836530239754633613, post:1842272422126387212). For PONS vs PUMP he ran revenue-multiple math: PONS at ~0.66x annual revenue vs PUMP at ~4.35x, so PONS must reprice (post:2091618376137187518). He calls this “fundamentals do not lie” (post:1945092324540788884). He also uses OI-to-market-cap and volume-to-market-cap as leading indicators of parabolic moves (post:2066298266120077776).

On-chain metrics are non-negotiable. He tracks holder growth, whale inflows, smart-money netflows, volume, and distribution. The single most important signal for him is **holder growth during sell-offs**: “if a lot of new buyers and holders keep stepping in every time there’s a sell-off, it’s almost always a smart idea to buy the dip” (post:1950178237151105299). He watches Nansen netflows and Dexscreener volume. He treats whale accumulation during dips as confirmation (post:1946495333195780455, post:1950435042372591726).

Sentiment is a contrarian instrument. He explicitly says to **buy when it feels scary and sell when it feels retarded** (post:1936108815403073948). He fades “cycle top” calls and doomposting as bottom signals, citing Fear & Greed extremes and Saylor FUD as opportunities (post:1986004042347130969, post:2063660234116104639). He says the most hated rallies pump hardest because sidelined capital becomes the fuel (post:1923771042989842669, post:1934343748357648524).

Relative strength is a core filter. He watches what holds up during BTC/ETH bloodbaths and buys the strongest horse. During the 2025 dips he pointed to USELESS and BONK outperforming, saying “relative strength vs every major memecoin” is proof of future leadership (post:1805361314732425575, post:1998428982102888542, post:2061431245226041374). He often asks: “what else do y’all need to see?” after showing a chart of higher lows.

His decision process is thesis-first, not price-first. He said: “i always post a thesis whenever i mention a coin” (post:1856825951239221746). He rejects copytrading and tells followers to form their own conviction (post:1944522334947410272, post:1950117974016180602). He builds a view from narrative, community, accessibility, volume, and only then sizes in. He says if you don’t have your own thesis, you will panic sell because it was borrowed conviction (post:2108216523907006964).

## Risk attitude

He is genuinely comfortable with losing everything on a memecoin. He said: “for every single memecoin i buy, i buy with the expectation that i might lose 100% of my investment” (post:1845063147171807694). He conditions himself so no crypto loss fazes him and treats the numbers as imaginary (post:1776021430884315193). That is not just posturing: he has posted $10m+ drawdowns, 8-figure round trips, and 80% dips on BONK without selling (post:1796102767066403127, post:1876674463648940411).

His risk management is asymmetric: he accepts 60–90% dips as the price of 100x, and says “if you can’t stomach the dips you don’t deserve the gains” (post:1775914030638477650, post:1776026551219368281). He says a few huge wins dwarf dozens of losses, so he does not need a high win rate. He said: “my approach is based on making sure the size of my wins greatly overwhelms that of my losses” (post:1932235802576085328).

The biggest evolution: **leverage**. Early on he used it heavily and publicly paid massive funding on BONK perps (post:1820184762293567989). He warned people to tone down leverage (post:1763741393799786847). But after the Oct 10, 2025 liquidation wipeout — where he lost all perps positions, peak worth $30M+ — he turned decisively anti-perps. He said he now has zero perps exposure on core positions so he can stomach any volatility (post:2095391343996539096). He tells followers: “you don’t need more leverage you need more time in the market” (post:2106108708069445861). He now says spot outperforms leverage because perps enrich exchanges and allow manipulation (post:2082807481210753042).

He publicly shares drawdowns to balance the narrative. He posted being down $3.6M in 24h, down ~$9M from peak, and still said he was unfazed (post:2096885768014733719, post:2097248578880098436). He frames volatility as the “gift” and says unrealized losses are not real until sold (post:2109723023502930302). He is adamant that the only job is to survive: “you have only one job here: SURVIVE that’s it don’t get liquidated. don’t panic-close high-conviction trades” (post:1909117312709390574).

## Favorite narratives (stance, and how it changed)

**Memecoin supercycle — bullish, unchanged.** From the start he believed memecoins are the leading sector (post:1763560840458813831). This never changed, even when memecoin dominance hit all-time lows in late 2025. He said that was the “perfect setup for a sector-wide melt-up” and cited prior sentiment lows preceding 440–2,500% rallies (post:2003453355692081220, post:2007146306611904556).

**BONK — bullish, held through everything, but no longer the top pick.** He was early BONK maxi: “the BONK revolution will be televised” (post:1861427399021207833). He held from $20m to $4b and through multiple 80% drawdowns (post:1859043765098623327). BONK remains a core bag and he has called it a deca-billion play (post:1940501332240355726). But by mid-2025, USELESS had clearly become his central thesis, and BONK was more of a large-cap pillar than the “runner.”

**FLOKI — bullish beta play.** He consistently played FLOKI as a levered beta to DOGE, BNB, and PEPE: “if you see $doge pumping hard, long floki” (post:1814697389820649973). He kept that stance through 2025 and into 2026, though it rarely took center stage.

**USELESS — from dark-horse conviction to the cycle’s defining runner.** This is the biggest narrative shift. In mid-2025 he started deep-diving USELESS as the honest “useless” meme (post:1923498440073330892). He then made it his primary bag, called it “the DOGE/SHIB-type play of this cycle” (post:2008417398446014817), and said it will be the first memecoin to hit $1B+ this cycle (post:2095145010832257102). He was relentless: comparing its chart to PEPE 2023, quoting volume/OI data, and evangelizing in English, Korean, and Chinese. Even after it was down 92%, he said conviction was unshaken (post:2019785568792526961). This replaced BONK as his flagship bet.

**Robinhood Chain — from skepticism to “THE chain of this cycle.”** By mid-2026 he was arguing Robinhood Chain would lead the next explosive onchain rally because Robinhood has massive retail distribution and listing flywheel (post:2075525662920642599, post:2093412994810077221). He called the 70–90% sell-off an accumulation window (post:2109871757175996430). This is new and forceful.

**PONS / Pons launchpad — bullish after data proved the moat, especially post-Uniswap entry.** He compared PONS revenue multiples to PUMP, said it’s undervalued (post:2091551249485238638), and argued competitor entry validated the moat (post:2087152884026679792). This became a core thesis in late 2026.

**Other recurring bullish narratives:** Cat memecoins (CAT, LMEOW, BONGO) — early strong bullish, later absorbed into the USELESS mega-thesis; Political/Trump memecoins — bullish around the 2024 election, later mostly dropped after round-tripping DOGEGOV/MAGA/GME meta; BTC memecoins/Runes — early “easiest 50-100x” call, later faded as attention moved back to centralized memes; AI memecoins — bullish as a narrative but not a consistent position; privacy coins (Nullmask MASK) — a very late opportunistic call in Oct 2026 (post:2111110757735600482).

That is why he now hammers “form your own thesis.”
