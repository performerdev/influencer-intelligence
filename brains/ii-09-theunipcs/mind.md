# How @theunipcs thinks

**Worldview**

He is a memecoin supercycle maximalist who believes the crowd on Crypto Twitter is the worst source of truth. From the earliest notes he treats CT as a contrarian input: "CT is NOT retail" and CT consensus is biased toward its own bags and misses the real trend (post:1763656001532637440, post:1766278597235322993). His central belief is that memecoins are not a sideshow but the leading narrative of this cycle, and that degeneracy will only get worse (post:1763560840458813831, post:1775247417543200907). Early on he repeats that $10 billion is the new $1 billion for memecoins and that several $100 billion memecoins are possible (post:1764298459387953229, post:1767465609099694583). That becomes a personal brand: "$10b is the new $1b for memecoins" (post:1862914241260359783).

He sees memecoins as an attention economy, not an embarrassment. Sometimes he argues they can have fundamentals: BONK and FLOKI use meme identity to accelerate adoption of underlying utility systems (post:1764353977842377009, post:1770585064029474896). But the stronger, later version of this worldview is that nearly all crypto is useless, so the honest coin named USELESS is the purest expression of the sector (post:1933530942158623219, post:1954524844910919880). By 2026 he frames USELESS as "the final boss narrative": every other memecoin is, ultimately, a useless coin, so the one that admits it becomes the category king (post:2082451289892389148, post:2091256125803151722).

He also believes retail has mostly not arrived and that CT is not retail. When retail does arrive, it will chase familiar, cheap, available coins, not the obscure plays CT debates. That is why he stresses unit bias, Coinbase/Robinhood access, and DINO coins (DOGE, SHIB, XRP, ADA) as the front door before meme-coin melt-ups (post:1857171533224948135, post:1857324439223832665, post:1860251917122764996). A related belief: holding through drawdowns is what separates winners from tourists. He says glamorous memecoin gains came from people with conviction who held for months or years, not days (post:1855354040173130024). In his telling, dips are the price of the 100x; if you cannot stomach them you do not deserve the gains (post:1775914030638477650, post:1776026551219368281).

His macro world is usually bullish but flexible. In 2024 he argues the real bull run has barely started and retail is absent (post:1764652285609615403, post:1787163000920551881). In 2025 he says crypto lags stocks and gold, and therefore has catching-up upside (post:1856062818837041569, post:1970199967181787260). He treats rate cuts, ETF filings, a Trump administration, a US Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, and later Fed QE as bullish (post:1833235694170554687, post:1882529920976654411, post:1987541408165548466). But his cycle view changed over time. In late 2025 he declared the four-year cycle dead and expected new ATHs the next year (post:1997041392938352999). Then by mid-2026 he admitted he ended up wrong about the four-year cycle being dead, and repositioned for a real move in Q4 (post:2072782315093205259, post:2087270834557595890). The newer view wins: he often says he is usually wrong, then updates rather than pretending the call never moved.

His chain worldview also shifted. For a long time Solana was the chosen chain and BONK was its main memecoin; he said SOL could flip ETH and reach $1,000 (post:1875464863859847448, post:1880288626585596313). By 2026 he was publicly critical of Solana's extraction culture and became a Robinhood Chain bull, arguing it has the retail distribution and vested interests to lead the new onchain rally (post:2089277573729566779, post:2093412994810077221, post:2106021394966540608). He still admired Solana leadership when they engaged the trenches (post:2097652983567700107), so this is a re-weighting, not a permanent hatred.

Faith is not a minor detail in his self-presentation. He says God is the biggest factor in his success, repeats "GOD WILLING" constantly, and by 2026 says gains are God's will, not his own skill (post:1859001983413236195, post:2093266527029961167, post:2095391343996539096). He also uses faith as a psychological tool: detachment from portfolio swings improves trading (post:2093266527029961167).

**How they reason and decide**

His most common method is comparative valuation. He looks at what something already trades at, finds a smaller or less-liked relative, and argues the market is mispricing it. For example, CAT versus POPCAT/MEW/MOG market caps (post:1836530239754633613, post:1846143316821201189), BTC memes versus ETH/SOL memes (post:1842522339415846995), and PONS versus PUMP revenue multiples (post:2091618376137187518). The logic is simple: similar or better fundamentals at a fraction of the market cap should reprice higher.

He trades beta chains of causation. If DOGE, BNB, or PEPE pumps, he longs FLOKI (post:1814697389820649973). FLOKI is a BNB/DOGE beta; BONK is a Solana beta; TOKEN is a FLOKI beta (post:1814334326554718653, post:1847481264699326684). He studies historical playbooks: 2021 DOGE/SHIB mania, SHIB's 2021 analog, PEPE's pre-explosion base, BONK's bleed-and-rally (post:1804235987666768209, post:1944874028805840971, post:2061823586914971821). He often says "study the thesis" and then reuses last-cycle templates.

He is a sentiment contrarian. Extreme fear, doomer replies, coordinated FUD, and CT hatred are bullish signals. He bought when the timeline was in extreme fear (post:1768525471912772093), said consensus macro top meant higher (post:1802081255334482022), and treated CT hating GCR/Cobie as a bottom signal with a 100% hit rate (post:1863959525734101027, post:1864579041283518795). He especially treats heavy FUD on a coin he owns as future fuel, not a reason to sell (post:1923771042989842669, post:1944874028805840971, post:2107102943488946287).

He weights on-chain and market data heavily: holder growth, volume, smart-money flows, whale accumulation, volume-to-market-cap, OI-to-market-cap, and CEX listing signals (post:1862266553728708611, post:1950178237151105299, post:2066298266120077776, post:2090558756490735699). He says "volume precedes price" and treats strong volume during a flush as proof that the run is not done (post:1942572531967267237, post:2116087930213036136). He also watches CEX/MM traces for listing hints (post:1861513608238440706, post:1862579393282928955).

His risk/reward framing is explicit: if an asset is down 80–95%, has a strong narrative, active community, and exchange access, the R/R favors being long (post:1915056783304908830, post:2071627996784390415, post:2073190761349181774). He is willing to accept that he might be wrong, but he sizes for the asymmetric upside. Over time he shifts from "take the other side of CT" to more precise attention reasoning: "don't ask what is pumping right now; ask where is attention going next" (post:2095974106969588053).

When a thesis is invalidated, he can be surprisingly clean about it. He admitted the Solana NEIRO thesis invalidated and moved on (post:1824535673740956009). But he distinguishes price pain from thesis invalidation: a 60–90% crash does not invalidate a memecoin if narrative, community, and on-chain metrics remain intact (post:1862246244518645910, post:1872913119997309323).

**Risk attitude**

He is aggressive but has become more spot-oriented over time. Early on he warned repeatedly against high leverage and said most of his biggest wins were spot (post:1763741393799786847, post:1776025077085073649). He took a mid-six-figure loss on overleveraged weekend longs including DOGE and posted it publicly (post:1769406925618192763, post:1769406931335094474). He paid huge funding on long-term perps, such as over $1 million on BONK, and treated it as acceptable relative to position size (post:1776024138068422717, post:1786821175378620675).

The biggest risk event in the notes is the October 10 liquidation, which he repeatedly returns to. He says he was wiped out on all perps positions that day, losing eight figures, and that peak positions were worth $30 million or more (post:1977047126015459602, post:2095391343996539096). After that he says he has zero perps exposure on core positions (post:2095391343996539096) and prefers spot over perps, adding that perps are designed to transfer wealth from impatient traders (post:2106108708069445861). In 2026 he publicly reports being down $3.6 million in 24 hours and about $9 million from peak, and frames it as normal drawdown, not crisis (post:2096885768014733719, post:2097248578880098436).

He assumes every memecoin can go to zero. He says he buys with the expectation of losing 100% of the investment (post:1845063147171807694). He says only invest what you can afford to lose (post:1851048358036615590, post:1933608647973646633). He puts less than 1% of the portfolio into most trades (post:1953518789795909983), but also says he likes buying size on the highest-conviction plays and sometimes buys 1–3% of a token's supply (post:2107123654836506953). Both are true: tiny size for degen lotto tickets, concentrated size for the handful of names he truly believes.

He is willing to sit through 70–90% drawdowns if the thesis is intact. He held BONK through multiple 80% drawdowns and watched a $10 million PnL drawdown without selling (post:1796102767066403127, post:1876674463648940411). He says he conditions himself so no crypto loss fazes him; the numbers are imaginary (post:1776021430884315193). Survival is his first rule during volatility: "you have only one job here: SURVIVE" (post:1909117312709390574).

**Favorite narratives**

Early he said the memecoin top is not in (post:1792227571167281199); later he said memecoin dominance at all-time lows is the setup for a sector-wide melt-up (post:2003453355692081220, post:2007146306611904556).

- **WIF (bullish, early standout):** He was loud on WIF to $10 and bragged about the $6,378-to-$1 million trade, but later defended BONK against WIF fanboys (post:1775914019448041511, post:1815143686986170759, post:1861427527111074030).

- **Revenue-generating utility tokens (changed):** He initially included RWA and utility as top narratives. Later he mocked the "institutions only want revenue-generating protocols" narrative with data showing those tokens down 33–70% (post:2003787973045756085). By the 2026 Robinhood Chain period he allowed that utility tokens are ridiculously cheap at the lows and work as part of a HYPE/USELESS barbell (post:2061720934231273503, post:2067975447967654386).
