# How @theunipcs thinks

## Worldview

His fixed idea from the start: memecoins are the dominant trade of this cycle, and $10 billion became the new $1 billion for memecoin leaders (1764298459387953229, 1862914241260359783). He repeats that CT is not retail and that fading CT consensus is often the profitable side; CT is biased toward its own bags and misses what retail will actually chase (1763656001532637440, 1857324439223832665, 2095974106969588053). Only three sectors ever matter to him, in order: memecoins, RWA, AI (1802085902778343486, 1812962237554041212, 1915733774945362124). Even with that, he insists he is not a single-coin maxi and prefers holding the best winners across a narrative (1768035919020401056).

He believes most of crypto is useless, and this became a defining narrative: the most honest memecoin is the one named USELESS, because every memecoin is ultimately a useless coin (1933530942158623219, 1954524844910919880, 1997041392938352999). Every major memecoin, he argues, goes through a 90–99% pullback after its first rally before the real parabolic move to billions; DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, WIF, BONK and FLOKI all did this (2003107147186360636, 2019785568792526961, 2024880406605033616). That belief lets him hold through drawdowns that others call death.

His view of the cycle changed over time. In 2024 he said the bull run had barely started and retail was absent (1764652285609615403, 1767120074840629438). In 2025 he still said the 4-year cycle was dead and new ATHs were coming (1997041392938352999). Then in 2026 he admitted he ended up wrong about the 4-year cycle being dead and reset his timing toward Q4 (2072782315093205259). Similarly, he moved from Solana as the won chain to Robinhood Chain as THE chain of 2026, while still crediting Solana leadership for finally engaging the trenches (1880288626585596313, 2093412994810077221, 2097652983567700107). He also criticized Solana's extraction culture as the problem, not the tech (2089277573729566779).

God and faith are central to his stated worldview. He says gains are God's will, not his own skill, and that faith gives him detachment from drawdowns (1859001983413236195, 2093266527029961167, 2094161494850273476, 2095391343996539096).

## How they reason and decide

He is a sentiment contrarian first. Extreme fear on the timeline tells him it is time to bid (1768525471912772093, 1767994756083105881). He treats CT doomposting, cycle-top consensus, Saylor FUD and widespread hate as bottom signals (1802081255334482022, 2063660234116104639, 1964361622291648964). The louder CT is about a topic, the harder he wants to countertrade it (1944672596366082227).

He studies historical analogs: 2021 SHIB/Doge mania, 2023 PEPE, early BONK, SAFEMOON, and prior memecoin cycles (1804235987666768209, 1944874028805840971, 2095416964852158565). His three-part memecoin framework is narrative strength, community strength, and liquidity/access (1818407937133408352, 1953798167931027690). He argues holder growth during sell-offs is the single most important signal, and smart-money/whale accumulation confirms the dip (1950178237151105299, 1950219400495116470, 1946495333195780455).

He uses market-cap comparisons to call coins undervalued: CAT vs POPCAT/MEW/MOG, BTC memecoins vs ETH/SOL memecoins, USELESS vs billion-dollar peers, PONS vs PUMP (1846143316821201189, 1935194245222002689, 2091618376137187518). He tracks volume-to-market-cap and OI-to-market-cap as leading indicators, and says volume precedes price (1942572531967267237, 1973785196353769689, 2066298266120077776). Relative strength against BTC/SOL during selloffs tells him which coin is the fastest horse (1805361314732425575, 2113537808472199595). He also reasons through beta relationships, such as FLOKI as a leveraged beta to DOGE/BNB/PEPE (1814697389820649973).

Catalysts drive timing: CEX listings, ETF/ETP filings, airdrops, elections, buybacks, and mainstream mentions (1828530428271313032, 1846514933216731535, 1881721798548492503, 2026648229589250116). He forms his own thesis rather than trading off someone else's list (1921214601888227748), and when a thesis is invalidated he often admits it publicly and moves on (1824535673740956009). He frames decisions in risk/reward terms and says the smartest move is often to trade the market instead of your bias (1993988966035366382).

## Risk attitude

Early on he was explicit about losing money and conditioning himself so no crypto loss fazed him; the numbers were imaginary (1769406925618192763, 1776021430884315193). He accepted 60% dips as the price of 100x, and held BONK through multiple 80% drawdowns and public PnL drops above $10 million (1775914030638477650, 1796102767066403127, 1855354040173130024). He warned against high leverage repeatedly even while using it, and said most of his biggest wins were spot (1763741393799786847, 1776025077085073649).

His risk stance hardened after the 10/10 liquidation event, when he was wiped out on all perps positions and lost eight figures (1977047126015459602, 2095391343996539096). After that he repeatedly said avoid leverage, buy spot and chill, and that he now holds zero perps on core positions so he can stomach volatility (1977642819897458755, 1985303490999030123, 2007857212601884828, 2095391343996539096). He also came to prefer spot over perps to avoid enriching exchanges and being manipulated (2082807481210753042). He says he uses isolated margin, avoids cross, and puts less than 1% of his portfolio into most trades (1953518789795909983). He assumes any memecoin can go to zero and says only invest what you can afford to lose (1845063147171807694, 1933608647973646633).

He is unusually transparent about drawdowns, publicly reporting being down $3.6M in 24 hours, down $9M from peak, and a portfolio drawdown from $28.5M to $8.9M without selling core bags (2096885768014733719, 2097248578880098436, 2114750389987774648). His one job, he says, is to survive and not get liquidated (1909117312709390574). After round-tripping eight figures, he learned to take profits routinely and be loyal to P&L, not bags (2084617347621351700, 1953553490845806702).
