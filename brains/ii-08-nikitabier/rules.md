## Entry
- Target an embarrassingly small audience first. “My rule of thumb is that the target audience should be embarrassingly small, so much so that you’re uncomfortable telling your friends and family what you’re working on.” (post:1255291891647688705)
- On days when your entire feed is euphoric Robinhood screenshots, buy VXX and TLT calls. “On days when your entire Twitter feed is euphoric screenshots of Robinhood accounts ↓ Buy $VXX and $TLT calls.” (post:1282875700416876544)
- Buy growth names trading under 10x sales when the market is broken. “Just did myself. Trading at 6x sales. Market is broken.” (post:1390335338774556678)
- Buy the dip when the selloff looks stupid relative to the numbers. “I doubled down on the 2nd dip after the earnings call. The sell off seemed stupid based on the numbers.” (post:1358170845038968832)
- Ignore the excitement around a space; find the best companies in a domain. “My heuristic going forward is to ignore the excitement around a space and instead find the best companies in a respective domain.” (post:1344396534398402562)
- Invest in founders who were running scams or hustles at 13, not the extracurricular kids. “My favorite Q to assess a founder is: what were you doing when you were 13? ... I never hesitate to invest in B.” (post:1344779790679564288)
- If a startup says they’re targeting Gen Z, pass. “It might be a bad heuristic but if a startup says they’re targeting Gen Z, I pass.” (post:1419860230205153285)
- Seed with a small group, see if it’s working, and grow; never “launch.” “Never “launch”. Seed with a small group, see if it’s working, and then grow.” (post:1672764498858373122)
- Be greedy when others are fearful. “Be greedy when others are fearful” (post:1869596750711030085)
- Pay whatever it takes to reach the first users; density matters more than CAC. “you should pay whatever it takes to reach that initial cluser—even $20 an install” (post:1844050353387348081)

## Exit and taking profit
- Take life-changing money at seed stage rather than risk losing it all chasing a bigger outcome. “I’ll take the life-changing money, instead of potentially losing it all in an attempt to enrich VCs.” (post:1386073453669748737)
- Sell secondary at seed stage when the opportunity arises. “it’s still usually highest EV to sell at seed stage if the opportunity arises.” (post:1386076327036346370)
- If you need a pros/cons list, the answer is no. “If you ever need a pros and cons list to make a decision, the answer is no.” (post:1839543958717841648)
- If an investor says they’ll invest if you find a lead investor, that’s a no and you should move on. “If an investor ever tells you they’ll invest if you find a “lead investor”: that’s a no and you should move on.” (post:1870642819481706611)
- If an opportunity to exit presents itself where you’d make enough to own a home, take it. “If an opportunity to exit presents itself where you would make enough to own a home, take it—even if it’s earlier than the expectations you set.” (post:1744428250976371044)
- Never sell anything if you live in California; sell calls instead of shares. “Never sell anything if you live in Taxifornia.” (post:1418634569352290305)

## Risk management
- Cut runway in half to force efficiency. “Cut a startup’s runway in half and watch magic happen” (post:1207422894944346112)
- Avoid lifestyle changes based on investment returns above 8%; they are borrowed from future years. “Any investment returns in excess of 8% are borrowed from future years. Consider that before making any lifestyle changes.” (post:1361437339214487556)
- Use interest-only loans and pledge stock portfolios as collateral to avoid down payments. “You can collateralize the down payment by pledging a stock portfolio ($0 down). And if you opt for interest-only, you can really juice your returns.” (post:1357549098694397952)
- Get a 10-year term so there’s a 3-year buffer if the market is down in year 7. “I got a 10y term so I’d have a 3 year buffer in case the market was down in Year 7 and I needed to wait it out” (post:1357551431687966723)
- Stick to Coinbase for crypto custody because they can sell shares to cover a catastrophic loss. “Stick to Coinbase. At least they can sell shares to cover a catastrophic loss.” (post:1443972042521395208)
- Go to cash when macro uncertainty spikes. “Went to 80% cash.” (post:1918678565840638354)
- Include a “why it won’t work” slide in every pitch. “For every pitch after that, I included a “why it won’t work” slide and investors funded us without hesitation.” (post:1393275250230530048)
- Draft a murderboard before launching. “Before launching a product, sit down and draft a murderboard: list out every possible criticism with the worst headline possible.” (post:1744091716423176433)
- Don’t raise VC for paywall-ad arbitrage apps. “the absolute last thing you should is raise outside funding for Type B” (post:1849291216166289772)
- Only invest in friends; everything else is a scam. “the only time I’ve made money in startups is when I invest in friends. Everything else is a scam.” (post:1814732228536009085)
- Don’t buy equity without pricing in a possible 50% valuation discount. “go ahead and pencil in a 50% discount and see if you'd still do it” (post:1819488281450692985)
