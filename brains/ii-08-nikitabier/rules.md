# @nikitabier's rules

## Entry
- Target an embarrassingly small audience first. "My rule of thumb is that the target audience should be embarrassingly small, so much so that you’re uncomfortable telling your friends and family what you’re working on." (1255291891647688705) (stated once)
- Version 1.0 should be so verticalized that target users feel it was made for them and founders feel embarrassed telling friends and family. "when a target user lands on the page, their only reaction is \"Well, shit. This was made for me. I better sign up.\" ... when you tell your friends & family what you're working on, it is so narrowly focused that you feel embarrassed" (1889370882696683755) (stated once)
- Never "launch"; seed with a small group, see if it is working, then grow. "Never “launch”. Seed with a small group, see if it’s working, and then grow." (1672764498858373122) (stated once)
- Remove "launch" from your vocabulary and replace it with "test." "You should remove the word “launch” from your vocabulary and replace it with “test”." (1800197768981762396) (stated once)
- If a social app has no hourly recurring sessions among some cohort, throw everything out. "If you’re building a social app and you’re not seeing recurring sessions on an hourly basis each day among some cohort of users, don’t waste another minute: throw everything out and go back to the drawing board." (1610710405411926016) (stated once)
- Test quietly in the American South and Midwest before coastal audiences. "Every app I’ve built I’ve quietly tested in the American South and Midwest for many months." (1660998272612220928) (stated once)
- Geofence California and New York out and launch there last. "I always geofence California and New York out of the app and launch there last. Not worth the distraction when you’re scaling up." (1729488294314917988) (stated once)
- Pay whatever it takes to reach the first cluster; density matters more than CAC. "you should pay whatever it takes to reach that initial cluser—even $20 an install" (1844050353387348081) (stated once)
- Don't build sharing flows until users hack it themselves with screenshots or demand already exists. "If you can, don’t build any sharing flows until (1) you see users hacking it themselves with screenshots or (2) demand to share that way is already demonstrated in an existing in-market app." (1607271350984658945) (stated once)
- Use a single authentication method aligned with distribution and friending. "you should only allow one method—and have that method be on the same “protocol” as your distribution & friending system" (1662100378500866049) (stated once)
- Never build an app to "meetup with friends." "Never build an app to \"meetup with friends.\"" (1481118418883399686) (stated once)
- If a startup says they are targeting Gen Z, pass. "It might be a bad heuristic but if a startup says they’re targeting Gen Z, I pass." (1419860230205153285) (stated once)
- Buy a small amount of shares of products or services that blow you away. "I usually buy a small amount of shares of products or services that blow me away—everything from Ubiquiti routers to household things like Dymonic Caulk to retail experiences like TJ Maxx." (1869071297126482350) (stated once)
- Ignore excitement around a space; find the best companies in a domain. "My heuristic going forward is to ignore the excitement around a space and instead find the best companies in a respective domain." (1344396534398402562) (stated once)
- Invest in founders who were running scams or hustles at 13, not the extracurricular kids. "My favorite Q to assess a founder is: what were you doing when you were 13? ... I never hesitate to invest in B." (1344779790679564288) (stated once)
- Be greedy when others are fearful. "Be greedy when others are fearful" (1869596750711030085) (stated once)
- Buy growth names trading under 10x sales when the market is broken. "Just did myself. Trading at 6x sales. Market is broken." (1390335338774556678) (stated once)
- Set terms on your own round, raise from many people, and take money immediately on a convertible note. "just set the terms on your own round and raise from many people. When anyone indicates interest, just immediately take their money on a convertible note." (1870642819481706611) (stated once)
- On days when the feed is euphoric Robinhood screenshots, buy VXX and TLT calls. "On days when your entire Twitter feed is euphoric screenshots of Robinhood accounts ↓ Buy $VXX and $TLT calls." (1282875700416876544) (stated once)

## Exit and taking profit
- Pivot until product-market fit is achieved. "When you don’t have product-market fit, keep pivoting until you do" (1119076765207371777) (stated once)
- Take life-changing money at seed stage rather than risk losing it all chasing a bigger outcome. "I’ll take the life-changing money, instead of potentially losing it all in an attempt to enrich VCs." (1386073453669748737) (stated once)
- Sell secondary at seed stage when the opportunity arises; it is usually highest EV. "it’s still usually highest EV to sell at seed stage if the opportunity arises." (1386076327036346370) (stated once)
- If an exit opportunity would let you own a home, take it even if earlier than expectations. "If an opportunity to exit presents itself where you would make enough to own a home, take it—even if it’s earlier than the expectations you set." (1744428250976371044) (stated once)
- Never sell anything if you live in Taxifornia; sell calls instead. "Never sell anything if you live in Taxifornia." (1418634569352290305) (stated once)
- If you need a pros and cons list, the answer is no. "If you ever need a pros and cons list to make a decision, the answer is no." (1839543958717841648) (stated once)
- If an investor says they'll invest if you find a lead investor, that's a no. "If an investor ever tells you they’ll invest if you find a “lead investor”: that’s a no and you should move on." (1870642819481706611) (stated once)
- Never accept performance-based earn-outs in an acquisition. "“We don’t do performance-based earn outs, because you don’t have control of the outcome.”" (2112020622062821693) (stated once)
