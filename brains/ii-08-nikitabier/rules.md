- Be greedy when others are fearful. "Be greedy when others are fearful" (post:1869596750711030085)

## Exit and taking profit
- Pivot until product-market fit is achieved. "When you don’t have product-market fit, keep pivoting until you do" (post:1119076765207371777)
- Take life-changing money at seed stage rather than chase VCs' bigger outcome. "I’ll take the life-changing money, instead of potentially losing it all in an attempt to enrich VCs." (post:1386073453669748737)
- Sell secondary at seed stage when the opportunity arises. "it’s still usually highest EV to sell at seed stage if the opportunity arises." (post:1386076327036346370)
- Never sell anything if you live in California; sell calls instead. "Never sell anything if you live in Taxifornia." (post:1418634569352290305, post:1418613233150169091)
- Take an exit that lets you own a home, even early. "If an opportunity to exit presents itself where you would make enough to own a home, take it—even if it’s earlier than the expectations you set." (post:1744428250976371044)
- If you need a pros/cons list, the answer is no. "If you ever need a pros and cons list to make a decision, the answer is no." (post:1839543958717841648)
- If an investor says they'll invest if you find a lead, that's a no. "If an investor ever tells you they’ll invest if you find a “lead investor”: that’s a no and you should move on." (post:1870642819481706611)

## Risk management
- Cut startup runway in half to force efficiency. "Cut a startup’s runway in half and watch magic happen" (post:1207422894944346112)
- Go to cash when macro uncertainty spikes. "Went to 80% cash." (post:1918678565840638354)
- Play for upside, not coupon payments. "We’re playing for upside, not coupon payments" (post:1910493830136164506)
- Use interest-only loans and pledge stock portfolios as collateral. "You can collateralize the down payment by pledging a stock portfolio ($0 down). And if you opt for interest-only, you can really juice your returns." (post:1357549098694397952)
- Get a 10-year term for a 3-year buffer. "I got a 10y term so I’d have a 3 year buffer in case the market was down in Year 7 and I needed to wait it out" (post:1357551431687966723)
- Avoid lifestyle changes based on returns above 8%. "Any investment returns in excess of 8% are borrowed from future years. Consider that before making any lifestyle changes." (post:1361437339214487556)
- Stick to Coinbase for crypto custody. "Stick to Coinbase. At least they can sell shares to cover a catastrophic loss." (post:1443972042521395208)
- Don't raise VC for paywall-ad arbitrage apps. "the absolute last thing you should is raise outside funding for Type B" (post:1849291216166289772)
- Only invest in friends; everything else is a scam. "the only time I’ve made money in startups is when I invest in friends. Everything else is a scam." (post:1814732228536009085)
- Assume all systems will break every few days and have redundancy plans. "We had to assume all systems would break every few days & have redundancy plans. And everything did: user support, 2FA, databases." (post:1637179063403008000)
- No crying in the casino. "No crying in the casino." (post:1899191398370508805)
