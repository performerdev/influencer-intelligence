# @nikitabier's rules

## Entry
- Buy VXX and TLT calls on days when Twitter is euphoric with Robinhood screenshots: “On days when your entire Twitter feed is euphoric screenshots of Robinhood accounts ↓ Buy $VXX and $TLT calls.” (post:1282875700416876544) (stated once)
- Test consumer apps quietly in the American South and Midwest first; coastal testing distorts data: “Every app I’ve built I’ve quietly tested in the American South and Midwest for many months. Testing on urban coastal audiences distorts your data and draws premature awareness to your experiment. If it works in Middle America, it will work everywhere.” (post:1660998272612220928)
- Don’t launch a real-friends social app Thursday to Sunday; you need sequential in-person days for word-of-mouth: “If you're launching a real-friends social app, be cautious releasing Thurs to Sunday—for 2 critical reasons: 1/ You need several sequential in-person days to get word-of-mouth going 2/ The quietest online days are Fri & Saturday One lost day can severely impair network formation.” (post:1591555991447175169) (stated once)
- Pay whatever it takes to reach the initial user cluster, even $20 an install: “you should pay whatever it takes to reach that initial cluser—even $20 an install.” (post:1844050353387348081) (stated once)
- If an investor says they’ll invest if you find a lead, move on: “If an investor ever tells you they’ll invest if you find a “lead investor”: that’s a no and you should move on.” (post:1870642819481706611) (stated once)
- Buy a small amount of shares in products or services that blow you away: “I usually buy a small amount of shares of products or services that blow me away” (post:1869071297126482350) (stated once)

## Exit and taking profit
- Take life-changing money instead of gambling for world domination: “I’ll take the life-changing money, instead of potentially losing it all in an attempt to enrich VCs.” (post:1386073453663748737)
- If an exit lets you own a home, take it even if earlier than expected: “If an opportunity to exit presents itself where you would make enough to own a home, take it—even if it’s earlier than the expectations you set.” (post:1744428250976371044) (stated once)
- Once an acquisition is in motion, fall in line: “Every investor who stood in the way of an acquisition has regretted it; once an acquisition is in motion, fall in line.” (post:1758975484031873056) (stated once)
- If you need a pros and cons list, the answer is no: “If you ever need a pros and cons list to make a decision, the answer is no.” (post:1839543958717841648) (stated once)

## Sizing
- Don’t give new hires less than 0.1% equity prelaunch: “If you’re a prelaunch company and you’re giving new hires less than 0.1% in equity, I don’t care if you’re goddamn Steve Jobs: you are architecting your company to fail.” (post:1298379141893382144) (stated once)
- Before buying equity, pencil in a 50% discount and see if you’d still do it: “go ahead and pencil in a 50% discount and see if you'd still do it.” (post:1819488281450692985) (stated once)

## Hold time
- Startups take 10 years; plan for a long hold: “Before you start a startup, remember: 1. It’s going to take 10 years 2. Money isn’t fun when you’re old” (post:1307698958341349376) (stated once)
- Stock market cycles keep getting faster; each successive one will be half the duration: “each successive one will always be half the duration of the previous one.” (post:1884060441149858289) (stated once)

## Risk management
- Don’t invest in founders active on Twitter: “The best way to lose all your money is invest in a founder who is active on Twitter.” (post:1351373529854275585) (stated once)
- If your product requires a partnership, run: “If your product requires a "partnership", run.” (post:1481118427129344009) (stated once)
- Draft a murderboard of worst headlines before launching, then reposition to be bulletproof: “Before launching a product, sit down and draft a murderboard: list out every possible criticism with the worst headline possible. Then, while preserving the core value proposition, reposition the product so it’s bulletproof against any criticism.” (post:1744091716423176433) (stated once)
- Set up redundancy for all commodity service providers: “When you’re launching your app, setup redundancy for all commodity service providers: 2 analytics providers, 2 sms providers, 2 of everything.” (post:1738720690772258841) (stated once)
- If a founder you invested in went to Art Basel, pencil in $0: “If a founder you invested in went to Art Basel, pencil in $0 right now.” (post:1735110553339588749) (stated once)
- If a company you invested in has a logo on the door before 1 million DAU, pencil in zero: “If a company you invested in has a logo on the door before 1 million DAU: Pencil in zero.” (post:1923222477817237796) (stated once)
- Most consumer businesses younger than 5 years can die overnight; mitigate risk once the product is resonating: “Most consumer businesses younger than 5 years can die overnight from a data breach, bad article, regulation, API shutdown, or key engineer leaving.” (post:1769882609591447897) (stated once)
