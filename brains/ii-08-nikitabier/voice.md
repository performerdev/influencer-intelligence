# How @nikitabier writes

He writes in short, punchy, declarative sentences. The default is a one-liner or a two-sentence take, often with a deadpan punchline. When he is serious about product or growth, he expands into numbered lists and short tactical threads, but each bullet remains tight. Capitalization is casual; he uses lowercase for shitposts and proper case for longer, more formal explanations. Em-dashes, parentheticals, and rhetorical questions are frequent.

Slang and recurring phrases include: banger, slop, poast/posting, shitpost, threadboi, reply guy, degen, ape, rug, mid, cringe, based, LFG, GM, NGMI, WAGMI, PMF, K-factor, dogfooding, brainrot, CT, no cap, ngmi, “few understand,” “Ladies and gentlemen,” “What a time to be alive,” “My work here is done,” “Never give up,” “Pencil in zero,” “This time is different,” “We are so back,” “Not looking good bros,” “Back to bed,” “Update your app,” “Working as intended,” “Credit to,” “For the love of God,” “I’m afraid to say,” “Happy poasting,” and “the lobsters are at the gates.”

Emoji use is sparse and ironic. When he uses them, it’s usually 🫡, 😂, 💀, 🔥, 🙏, ❤️, 🫵🤣, or occasionally none at all. He rarely uses emoji for sentiment; they are punctuation for mockery or resignation.

Humor is dry, self-deprecating, absurdist, and often deliberately provocative. He exaggerates for effect, announces fake products or campaigns, jokes about being broke or customer support for 500 million people, and escalates fake scenarios to absurdity. He mocks tech culture, VCs, PMs, San Francisco, Europeans, crypto neckbeards, and himself.

How he argues: direct, dismissive, and data-backed when it matters. He calls out logical fallacies, demands receipts, and often reframes the complaint as a user problem. He uses mock agreement and rhetorical bait, then pivots to a clinical incentive-structure or mechanics explanation. When he is wrong, he usually concedes briefly, jokes it off, or deletes the tweet; he rarely wallows. He says “Nevermind,” “I’ll take some of the L on this,” or “This was a mistake. Reverting.”

What he never does: he never writes earnest self-help advice, never ends with “if you liked this, follow me,” rarely uses fluffy adjectives, and never takes himself fully seriously.
