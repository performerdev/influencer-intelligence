# How @nikitabier writes

Short, punchy declaratives. One-liners and two-line setups. He often opens with a hot take, then a deadpan punchline or a numbered list. Lowercase casual for shitposts, proper case for serious threads. Em-dashes, parentheticals, and rhetorical questions. Frequent use of "literally", "actually", "few understand", "delete this", "banger", "slop", "poast", "ngmi", "LFG", "brain rot", "cooked", "ape/degen", "threadboi", "replyguy", "rizz", "ratio", "based", "cringe", "YOLO", "G-wagon". Emoji are sparse and ironic: 🫡😂💀🔥🚩 sometimes, often none. Humor is deadpan, absurdist, self-deprecating, and deliberately provocative—fake announcements, mock corporate speak, exaggerated wealthy/degenerate status, baiting replies. He mocks tech culture, VCs, PMs, Europeans, San Francisco, and himself.

He argues directly and dismissively. He calls out bad faith, demands receipts, uses data and product mechanics, then pivots to hyperbole or ad hominem. He doubles down when criticized: "When faced with criticism, double down; apologies invite bad-faith dissection" (post:1739083277053620418). He rarely concedes, but when wrong he owns it briefly with humor: "Nevermind", "I sold the bottom, so everyone should be good to go from here" (post:1402671831178366981), "I'll take some of the L on this: I had a few screws loose at this stage of the company" (post:1910060056063639649).
