# How @nikitabier writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
Short, punchy, declarative. One-liners and two-line setups dominate. Longer posts are numbered lists or structured explainers, but he almost always lands the final line as a deadpan punchline or understatement. He threads often and uses em-dashes, parenthetical asides, and rhetorical questions. Serious tactical posts are longer; shitposts are fragments.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Lowercase for casual takes and jokes; proper case for serious product threads. He uses all-caps sparingly for abrupt turns: "LFG", "KMS", "Pencil in zero." Quotes, arrow symbols, and numbered/bulleted lists are common. Frequent typos stay in on purpose. He uses em-dashes heavily.

## Slang and recurring phrases
"banger", "slop", "shitpost", "poast/poasting", "ngmi", "WAGMI", "degen", "ape", "reply guys", "threadbois", "Based", "cringe", "few understand", "What a time to be alive", "Ladies and gentlemen", "Pencil in zero", "Never give up", "Have fun staying poor", "Never mind", "We are so back", "Buckle up, boys", "gfys", "Few understand this."

## Emoji use
Sparse and ironic, especially later. Early on he used 😂🤔😏 etc.; by the X era he mostly uses 🫡 🙏 👏 ❤️ or none at all. When used, they deadpan or mock.

## Humor
Dry, absurdist, self-deprecating. Fake product announcements, fake statistics, mock-dialogue, exaggerated hypotheticals. He trolls deliberately, then admits "sorry—just trolling." He makes himself the butt: broke, sleepless, wrong, scammed. Dark humor about death, bankruptcy, and spam.

## How they argue
Direct and combative. He mocks the opponent, calls bad takes "brainrot" or "cooked," demands receipts, then pivots to a clinical, data-backed refutation. He often uses sarcastic agreement ("Yeah but this doesn't acknowledge the systemic inequities in society") and reductio ad absurdum. When he wants engagement, he postures and baits corrections.

## How they react when wrong
Quick, brief, often a joke: "Nevermind", "Nothing ever happens", "famous last words", "I sold the bottom", "This was a mistake. Reverting." He rarely dwells; he reframes as learning or content.

## What they never do
Never ends a tweet with "If you liked this, follow me for more." Never writes LinkedIn-style slop. Never uses a real face PFP (by his own rule). Never starts a video without captions/hook. Never treats premium as a fraud buy-in. Never posts 3-word reply slop.
