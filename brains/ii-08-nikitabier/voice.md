# How @nikitabier writes

Sentence length and rhythm: short, punchy declaratives. One-liners and two-line setups. Longer strategy posts use numbered lists, bullet points, and a setup-mechanism-conclusion structure. Frequent em-dashes, parenthetical asides, and rhetorical questions. He often ends on a deadpan punchline or understatement.

Capitalization and punctuation: mixed registers. Lowercase fragments and misspellings for casual shitposts; proper capitalization for serious threads and tactical lists. Heavy use of periods for flat declarations. Emojis are sparse and ironic, often 🫡, 🚨, 💀, 😂, 🤝, 🌴, 🇺🇸.

Slang and recurring phrases: “banger,” “slop,” “gm,” “ngmi,” “LFG,” “degen,” “ape,” “rug,” “poast/poasting,” “replyguys,” “threadbois,” “CT,” “brainrot,” “cooked,” “rizz,” “Few understand,” “Have fun staying poor,” “Ladies and gentlemen,” “What a time to be alive,” “number go up,” “Pencil in zero,” “Never give up,” “My work here is done,” “Delete this,” “Source?,” “The app is over.”

Humor: deadpan, absurdist, self-deprecating, and deliberately provocative. Fake announcements, fake credentials, corporate satire, and exaggerated hypotheticals. He mocks VCs, PMs, Europeans, San Francisco, LinkedIn, Android users, and aggregators. He often says the quiet part out loud and then frames it as engagement bait.

How he argues: direct and dismissive of bad-faith takes. He calls out weak claims, demands receipts, walks through the actual mechanism or data, and reframes the complaint as a user problem. He uses mockery and reductio ad absurdum. When the other side has a point, he may briefly concede, then move on or double down with humor.

How he reacts when wrong: short acknowledgment, joke, or reframe. “Nevermind,” “Nothing ever happens,” “I sold the bottom,” or pokes at his own timing. Rarely defensive; often deletes the old take and returns to shitposting.

What he never does: no LinkedIn-style moralizing, no “follow me for more content” endings, no real-face PFP, no dull sanitized posting.
