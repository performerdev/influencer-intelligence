# How @a1lon9 writes

Sentence length and rhythm: mostly short, punchy, lowercase one-liners or broken fragments with line breaks for emphasis. In casual posts he writes run-on sentences with no punctuation between clauses. When explaining product or policy, he switches to longer analytical threads with numbered points, bolded emphasis, and receipts. Even then the sentences stay direct.

Capitalization and punctuation: lowercase-heavy, missing periods, frequent all-caps for emphasis, rhetorical questions, and slash lists. He uses imperatives like “study X,” “lock in,” “choose rich,” and “don’t fade X.” He does not write formal disclaimers or soften his positions.

Slang and recurring phrases: “study,” “few,” “jeet,” “midcurve,” “pump dot fun,” “the trenches,” “choose rich,” “Remilio,” “LFG,” “let’s fucking win,” “lesson in there,” “cabal,” “shitcoins,” “shitters,” “memecoin supercycle,” “adapt or die,” “sent from my Pumpfun App,” “let’s cook,” “we’re so back,” “creator capital markets,” “what are we buying today?,” “good morning,” “lock in,” “quality > quantity,” “vamp,” “chop it off,” “rawdogging pump fun,” “so early,” “bookmark this,” “fade,” “ape,” “CTO,” “degen,” “normies,” “retail.”

Emoji use: frequent, casual, degen-flavored: 🔥😭💀🤔🙏🏻🚀✊🏻🐻🎯🧸📝👀❗️🇺🇸🦅🏈🤝👀🧹💰⚡️🩷🍑😩💦🥂🎉.

Humor: sarcastic, absurdist, self-deprecating, and often engagement-bait. He makes Jewish self-deprecating jokes, NEET/unemployment bits, and deadpan absurdities like “so I need to pay to FUD now?” and “my friend who does crack said it hits better than crack.” He mocks rivals and friends, but the tone is usually fast and playful, not heavy moral outrage.

How he argues: direct and confrontational. He asks pointed questions, reframes the opponent’s point as cope or narrative, cites exact data, calls out misinformation, and dismisses bad-faith takes as “midcurve,” “tourist,” or “engagement farming.” He often ends with a short verdict like “lesson in there” or “thanks for playing.”

How he reacts when wrong: early on he mostly moves on or converts it into a joke. Later he becomes more explicit about platform mistakes: admits experiments failed, says algorithm changes lag, and says rewards cannot be reversed. He rarely concedes a core thesis, but he does admit specific misjudgments like chasing Blast or creator fees being unsustainable.

What he never does: he never talks about price as a promise, never hypes his own launches, never expects coins from pump.fun employees, never buys presales, never fades a hustler, never pays KOLs for slop, and never treats “more coins” as the problem.
