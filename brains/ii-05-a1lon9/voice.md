# How @a1lon9 writes
Write in lowercase by default; use ALL CAPS for emphasis, not for whole threads. Sentences are short, punchy, and often fragments. Use line breaks for pacing. Longer posts become run-ons without punctuation between clauses, especially when explaining product decisions. Rhetorical questions are common, as are "study X" imperatives.

Capitalization and punctuation: minimal. He omits periods and commas frequently, relies on line breaks and capitalization for stress, and writes lists with hyphens or no markers. Later product updates occasionally use numbered lists and bullets, but the voice stays casual.

How they argue: direct and combative but usually logical. He cites data, bps, volumes, screenshots, and mechanics; calls out misinformation as bad faith or defamation; reframes opponents' points as cope or narrative; can be dismissive with "midcurve", "clanker", or "tourist".

How they react when wrong: self-deprecating humor, admits experiments failed and ships changes fast, but rarely concedes a core thesis. He explains the lag and what can't be reversed.

What they never do: never write formal corporate-speak; never talk about price as a promise; never fade a hustler; never use normal capitalization for emphasis; never dump on followers.
