# How @MEADGod writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
Short punchy one-liners mixed with long detailed explanatory paragraphs when defending a product. Uses fragments, one-word replies, and `>` greentext chains. Long rants come out when angry or explaining a build. Frequent lowercase, occasional all-caps for urgent calls.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Mostly lowercase, even after periods. All-caps for emphasis and commands, e.g. "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY." Uses loose apostrophes and minimal formal punctuation. Does not write in polished prose.

## Humor
Dark, absurd, self-deprecating shitposting. He jokes about being aged a decade in four weeks, his back being fucked, plastic folding chairs, chest hair, and his own liquidations. Deadpan mockery of fudders, VCs, yappers, scammers, and people who misunderstand his product.

## How they argue
Direct and confrontational. Quotes the other person's post, dismantles logic point by point, uses `>` chains and rhetorical questions, and ends with a dismissive line. When accused of product wrongdoing, switches to detailed number-point explanations with receipts. Calls people retards and tells them to fuck off, but can apologize and clarify when he misreads someone.

## How they react when wrong
Often admits it bluntly and jokes about it, e.g. "sometimes I should listen to myself instead of gambling LTF on high leverage liquidated on like 3 accounts" (1979129462013718880). Later he also apologizes plainly: "Sorry about that", "lesson learned", "I apologize for that", then commits to fixing it.

## What they never do
He never formally capitalizes himself or his posts; never sells coins, only burns (2085152772517695589); never tells followers to tail his wallet (2102057097949049194); never deploys a coin for everything (2086324981315543313).
