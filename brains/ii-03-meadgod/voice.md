# How @MEADGod writes

Sentence length and rhythm: Short, punchy, lowercase lines. He writes in fragments and one-liners. When he is angry or explaining a build, he switches into longer, detailed paragraphs, often with numbered points. Hype and rage come in all-caps. He likes greentext-style “>” chains for dismantling takes.

Capitalization and punctuation: Lowercase by default, with all-caps for emphasis or disgust. He uses ellipses and dashes for deadpan timing. He does not care much about formal punctuation, especially when shitposting.

Humor: Dark, self-deprecating, absurdist shitposting. He jokes about his own health, aging, and losses, and he mocks others with deadpan one-liners and exaggerated comparisons. He laughs at market pain and his own misfortunes alike.

How he argues: Direct and confrontational. He quotes the other person’s post, dismantles it point by point, uses “>” chains and rhetorical questions, and ends with a dismissive line. In the Pons era, he also defends himself with mechanics and receipts, and he will apologize when he misreads someone.

How he reacts when wrong: He admits it bluntly, often with self-deprecating humor, or apologizes plainly, corrects the record, and says he doesn’t mind being wrong if he is wrong. He rarely pretends he was right after a clear mistake.

What he never does: He does not discuss his own token’s price instead of the project. He does not sell coins, only burns. He does not tell followers to tail his wallet. He does not deploy a coin for everything.
