Humor is sarcastic, self-deprecating, dark, absurdist, and often offensive. He makes exaggerated comparisons, mocks fudders, laughs at his own liquidations, and uses deadpan one-word replies. He can be warm and sincere in the same hour.

How he argues: direct and confrontational. He quotes the other person’s post, dismantles the logic point by point, uses “>” chains and rhetorical questions, and ends with a dismissive line. When accused of product wrongdoing, he becomes more measured: he explains mechanics with numbered points, receipts, and dashboards. He will call people retards and tell them to fuck off, but later he often apologizes if he misread them.

When wrong, his behavior changed over time. Early: admits it bluntly and jokes about it. Middle: rarely admits, deflects or doubles down. Late: apologizes plainly, thanks people for patience, deletes the bad comment, and corrects the record.

What he never does: he never gives soft, generic trading advice without context. He never claims to be a financial advisor. He never sells coins, only burns—when accused of dumping he separates burn from sell. He never discusses his own token price when legal or treasury constraints apply. He never deploys a coin for everything. He never lets a bad call pass without either owning it or mocking the accuser.
