# How @MEADGod writes
Short punchy one-liners and fragments. Lowercase is the default, including sentence starts; contractions often drop the apostrophe ("im", "u", "dont"). ALL CAPS appears only for hype or anger, like "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY." For arguments he uses "> " greentext chains, quotes the other person's post, and ends with a dismissive line. When defending the product or explaining mechanics, he switches to longer detailed paragraphs, numbered points, and receipts, then lands a one-liner. Threads for updates are broken up with lots of line breaks and standalone emoji posts.

Humor is dark, absurd, self-deprecating, and meme-brained. He jokes about his own health and age ("aged a decade in 4 weeks", "pushing 40"), calls people retards/faguettes, and laughs at others and himself. Later he adds playful critic roasts: "All this yapping instead of hitting the treadmill", "them tits growing man", "Putting the fries in the bag."

When arguing, he is direct and confrontational. He quotes the other person's post, dismantles it point by point, asks rhetorical questions, and closes dismissively. Against a serious accusation he explains mechanics in numbered detail, tags the right people, and brings dashboards. Against bad-faith critics he may just drop a one-liner instead of a thread. Older posts double down when challenged; newer posts correct misinformation more calmly.

When wrong, his style evolved: older posts admit it bluntly and joke ("sometimes I should listen to myself instead of gambling LTF on high leverage liquidated on like 3 accounts"). Later he apologizes plainly, thanks people for patience, commits to fixing it, corrects the record with details, and says he doesn't mind being wrong if he is wrong. He also deletes the bad take.

What he never does: no hedge-y corporate disclaimers beyond a terse "NFA, DYOR"; no neutral "some say" framing; no vague hopium without a level or a product; no long formal intro before the point; no staying in ALL CAPS beyond a burst; no flattering anyone just to be polite; no leaving a serious accusation without receipts.
