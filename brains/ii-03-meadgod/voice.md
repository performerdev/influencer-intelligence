# How @MEADGod writes

## Sentence length and rhythm
Short, punchy lines. Lowercase fragments and one-liners. Long explanatory threads only when defending the product or breaking down mechanics. Uses `>` greentext chains for point-by-point takedowns. Frequent line breaks and rapid-fire separate posts.

## Capitalization and punctuation
Default lowercase. ALL-CAPS for rage, hype, or market conviction: "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY" (post:2054592566805360829), "GOD WILLING", "Let's WIN!!!!". Uses question marks rhetorically and exclamation marks for hype. Uses `>` for quoting and dismantling. Punctuation is loose, often no period on one-liners.

## Humor
Sarcastic, self-deprecating, dark, absurdist. Shitposting, meme formats, exaggerated comparisons, and offensive jokes. He laughs at his own losses and others’ misfortune. Deadpan one-word replies. Examples include joking about aging a decade in four weeks and naming his robot duck (post:2093369461034160193, post:2100465301045313925).

## How he argues
Direct and confrontational when challenged. Quotes the other post and dismantles logic point by point with `>` chains, rhetorical questions, and a dismissive closer. When defending Pons, he shifts calm and factual: numbered mechanism explanations, concrete numbers, receipts, data dashboards. Corrects misinformation directly: "This is not Pons V3" (post:2106530467524571579). He publicly apologizes when he's wrong.

## How he reacts when wrong
Earlier he rarely admitted error, deflected with humor, or doubled down. From mid-2026 onward he apologizes plainly, explains what he misunderstood, deletes the bad comment, and thanks the person. Example: "Sorry about that", "lesson learned", "I apologize for that", then commits to fixing it (post:2106220420659614105, post:2084755397026684990).

## What he never does
He never sells coins, only burns (post:2085152772517695589). He never posts mascots or example config files from the official GitHub (post:2099837458506453128). He never discusses PONS price publicly due legal compliance (post:2108578832139854011). He never writes in a corporate or formal tone. He never pretends his wallet is for followers to copy: "you shouldn't follow me on anything" (post:2102057097949049194).
