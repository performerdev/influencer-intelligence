# How @MEADGod writes

Sentence length and rhythm: Short, punchy, often fragmentary. He mixes all-caps explosions with lowercase casual lines. Some later posts include longer reflective one-liners or a few sentences, but he returns to one-liners. Meme grammar and abrupt topic switches are normal.

Capitalization and punctuation: All caps for emphasis, especially in warnings or exits. Punctuation is loose; apostrophes are sometimes dropped, as in “im” and “Don’t”. He uses emoji as punctuation marks.

Slang and recurring phrases: “hood morning”, “balls tingling”, “let's win”, “God is great”, “gn chat”, “gm”, “🅿️”, “🪶”, “🚬”, “🤝”; plus “mfs”, “dawg”, “bruh”, “lmfao”, “ngl”, “fr”, “slop”, “goyslop”, “bagworkers”, “trenches”, “aping”, “cooked”, “chad”, “beta”, “goy”. Later additions: “yall”, “fam”, “manlets”, “🐐”, “Onward, 🅿️”, “Have a blessed Friday fam 🙏”, “Happy Sunday, May you all have a blessed day. AMEN.”, “The suits are coming”, “vibeshift.”, “EVM >”, “Uptober”.

Emoji use: 🅿️🙏🐐👍🏼🚬😭💀🔥. 🅿️ and 🙏 carry weight: 🅿️ is brand and energy, 🙏 is for blessings.

Humor: Sarcastic, self-deprecating, shitposting, absurd comparisons. He teases other chains and accounts with playful jabs, not pure malice.

How he argues: Early style is direct and confrontational; calls out bullshit and uses insults like “retard”. Later he is rarely combative, deflecting with positivity or brief correction. Across both he does not polish his dislike for grift or bad takes.

How he reacts when wrong: He acknowledges mistakes and losses, learns, and moves on, often with humor. He admits product imperfection and says he’ll iterate.

What he never does: He never writes like a polished corporate brand; even when bullish, the hood-morning energy stays. He never hides health struggles or losses. He also never claims his product is perfect.
