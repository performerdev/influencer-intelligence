# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview
- **DeFi is mostly fake TVL and incentive-chasing; only a few venues are legit.** He reads validator emissions and BEX incentives as evidence that most "users" and volume are rented, not real. He later sharpens this into "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (1963860768929063302, 1969059963013071208, 2035807710428451140).
- **Stablecoins will cascade.** He expects stablecoins backed by other stablecoins and off-chain entities to depeg into the next bear market, repeating the warning into summer 2026 (1965327296056996015, 1965490929625956439, 1984606423997612348, 2051386212628246795).
- **Privacy is a human right and on-chain finance should be private by default.** Early Roots-era peak, and it survives the Roots shutdown; by late 2026 he argues transparency makes people appreciate privacy in finance even more (1980749499074003227, 1981316578135208422, 2106214629454094469, 2106216341745148127).
- **Wallet counts are not users.** The real user base is tiny; bot farms dominate CT and Kaito-style engagement. Organic reach is dead (1965834271916175582, 1971306267684766061, 1980165662090883232, 2004574119220920694).
- **Founders must stick to what they built.** Grant-hoppers and flip-floppers are losers; the people who ship and stay are the only ones he values (1967149024278143387, 1973781980002337096).
- **Chasing trends always loses.** Build your own category and make others chase you; be your own edge instead of a wrapper around Aave/Morpho or someone else's infrastructure (1982395623220662753, 1982395907904880904, 1984053107601633353).
- **CEX UX keeps retail sidelined.** On-chain perps and clean off-ramps are the fix; CEX UI/UX is terrible (1976054324968235405, 1977690653246063086).
- **Neobanks wrapping Aave/Morpho for +5% are "cancerous" and add no innovation.** He distinguishes wrappers from products with their own infrastructure (1984053107601633353, 1984919233466102060).
- **InfoFi, Kaito and airdrop farming are the worst things to happen to the space.** No airdrop is worth farming if you don't believe in the product (1998398078802399535, 1994405492517486596, 2067919596506468497).
- **Token buybacks are often a psyop; crypto tokens are suicide for most projects.** Teams have no feel for the cycle, and most altcoins are uninvestable because few succeed (2007401338372526381, 2031850768136093962).
- **AI economics changed over the year.** Early 2026 he argues AI is not replacing workers; it increases workload and needs heavy human cleanup, and LLM subscription prices are VC-subsidized and will rise (2021206344380920248, 2025851761731281035, 2025852190460395874). By mid-2026 he flips partially: AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear, but the AI bubble will burst because cheap models commoditize the space (2033112821752623130, 2064989599487651963, 2055404792563057128).
- **Europe and Germany are mismanaged, overtaxed and over-regulated.** Leaving is the move for smart EU people (1982191593873412546, 2040112263760404592, 2047062662698201200).
- **Crypto is bloated with grifters and too little innovation.** Most will return to 9-5s; the industry has made little progress (1975443367812288834, 2018249252598682003, 2018499924464222711).
- **God, family, health and work are what he is all in on.** Numbers on a screen don't change happiness; "God giveth and God taketh away" (2033725422551371892, 2081068081585992049).
- **Building real revenue-generating products beats chasing shitcoins.** A token utility shift is coming, and Pons is the vehicle (2068271828791894245, 2068271923302113780, 2078221139285795194).
- **Robinhood chain is the main battlefield of this cycle.** It is purpose-built for speculating on all assets, especially RWAs and memes; "the people's launchpad" must be fair, not bundled coordinated dogshit (2078221139285795194, 2081860851250671905, 2097091081087172767).
- **The upcoming cycle will be all about EVM.** Solana ecosystem is hated and fading (2081959280391958920, 2072345596342460787).
- **Chinese users will lead the Robinhood bullrun.** "Crypto isn't crypto without Chinese participation" (2089777699724108234, 2099064380251836553).
- **In the AI era, reputation is the most important asset.** A successful reputation layer will not be code-based but P2P/human-oracle in the real world as AI, bots and robots rise (2106846254122647750, 2106905263080923407).
- **Owning one whole Bitcoin will be a sign of wealth.** He has a DCA plan to get there (2108404333561930170).
- **Onchain has its own cycles; you can't fight the market.** But the 4-year cycle remains the best indicator since inception (2104973021413093804, 2104975964144828704).

## How they reason and decide
- **Reads incentives and emissions to judge sustainability.** For Berachain, he sizes up BEX rewards and validator emissions to decide if a product is real or just bribe-driven (1965482203317764572, 1965515870836589000, 1965519189659316732).
- **Pattern recognition and contrarian observation of other traders' hit rates.** He fades CT consensus and tracks who is actually right (1978326822719725791, 1982036053297934672).
- **Judges projects by whether they have their own infrastructure/edge rather than being a wrapper.** Wrapper neobanks fail; native chains and real revenue win (1984053107601633353, 1982395907904880904).
- **Thinks in cycles: bear market timing, bottom targets, when to bid.** Early bear targets were HYPE $8, ETH $1,200-1,500, BTC $47K, SOL $64 or $45; he expected the bear to run ~9 more months from Dec 2025 (1996980501064646853, 2001662494092280314, 2000656074400317930).
- **Weighs counterparty risk and whether a chain's native apps can survive without mercenary incentives.** Counterparty risk being "butchered to almost zero" is a design goal; native organic usage matters more than temporary bribes (1965527217326244147, 1986035133908979972).
- **Evaluates people by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk.** Hiring friends/obsessed people is right; the biggest companies were founded by friends grinding (1967149024278143387, 2022331892822053297, 2068748351063892090).
- **Uses gut feeling and past cycle patterns to call tops/bottoms.** "I don't like to fade my gut"; applies data-driven health/body data to diagnose stress and markets (2052596146749542451, 2062810617719701592, 2059864376979362121).
- **Reads market structure via specific levels and timelines.** Examples: 57K by Friday, 50K by end of July, 60K into weekend, October echo bubble (2069836559948386506, 2069334649134862522, 2071600546356564409, 2062418191242932399).
- **Contrarian: when everyone grasps for bottom signals, expect more pain; when grifters leave, expect a relief rally.** Expects a big player to get nuked before the final bottom (2062949976821403963, 2032512392144498905, 2019526140688306463).
- **Explains mechanics in detail with numbered points when accused.** For Pons tax changes, routing, market cap and buybacks he walks through the mechanism with receipts (2097602877925609720, 2080795196573229358, 2106778812197314652).
- **Uses community dashboards and data to back claims.** He shares revenue/volume numbers and corrects himself when the data changes (2096062642708271463, 2095038780340093102).
- **Distinguishes burn from sell when accused of dumping.** "I never sold, I burned - big difference" (2085152772517695589, 2102057097949049194).
- **Reasons from alignment: only assets issued by Robinhood/Bitstamp.** Keeps it simple like Lego blocks (2098571001084748093, 2084761983652417903).
- **Reads market maker behavior: stock token squeezes get arbitraged away because MMs mint to re-peg.** Thin liquidity squeezes are faded (2094220402683793550, 2094221025433022493).
- **Uses community polls/questions to decide product direction.** Asks the crowd rather than guessing (2084753264591937918, 2085474118065938629).
- **Anchors regime calls on the 4-year cycle.** Even in late 2026, when he thinks the bottom is in, he still calls it technically a bear because the cycle says so (2104975101829476377, 2104975964144828704).
- **Compares current action to 2023 FTX spike-down to frame entries.** Uses historical analogies for accumulation (2108404919375946163).
- **Judges product direction by revenue, extraction and community benefit rather than hype.** V3's value prop is measured that way (2106020226458370443).
