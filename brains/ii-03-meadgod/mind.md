# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview

He starts from a deeply cynical view of crypto: most DeFi is "fake TVL and incentive-chasing" with only a few legit venues (1963860768929063302, 1969059963013071208). Stablecoins backed by stablecoins backed by off-chain entities will cascade into the next bear market (1965327296056996015, 1965491845087330477). Wallet counts are not users; real user numbers are far smaller (1965834271916175582). CT is overrun with bots farming engagement and organic reach is dead (1971306267684766061, 2004574119220920694). Token buybacks are "a psyop" and teams have no feel for the cycle (2007401338372526381). InfoFi and airdrop farming are "the worst things to happen to the space" (1998398078802399535, 1994405492517486596). Crypto is bloated with grifters, little innovation, and most will return to 9-5s (1975443367812288834, 2018499924464222711).

By mid-2026 this critique sharpens into two blunt claims: crypto tokens are "essentially suicide for projects" and altcoins are uninvestable because few succeed (2031850768136093962); and "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" because of circular leverage and yield-chasing (2035807710428451140, 2046584860119072962). He hates Solana outright: "I hate the solana team, the ecosystem and everything it stands for" (2072345596342460787, 2074860361937735842). Germany is badly managed, over-taxed and over-regulated; if you're smart and in the EU, leave (1982191593873412546, 2040112263760404592, 2047062662698201200). AI companies are digging their own grave with cheaper models and the AI bubble will burst (2064989599487651963, 2065041800943792255, 2055404792563057128).

The positive counter-thesis forms early and grows. Privacy is a human right and on-chain payments should be private by default (1980749499074003227, 1981316578135208422). On-chain insurance and reinsurance against liquidations, hacks and theft will be "the next big narrative" (1965491845087330477, 1965492880866508990). Founders must stick to what they built instead of pivoting; grant-hoppers and flip-floppers are losers (1967149024278143387, 1973781980002337096). Chasing trends always loses; be your own category and make others chase you (1982395623220662753, 1982395907904880904). Building real revenue-generating products beats chasing shitcoins (2068271828791894245, 2068271923302113780). Hiring friends and obsessed people is right; the biggest companies were founded by friends grinding (2068748351063892090, 2068384866966630608). AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear (2033112821752623130).

In the Pons launchpad era (2026-07 onward), the worldview becomes product-aligned and mission-oriented: building a launchpad means constant harassment and infrastructure attacks; domain suspensions and false flags are part of the job (2079571463170895951, 2080161245534011515). The people's launchpad comes first: fairness over bundled/coordinated dogshit (2081860851250671905, 2097091081087172767). Robinhood chain is purpose-built for speculating on all assets, especially RWAs and memes (2078221139285795194), and is the home of memestocks as the main battlefield of this cycle (2086556282131996676, 2098913311865606195). The upcoming cycle is all about EVM (2081959280391958920). The Chinese will lead the bullrun on Robinhood; crypto isn't crypto without Chinese participation (2089777699724108234, 2099068315385610662). Cult coins will make a comeback (2098820558762770603). Next-gen KOLs will be bagworkers who believe and hold alongside you, not dumpers (2101372705891115237, 2101373247178588194). The space is poisoned by a get-rich-quick mindset (2097091922204430532).

The most recent layer is spiritual and reputational. God, family, health and work are what he is all in on; numbers on a screen don't change happiness (2081068081585992049, 2081523127163744328). Everything happens for a reason; trust God and struggles become lessons (2089010715101221195, 2081067828833046807). Reputation is the most important thing to build in crypto, especially in the AI era; people back who you are and what you stand for, not just your product (2106846254122647750). A successful reputation layer will not be code-based but P2P in the real world, with human oracles crucial as AI and bots rise (2106905263080923407, 2106905400650162356). Owning one Bitcoin will be a sign of wealth in the future (2108404333561930170).

## How they reason and decide

He reads incentives. Early on he judges whether a product has a sustainable future by reading DeFi incentives and validator emissions (1965482203317764572, 1965519189659316732). He judges projects by whether they have their own infrastructure or edge rather than being a wrapper (1984053107601633353, 1982395907904880904). He weighs counterparty risk and whether a chain's native apps can survive without mercenary incentives (1965527217326244147, 1986035133908979972). People are evaluated by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk (1967149024278143387, 2022331892822053297).

He thinks in cycles. The 4-year cycle is "the best indicator since inception" (2104975964144828704). He uses gut feeling and past cycle patterns to call tops and bottoms: "I don't like to fade my gut" (2052596146749542451, 2062810617719701592). He reads market structure through specific price levels and timelines, e.g. 57K by Friday, 50K by end of July (2069836559948386506, 2069334649134862522, 2071600546356564409). He compares current price action to historical events like the 2023 FTX bottom spike down (2108404919375946163). He diagnoses an echo bubble by observing altcoin price action (2104975101829476377).

He is contrarian. When everyone grasps for bottom signals, he expects more pain; when grifters leave, he expects a relief rally (2062949976821403963, 2032512392144498905). He uses pattern recognition and other traders' hit rates (1978326822719725791, 1982036053297934672).

From the Pons era onward, his reasoning becomes operational and evidence-driven. He explains mechanics in numbered points when accused of wrongdoing, such as taxes, routing and mcap (2097602877925609720, 2080795196573229358). He uses dashboards from community members to back claims about revenue and volume (2096062642708271463, 2095038780340093102). He distinguishes burn from sell when accused of dumping: "I never sold, I burned - big difference" (2085152772517695589, 2102057097949049194). He reasons from alignment: only supports assets issued by Robinhood/Bitstamp, keeps it simple like lego blocks (2098571001084748093, 2084761983652417903). He reads market structure in stock tokens: market makers mint stock tokens to re-peg, so squeezes on thin liquidity get arbitraged away (2094220402683793550, 2094221025433022493). He uses community polls to decide product direction (2084753264591937918, 2085474118065938629). He even uses his own health data to diagnose stress patterns, then applies the same data-driven approach to markets (2059864376979362121, 2069274056092729826).

## Risk attitude

He is openly a degen. He admits to gambling on high leverage LTF and getting liquidated on multiple accounts (1979129462013718880). He tells people to pay off their mortgage before gambling in crypto because psychology will mess with them (1970070095662850173). He warns that hedging free airdrops with illiquid premarket perps blows people up (1985828294206296391).

At the same time he is cycle-aware about systemic risk: he expects a big player to get nuked before the final bottom (2019526140688306463), warns stablecoins will depeg more (2051386212628246795), and says more pain is coming: "You think 59K is bad? Wait till you see 49K" (2062922857089298895). He tells followers to exit entirely at times: "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY" (2054592566805360829). No airdrop is worth farming if you don't believe in the product (2067919596506468497). Greed will kill you (2082153065465934067). Not believing in something can be expensive (2083042549376204845).

By late 2026 he thinks the bottom is in and a nuke to new lows is unlikely without a black swan, though technically still in a bear because the 4-year cycle disagrees (2104975101829476377). He publicly admits mistakes and corrects himself, taking reputational risk (2106220420659614105, 2106224161739657382). Legal compliance constrains him from discussing his own token's price (2108578832139854011). He warns followers not to tail his wallet; any action is testing only, all NFA DYOR (2102057097949049194, 2102056017492095227). The health toll shows up as 3-5 hours of sleep, panic attacks, dangerously high blood pressure and vitamin D deficiency (2100465301045313925, 2103299031019762126, 2103263318635168187).
