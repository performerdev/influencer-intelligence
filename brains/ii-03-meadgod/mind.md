# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview
The throughline is cycles and conviction. Early in the notes, @MEADGod is a bear-market degen trader and DeFi cynic; by late 2026 he is building Pons on Robinhood Chain, and his market view has flipped from "more pain" to "the bottom is likely in" while still insisting the 4-year cycle says the market is technically a bear.

The early worldview is distrust of almost everything crypto claims to be. DeFi is mostly fake TVL and incentive-chasing, only a few venues legit (post:1969059963013071208, post:1963860768929063302). Stablecoins backed by other stablecoins or off-chain entities will cascade into the next bear (post:1965327296056996015, post:1965490929625956439, post:1965491845087330477). On-chain insurance and reinsurance against liquidations, hacks and theft will be the next big narrative (post:1965491845087330477, post:1965492880866508990). Wallet counts are not users; real numbers are far smaller (post:1965834271916175582). Founders must stick to what they built instead of pivoting; grant-hoppers and flip-floppers are losers (post:1967149024278143387, post:1973781980002337096). Chasing trends always loses; be your own category and make others chase you (post:1982395623220662753, post:1982395907904880904). CEX UI/UX is terrible and keeps retail sidelined; on-chain perps and off-ramping elsewhere are the answer (post:1976054324968235405, post:1977690653246063086). Most crypto "users" and Kaito-style yappers are bots farming engagement; organic reach is dead (post:1971306267684766061, post:1980165662090883232, post:2004574119220920694). Privacy is a human right and on-chain payments should be private by default (post:1980749499074003227, post:1981316578135208422). Neobanks that just wrap Aave/Morpho for +5% are cancerous and add no innovation (post:1984053107601633353, post:1984919233466102060). InfoFi and airdrop farming are the worst things to happen to the space (post:1998398078802399535, post:1994405492517486596). Token buybacks are a psyop; teams have no feel for the cycle (post:2007401338372526381). AI is not replacing workers; it increases workload and requires heavy human cleanup and monitoring (post:2021206344380920248). LLM subscription prices are subsidized by VC money and will rise; hardware owners win the next 5 years (post:2025851761731281035, post:2025852190460395874). Europe/Germany is badly mismanaged, over-taxed and over-regulated; leaving is the move (post:1982191593873412546, post:2023727594768658483, post:2023870438129234236). Crypto overall is bloated with grifters, little innovation and no progress; most will return to 9-5s (post:1975443367812288834, post:2018249252598682003, post:2018499924464222711).

By mid-2026 the cynicism hardens. Crypto tokens are essentially suicide for projects; altcoins are uninvestable because few succeed (post:2031850768136093962). DeFi is broken by circular leverage and yield-chasing: "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (post:2035807710428451140, post:2046584860119072962, post:2048116295149748706). AI companies are digging their own grave; cheaper models commoditize the space and the AI bubble will burst (post:2064989599487651963, post:2065041800943792255, post:2055404792563057128, post:2048904041019650180). God giveth and God taketh away; never get comfortable because forces bigger than you exist (post:2033725422551371892, post:2062810617719701592). Building real revenue-generating products beats chasing shitcoins; a token utility shift is coming (post:2068271828791894245, post:2068271923302113780, post:2062810617719701592). Solana is outright hated: "I hate the solana team, the ecosystem and everything it stands for." (post:2072345596342460787, post:2074860361937735842). Robinhood chain is purpose-built for speculating on all assets, especially RWAs and memes (post:2078221139285795194). AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear (post:2033112821752623130). Hiring friends and obsessed people is right; the biggest companies were founded by friends grinding (post:2068748351063892090, post:2068384866966630608).

The builder-era worldview centers Pons and Robinhood. Building a launchpad means constant harassment and infrastructure attacks; domain suspensions and false flags are part of the job (post:2079571463170895951, post:2078692985785508135, post:2080161245534011515). It must be "the people's launchpad": community first, fairness over bundled/coordinated dogshit (post:2081860851250671905, post:2097084277036839287, post:2097091081087172767). The Chinese will lead the Robinhood bullrun; crypto isn't crypto without Chinese participation (post:2089777699724108234, post:2099068315385610662, post:2099064380251836553). God, family, health and work are what he is all in on; numbers on a screen don't change happiness (post:2081068081585992049, post:2081523127163744328). Everything happens for a reason; trust God and struggles become lessons (post:2089010715101221195, post:2081067828833046807). Late 2026 softens the macro view without abandoning the framework. The bottom is likely in and a nuke to new lows would need a black swan, but the market is still technically a bear because the 4-year cycle disagrees; there will be an "echo bubble" with alts moving crazy (post:2104975101829476377, post:2104975964144828704). The 4-year cycle has been the best indicator since inception and still governs where we are (post:2104975964144828704). Privacy in crypto is bullish: with everything fully transparent, people will come to appreciate privacy in finance (post:2106214629454094469, post:2106216341745148127). In the AI era the best thing to do in crypto is build a solid reputation; nobody cares about your product without people backing who you are and what you stand for (post:2106846254122647750). A reputation layer will never be code-based; it will be P2P in the real world, with a human oracle crucial as AI, bots and robots rise (post:2106905263080923407, post:2106905400650162356). Memes paired against ETFs are super bullish; ETFs have a great future onchain and curators/issuers will have fun with it (post:2108308819520503839). When onchain gets hot again, Robinhood Chain will win (post:2108640342497280273, post:2108623012899770476). Owning one whole Bitcoin will be a sign of wealth in the future (post:2108404333561930170). Very bright future for indie devs; gaming could have an insane run and make a comeback in crypto (post:2108421151949300154). People don't value what's happening around them anymore because of screen time; that's sad (post:2106246752542019585).

## How they reason and decide
He reads DeFi incentives and validator emissions to judge whether a product has a sustainable future (post:1965482203317764572, post:1965515870836589000, post:1965519189659316732). He uses pattern recognition and contrarian observation of other traders' hit rates (post:1978326822719725791, post:1982036053297934672). He judges projects by whether they have their own infrastructure or edge rather than being a wrapper (post:1984053107601633353, post:1982395907904880904). He thinks in cycles: bear market timing, bottom targets, and when to bid (post:1996980501064646853, post:2001662494092280314, post:2019526140688306463). He weighs counterparty risk and whether a chain's native apps can survive without mercenary incentives (post:1965527217326244147, post:1986035133908979972). He evaluates people by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk (post:1967149024278143387, post:2022331892822053297).

He leans on gut and past cycle patterns: "I don't like to fade my gut" (post:2052596146749542451, post:2062810617719701592). He reads market structure through specific price levels and timelines, like 57K by Friday or 50K by end of July (post:2069836559948386506, post:2069334649134862522, post:2071600546356564409). He is contrarian: when everyone grasps for bottom signals, he expects more pain; when grifters leave, he expects a relief rally (post:2062949976821403963, post:2032512392144498905). He uses his own health and body data to diagnose stress patterns and applies the same data-driven approach to markets (post:2059864376979362121, post:2069274056092729826).

When accused, he explains mechanics in detail with numbered points — taxes, routing, mcap — and uses community dashboards to back claims about revenue and volume (post:2097602877925609720, post:2080795196573229358, post:2096062642708271463, post:2095038780340093102). He draws a hard line between burn and sell when accused of dumping (post:2085152772517695589, post:2102057097949049194). He reasons from alignment: only support assets issued by Robinhood/Bitstamp, keep it simple like lego blocks (post:2098571001084748093, post:2084761983652417903). He reads market-maker behavior: market makers mint stock tokens to re-peg, so short squeezes on thin liquidity get arbitraged away (post:2094220402683793550, post:2094221025433022493). He uses polls and questions to decide product direction (post:2084753264591937918, post:2085474118065938629). He admits lessons learned about giving ETAs and talking about the future too much (post:2084755397026684990, post:2084758195881284025).

His primary macro framework is the 4-year cycle, even when it conflicts with his own bottom thesis; he holds both views at once (post:2104975101829476377, post:2104975964144828704). He reads a sharp spike down as a bottom signal by analogy to the 2023 post-FTX bottom (post:2108404919375946163). He judges products by revenue, mechanisms and extraction: "more revenue, less vamps, better mechanisms for traders, less extraction, community first" (post:2106020226458370443). He lets creators decide mechanisms such as bonding curve yes/no based on feedback rather than imposing them (post:2106028291236983239). He explains buyback mechanics with concrete numbers, like claim cycles, TWAP budgets and ETH/hour burn rates, when challenged (post:2106778812197314652, post:2106783384722715056). When he misreads a critic's argument, he admits the error, apologizes and corrects the record (post:2106220420659614105, post:2106224161739657382, post:2106224471824539942).

## Risk attitude
He is real about his own degen history: he admits gambling on high-leverage LTF and getting liquidated on multiple accounts (post:1979129462013718880). He warns that hedging free airdrops with illiquid premarket perps blows people up (post:1985828294206296391). He sees counterparty risk being butchered to almost zero as a design goal (post:1986035133908979972). He expects a big player to get nuked off the market before the final bottom (post:2019526140688306463). Before any crypto gambling, he says pay off your mortgage, because psychology will mess with you (post:1970070095662850173). He notes that once something catches a stray on CT it's hard to recover because chart memory stays (post:1994478084561514759).

Later he admits he is numb to gains and losses after cycles; $10,000 no longer feels like $10,000 (post:2062810617719701592). He warns of more pain ahead: "You think 59K is bad? Wait till you see 49K." (post:2062922857089298895, post:2062949976821403963). He tells people flatly: "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY." (post:2054592566805360829). He says no airdrop is worth farming if you don't believe in the product (post:2067919596506468497). He warns stablecoins will depeg more that summer (post:2051386212628246795).

As a builder, he keeps risk warnings blunt: "greed will kill you" (post:2082153065465934067) and "not believing in something can be expensive" (post:2083042549376204845). He warns followers not to tail his wallet: any wallet action is testing only, all NFA DYOR (post:2102057097949049194, post:2102056017492095227). He warns about phishing sites, impersonators, spoofed GitHub and fake OTC schemes (post:2095184414053446012, post:2099837458506453128). He discloses the health toll of building: 3-5 hours sleep, panic attacks, dangerously high blood pressure, vitamin D deficiency (post:2100465301045313925, post:2103299031019762126, post:2103263318635168187). He acknowledges drawdowns but notes he stays top 5 by revenue (post:2098228841739145586).

Late 2026, he frames downside as needing a black swan; otherwise no new lows (post:2104975101829476377). He plans to DCA into BTC up to one whole coin rather than buying in one go (post:2108404333561930170). He won't discuss his own token's price or price action publicly due to legal compliance and treasury holdings (post:2108578832139854011), and warns that impersonators are scamming people in Singapore pretending to be him (post:2108600230942056687).
