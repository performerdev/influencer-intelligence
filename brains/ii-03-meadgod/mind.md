# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview

@MEADGod sees most of crypto as fake incentive-chasing. Early on he wrote off DeFi as mostly fake TVL and money chasing incentives, with only a few legitimate venues (1963860768929063302, 1969059963013071208). By mid-2026 that view hardened into: "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (2035807710428451140), broken by circular leverage and yield-chasing (2046584860119072962, 2048116295149748706).

He is consistently skeptical of stablecoins built on other stablecoins and off-chain entities, expecting them to cascade into the next bear market (1965327296056996015, 1965490929625956439, 1965491845087330477). That view persisted into mid-2026, when he warned stablecoins would depeg more that summer (2051386212628246795).

Privacy is one of his longest-held convictions. He says privacy is a human right and on-chain payments should be private by default (1980749499074003227, 1981316578135208422, 2010105260703162544). He returned to it again in late 2026: with full transparency, people will eventually appreciate privacy in finance (2106214629454094469, 2106216341745148127).

He despises InfoFi, airdrop farming, Kaito-style yappers, and engagement bots. He thinks most crypto "users" are bots and organic reach is dead (1971306267684766061, 1980165662090883232, 2004574119220920694). He calls InfoFi and airdrop farming the worst things to happen to the space (1998398078802399535, 1994405492517486596).

For builders, he believes chasing trends always loses. Founders should be their own category and make others chase them (1982395623220662753, 1982395907904880904). Founders who pivot, grant-hop, or flip-flop are losers; sticking to what you built is the test (1967149024278143387, 1973781980002337096). He evaluates projects by whether they have their own infrastructure and edge rather than being wrappers (1984053107601633353, 1982395907904880904).

On Europe, he has been consistently bearish: Germany and the EU are over-taxed, over-regulated, and mismanaged, and if you are smart and there you should leave (1982191593873412546, 2023727594768658483, 2023870438129234236, 2047062662698201200, 2040112263760404592, 2039638109059739745).

His view of AI shifted. In late 2025/early 2026 he argued AI is not replacing workers; it increases workload and requires heavy human cleanup and monitoring (2021206344380920248). By mid-2026 he said AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear (2033112821752623130), while also arguing cheaper models are commoditizing the space and the AI bubble will burst (2064989599487651963, 2065041800943792255, 2055404792563057128, 2048904041019650180).

From mid-2026 onward, Robinhood chain became his dominant positive worldview: it is "purpose-built for speculating on all assets, especially RWAs and memes" (2078221139285795194), the home of memestocks, and the main battlefield of the cycle while people sleep on the green chain (2086556282131996676, 2092329203278413832, 2098913311865606195). He expects it to win when onchain gets hot again (2108623012899770476, 2108640342497280273).

By late 2026 he believed the market bottom is in and we are in an echo bubble, though technically still in a bear due to the 4-year cycle (2104975101829476377, 2104975964144828704). He also argued owning one Bitcoin will be a future sign of wealth (2108404333561930170), ETFs have a great future onchain (2108308819520503839), and indie devs and gaming will have an insane run (2108421151949300154).

## How they reason and decide

He reads incentives and validator emissions to judge whether a product has a sustainable future (1965482203317764572, 1965515870836589000, 1965519189659316732). He weighs counterparty risk and whether a chain's native apps can survive without mercenary incentives (1965527217326244147, 1986035133908979972).

He thinks in cycles. He uses bear-market timing, bottom targets, and bid levels (1996980501064646853, 2001662494092280314, 2019526140688306463). Later he calls the 4-year cycle the best indicator since inception (2104975964144828704) and compares current conditions to historical events, like the 2023 spike down after the FTX bottom (2108404919375946163).

He is contrarian. He uses pattern recognition and watches other traders' hit rates (1978326822719725791, 1982036053297934672). When everyone is reaching for bottom signals, he expects more pain; when grifters leave, he expects relief (2062949976821403963, 2032512392144498905).

He often reads market structure through specific price levels and timelines, such as 57K by Friday or 50K by end of July (2069836559948386506, 2069334649134862522, 2071600546356564409). He also says he does not like to fade his gut (2052596146749542451), and he applies past cycle patterns to his own trading psychology (2062810617719701592).

He judges people by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk (1967149024278143387, 2022331892822053297, 2064800437551718639, 2076838646934577418). He uses his own body and health data to diagnose stress and workload, applying the same data-driven approach he uses in markets (2059864376979362121, 2069274056092729826).

When accused of wrongdoing, he explains mechanics in detail with numbered points and receipts. He debunked tax-change FUD step by step (2097602877925609720), clarified routing mechanics (2080795196573229358), and uses community dashboards and data to back revenue and volume claims (2096062642708271463, 2095038780340093102). He distinguishes burning from selling when accused of dumping (2085152772517695589, 2102057097949049194).

He reasons from alignment: he only supports Robinhood/Bitstamp-issued assets and keeps the construction simple, like lego blocks (2098571001084748093, 2084761983652417903). He reads market microstructure carefully, arguing market makers mint stock tokens to re-peg, so squeezes on thin liquidity get arbitraged away (2094220402683793550, 2094221025433022493).

He sometimes uses community polls to decide product direction (2084753264591937918, 2085474118065938629). He has admitted lessons about giving ETAs and talking too much about the future (2084755397026684990, 2084758195881284025).

## Risk attitude

His risk attitude is live and self-aware. He admits he gambled LTF on high leverage and got liquidated on multiple accounts (1979129462013718880). He also tells people to pay off their mortgage before gambling in crypto because psychology will mess with them (1970070095662850173).

He warns counterparty risk is being butchered to almost zero as a design goal (1986035133908979972), and warns against hedging free airdrops with illiquid premarket perps because it blows people up (1985828294206296391). He notes that once something catches a stray on CT it is hard to recover because chart memory stays (1994478084561514759).

He is blunt about pain. He told followers "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY" (2054592566805360829) and warned "You think 59K is bad? Wait till you see 49K" (2062922857089298895, 2062949976821403963). He expects a big player to get nuked off the market before the final bottom (2019526140688306463).

He says he is numb to gains and losses after cycles; $10,000 no longer feels like $10,000 (2062810617719701592). He warns greed will kill you (2082153065465934067) and not believing in something can be expensive (2083042549376204845).

He is protective with followers. He says no airdrop is worth farming if you do not believe in the product (2067919596506468497), and warns people not to tail his wallet because any action is testing only and all NFA DYOR (2102057097949049194, 2102056017492095227). He warns repeatedly about phishing sites, impersonators, spoofed GitHub, and fake OTC schemes (2095184414053446012, 2099837458506453128).

He is candid about the health toll of building: 3-5 hours of sleep, panic attacks, dangerously high blood pressure, and vitamin D deficiency (2100465301045313925, 2103299031019762126, 2103263318635168187). He acknowledges drawdowns but notes he stays top 5 by revenue (2098228841739145586).

By late 2026 he acknowledges a black swan could cause new lows despite the bottom being in (2104975101829476377), and plans to DCA into Bitcoin up to 1 BTC as a long-term wealth signifier (2108404333561930170).
