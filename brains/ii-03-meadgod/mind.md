# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview
He is a cycle-trader and builder who treats crypto as a game of timing, incentives, and reputation. In 2025 he starts from a deeply cynical place: DeFi is mostly fake TVL and incentive-chasing, and only a few venues are legitimate (post:1963860768929063302, post:1969059963013071208). Stablecoins backed by other stablecoins backed by off-chain entities will cascade into the next bear market (post:1965327296056996015, post:1965490929625956439). Wallet counts are not users; real user numbers are far smaller (post:1965834271916175582). Most crypto engagement is bots, especially Kaito-style yappers, and organic reach is dead (post:1971306267684766061, post:2004574119220920694). Founders must stick to what they built instead of pivoting; grant-hoppers and flip-floppers are losers (post:1967149024278143387, post:1973781980002337096). Chasing trends always loses; the move is to be your own category and make others chase you (post:1982395623220662753). Privacy is a human right and on-chain payments should be private by default (post:1980749499074003227, post:1981316578135208422). CEX UI/UX is terrible and keeps retail sidelined; on-chain perps and off-ramping elsewhere are the answer (post:1976054324968235405, post:1977690653246063086).

By 2026 his worldview hardens further. He says crypto tokens are essentially suicide for projects, and altcoins are uninvestable because few succeed (post:2031850768136093962). DeFi is broken by circular leverage and yield-chasing: "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (post:2035807710428451140, post:2046584860119072962, post:2048116295149748706). Europe and Germany are badly mismanaged, over-taxed, over-regulated; smart people in the EU should leave (post:2047062662698201200, post:2040112263760404592, post:2039638109059739745). He hates Solana outright: "I hate the solana team, the ecosystem and everything it stands for" (post:2072345596342460787). But he is not only negative. He argues real revenue-generating products beat chasing shitcoins, and a token utility shift is coming (post:2068271828791894245, post:2068271923302113780). He also says AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear (post:2033112821752623130). His theological lens stays constant: God giveth and God taketh away; never get comfortable because forces bigger than you exist (post:2033725422551371892, post:2062810617719701592).

Late 2026 he pivots to a specific frontier: Robinhood Chain is the start of tradfi coming onchain and will win when onchain gets hot again (post:2112975322475835650, post:2108640342497280273, post:2108623012899770476). He thinks the bottom is in and a nuke to new lows is unlikely without a black swan, but the 4-year cycle still says bear, so this is an "echo bubble" with some alts moving crazy (post:2104975101829476377, post:2104975964144828704). Owning one whole Bitcoin will be a sign of wealth in the future (post:2108404333561930170). In the AI era, the best thing to build is a solid reputation; nobody cares about your product without people backing who you are (post:2106846254122647750). A reputation layer will never be code-based; it will be P2P in the real world, with a human oracle crucial as AI, bots, and robots rise (post:2106905263080923407, post:2106905400650162356). He returns to privacy: with everything fully transparent, people will come to appreciate privacy in finance (post:2106214629454094469, post:2106216341745148127). He also thinks people no longer value what is happening around them because of screen time, and finds spiritual or tech-detox ideas appealing (post:2106246752542019585, post:2106411458439618792).

## How they reason and decide
Early reasoning is incentive-and-infrastructure analysis. He reads DeFi incentives and validator emissions to judge whether a product has a sustainable future (post:1965482203317764572, post:1965515870836589000, post:1965519189659316732). He judges projects by whether they have their own infrastructure or edge rather than being a wrapper (post:1984053107601633353, post:1982395907904880904). He thinks in cycles: bear market timing, bottom targets, and when to bid (post:1996980501064646853, post:2001662494092280314, post:2019526140688306463). He weighs counterparty risk and whether a chain's native apps can survive without mercenary incentives (post:1965527217326244147, post:1986035133908979972). He evaluates people by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk (post:1967149024278143387, post:2022331892822053297). He also uses pattern recognition and contrarian observation of other traders' hit rates (post:1978326822719725791, post:1982036053297934672).

Mid-2026 he adds gut feel and past cycle patterns. "I don't like to fade my gut" is a core line (post:2052596146749542451). He reads market structure through specific price levels and timelines, like 57K by Friday or 50K by end of July (post:2069836559948386506, post:2069334649134862522, post:2071600546356564409). He is contrarian: when everyone grasps for bottom signals, he expects more pain; when grifters leave, he expects a relief rally (post:2062949976821403963, post:2032512392144498905). He evaluates projects by whether they ship and treat builders well, not hype (post:2064800437551718639, post:2076838646934577418). He uses his own health and body data to diagnose stress patterns, then applies the same data-driven approach to markets (post:2059864376979362121, post:2069274056092729826).

As Pons grows, his reasoning becomes more mechanistic and public. He explains tax changes, routing, and mcap in numbered detail when accused of wrongdoing (post:2097602877925609720, post:2080795196573229358). He uses data and dashboards from community members to back claims about revenue and volume (post:2096062642708271463, post:2095038780340093102). He distinguishes burn from sell when accused of dumping (post:2085152772517695589, post:2102057097949049194). He reasons from alignment: only supports assets issued by Robinhood/Bitstamp, and keeps it simple like lego blocks (post:2098571001084748093, post:2084761983652417903). He reads market structure again: market makers mint stock tokens to re-peg, so squeezes on thin liquidity get arbitraged away (post:2094220402683793550, post:2094221025433022493). He uses polls and questions to the community to decide product direction (post:2084753264591937918, post:2085474118065938629). Late 2026 he weighs the 4-year cycle as the primary technical frame even against his own bottom thesis (post:2104975101829476377, post:2104975964144828704). He compares current price action to the 2023 spike down after the FTX bottom to judge where we are (post:2108404919375946163). He reasons about tokenomics mechanics in detail, including claim cycles, TWAP buybacks, and splitter budgets, and explains them step by step to critics (post:2106778812197314652, post:2106783384722715056, post:2106224161739657382). He lets creators decide mechanisms rather than imposing top-down rules (post:2106028291236983239, post:2107563835380908128). When shown he misread a critic's argument, he re-reads, concedes, and corrects himself (post:2106220420659614105).

## Risk attitude
He has a high pain tolerance but swears he has learned discipline. He admits to gambling on high leverage LTF and getting liquidated on multiple accounts (post:1979129462013718880). He warns that hedging free airdrops with illiquid premarket perps blows people up (post:1985828294206296391). He tells people to pay off their mortgage before taking crypto risk because psychology will mess with them (post:1970070095662850173). He expects a big player to get nuked off the market before the final bottom (post:2019526140688306463). Chart memory stays, so once something catches a stray on CT it is hard to recover (post:1994478084561514759).

By mid-2026 he is honest about numbness: after cycles, $10,000 no longer feels like $10,000 (post:2062810617719701592). He warns there is more pain ahead: "You think 59K is bad? Wait till you see 49K" (post:2062922857089298895, post:2062949976821403963). He tells people to exit the market entirely: "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY" (post:2054592566805360829). He says no airdrop is worth farming if you don't believe in the product (post:2067919596506468497). He warns stablecoins will depeg more that summer (post:2051386212628246795). He still has edge and conviction: "greed will kill you" (post:2082153065465934067), and "not believing in something can be expensive" (post:2083042549376204845). He warns followers not to tail his wallet because any action is testing only, and all is NFA, DYOR (post:2102057097949049194, post:2102056017492095227). He runs constant phishing, spoofing, fake GitHub, and impersonator warnings (post:2095184414053446012, post:2099837458506453128, post:2108600230942056687, post:2116353840408592875). He also admits the health toll of building: 3-5 hours of sleep, panic attacks, dangerously high blood pressure, and vitamin D deficiency (post:2100465301045313925, post:2103299031019762126, post:2103263318635168187). Late 2026 he frames downside through black-swan scenarios rather than day-to-day volatility (post:2104975101829476377), and respects legal compliance by refusing to discuss his own token's price or price action given its size and treasury holdings (post:2108578832139854011).
