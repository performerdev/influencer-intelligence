# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview
@MEADGod is built for bear markets. The earliest note from the 2026-04/10 batch is blunt: bear markets are his time to shine, and he thrives when others are down (post:2042303803782021472). That orientation colors everything. He does not wait for up-only conditions; he treats pain as his home turf.

At the same time, he views crypto as a field full of grifters, scammers, and people who do not build. He calls them out repeatedly, naming behavior and projects rather than vague complaints (post:2045533841226649880, post:2046385617592864858, post:2046584860119072962, post:2054996472341352926). His filter is sharp: most altcoins are dogshit with no purpose, and only revenue-powered tokens will win (post:2068271828791894245, post:2068271923302113780). He pairs that cynicism with a builder ethos: building something people want is worth more than chasing pumps (post:2062810617719701592, post:2055256053881704754). Great companies are made by hiring friends and grinding together, not by assembling mercenaries (post:2068384866966630608, post:2068748351063892090, post:2097031481684017166).

God is central to @MEADGod, not a bit of decoration. He thanks God, prays, and frames blessings as divine (post:2093764518870479353, post:2095212521502261681, post:2094863620035293345, post:2095343838135021734). Health and stress are equally real subjects. He learned to listen to his body and rest after pushing too hard (post:2057142130628518365, post:2059331330899316824, post:2059864376979362121, post:2100465301045313925). He does not separate body, soul, and market.

By late 2026-10, new layers appear. He says reputation is the most important asset in crypto, especially as the AI era makes identity matter; nobody cares about your product without people backing who you are (post:2106846254122647750). He says the future is private (post:2106214629454094469), EVM is superior (post:2106830467357888971), and institutions and suits are coming into crypto (post:2106021155387658608, post:2108308819520503839). He also sees ETFs having a great future onchain; pairing memes against ETFs is a big idea (post:2108308819520503839). Loyalty and work ethic deserve public recognition, not silence (post:2106487146408247332).

## How they reason and decide
He does not reason like a pure chartist. He uses gut and data together. In his own words: “I don't like to fade my gut” (post:2052596146749542451). But gut is not enough. He validates with data and metrics, including his own health data and revenue numbers when judging a product or direction (post:2059864376979362121, post:2099333424389480619).

He leans on historical patterns and market cycles to predict moves (post:2062810617719701592, post:2069836559948386506). His history is specific, not generic trading wisdom: “Back in 2018 I learned how to ape tops...” (post:2062810617719701592). That lesson makes him wary of repeating euphoric mistakes.

He also reasons like a builder. The way to win is to build and ship fast rather than talk (post:2076838646934577418, post:2077217434134614206). He judges projects by the people and reputation behind them, not just the product (post:2106846254122647750). In late 2026-10, he reads market bottoms by comparing current price action to past capitulation events, specifically the FTX bottom in 2023 (post:2108404919375946163). He also builds conviction from vibes and personal sentiment rather than charts alone (post:2108580164435419480, post:2106910359378411575). So his decision engine is: felt sense first, then historical comparison, then metrics or reputation as confirmation.

## Risk attitude
He accepts losses and setbacks without dwelling. He acknowledges them and moves forward (post:2048976696510951603, post:2062810617719701592). He warns about crypto-specific risks: stablecoin depegs, scams, and market downturns (post:2051386212628246795, post:2076357478363648047, post:2062949976821403963). He does not pretend risk does not exist.

He also treats his own body as a risk surface. Health and rest are not soft topics; they are survival requirements (post:2057142130628518365, post:2059331330899316824). His stated approach is calculated risk without overexposing himself (post:2062810617719701592). He also warns followers about impersonators and scammers pretending to be him, a different kind of risk management aimed at protecting his people (post:2108600230942056687). Finally, he is honest about product risk: Pons is far from perfect, and he commits to iterating rather than pretending otherwise (post:2107562852932558939).

By late 2026-10 he still says it will win when onchain gets hot again (post:2108640342497280273, post:2108623012899770476), and he ties that conviction to institutions and suits entering crypto (post:2106021155387658608, post:2108308819520503839).

**Pons** — bullish, his own project. He posts about building Pons with pride and shares milestones (post:2076778507623874607, post:2099333424389480619, post:2097181588370911469). Later he remains bullish: he publicly thanks loyal supporters by name and launches the web app with humility, not pure hype (post:2106487146408247332, post:2107562852932558939).

**AI** — started bearish, then mixed. Early: AI companies are digging their own grave and the AI bubble will burst (post:2064989599487651963, post:2056958424500375734, post:2065041800943792255). By late 2026-10 he is mixed on agentic AI x crypto, skeptical but not wholly dismissive (post:2108225937200615593). This is a partial view change toward nuance.

**Bitcoin** — changed. In the 2026-04/10 batch he was bearish (post:2062922857089298895, post:2067633875375861816). By late 2026-10 he turned bullish and bought spot for the first time since January 2023, comparing the moment to the FTX bottom (post:2108403501105590570, post:2108404919375946163). This is the cleanest view change in the notes: bearish earlier in the year, capitulation-buyer by late 2026-10.

**Solana** — bearish consistently. He mocked it and its defenders, including “Solana Manlets” in late 2026-10 (post:2072345596342460787, post:2074969246455464422, post:2108426556322324896).

**Ethereum** — mixed earlier (post:2063520143456841832, post:2102511365286219958), then his later claim that EVM is superior tilts positive for the ecosystem (post:2106830467357888971).

**Hyperliquid** — bullish in the earlier batch (post:2069999384960360658, post:2067332763875750249). It is the one coin he said he was comfortable being long.

Late additions: **onchain ETFs / stock tokens** — bullish, a big idea (post:2108308819520503839). **Privacy** — bullish, “the future is private” (post:2106214629454094469).
