# How @MEADGod thinks

## Worldview

He sees crypto as overrun with grifters, bots, fake engagement, and circular incentives. Early on he was already blunt: "DeFi is mostly fake TVL and incentive-chasing; only a few venues are legit" (post:1963860768929063302) and "Wallet counts are not users; real user numbers are far smaller" (post:1965834271916175582). By 2026 the cynicism hardened: "there is no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (post:2035807710428451140), and "Crypto tokens are essentially suicide for projects; altcoins are uninvestable because few succeed" (post:2031850768136093962). But he is not a pure hater — he builds what he thinks others are missing. He calls Pons "the people's launchpad: community comes first, fairness over bundled/coordinated dogshit" (post:2081860851250671905) and argues "Building real revenue-generating products beats chasing shitcoins; token utility shift is coming" (post:2068271828791894245).

His view of stablecoins is a through-line of suspicion. In 2025 he warned: "Stablecoins backed by other stablecoins backed by off-chain entities will cascade into the next bear market" (post:1965327296056996015). In mid-2026 he still expected "stablecoins will depeg more this summer" (post:2051386212628246795). The solution in his mind is not to trust more, but to make privacy native: "Privacy is a human right and on-chain payments should be private by default" (post:1980749499074003227). That thesis doesn't fade; in late 2026 he says full transparency elsewhere "will make people appreciate privacy in finance over time" (post:2106214629454094469).

He has a strong founder-builder orientation. "Founders must stick to what they built instead of pivoting; grant-hoppers and flip-floppers are losers" (post:1967149024278143387). Later he extends this to hiring: "Hiring friends/obsessed people is right; biggest companies were founded by friends grinding" (post:2068748351063892090). He respects shipping over talk: "Evaluates people by whether they ship and stick around, not by talk" (post:2022331892822053297).

His macro/religious frame is explicit and repeated: "God giveth and God taketh away; never get comfortable because forces bigger than you exist" (post:2033725422551371892), and "God, family, health and work are what he is all in on; numbers on a screen don't change happiness" (post:2081068081585992049). He treats struggles as lessons: "Everything happens for a reason; trust God and struggles become lessons" (post:2089010715101221195).

Geographically he is anti-Europe in the strongest terms: "Europe/Germany is badly mismanaged, over-taxed and over-regulated; leaving is the move" (post:2047062662698201200). He also loathes Solana: "I hate the solana team, the ecosystem and everything it stands for" (post:2072345596342460787). His preferred battleground became Robinhood Chain: "Robinhood chain is purpose-built for speculating on all assets, especially RWAs and memes" (post:2078221139285795194), and later "Tradfi is coming onchain; Robinhood Chain is the start of a new frontier and will win when onchain gets hot again" (post:2112975322475835650).

His AI view changed over time. In 2025 he thought "AI is not replacing workers; it increases workload and requires heavy human cleanup and monitoring" (post:2021206344380920248). By 2026 he had shifted to accepting displacement: "AI taking jobs is good, not something to fear" (post:2033112821752623130), while still calling the AI bubble a bubble: "AI companies are digging their own grave; cheaper models commoditize the space and the AI bubble will burst" (post:2064989599487651963).

He thinks crypto itself is bloated and most players will wash out: "Crypto is bloated with grifters, little innovation and no progress; most will return to 9-5s" (post:1975443367812288834). Yet he also says the next cycle will favor believers, bagworkers, and cult coins: "Cult coins will make a comeback" (post:2098820558762770603), and "The space is poisoned by a get-rich-quick mindset; the time of bundled and coordinated dogshit is ending" (post:2097091922204430532).

## How they reason and decide

His primary frame is cycle timing and the 4-year cycle. In 2025 he set explicit bottom targets: "Will buy a heck ton of HYPE at $8 and ETH at $1,200-1,500 BTC around $47K And then also some SOL at $64 or even $45 if we go that low. Yuh." (post:2000656074400317930). He expected a long grind: "9 months left in this bear market" (post:1996980501064646853). By 2026 he was calling levels again: "if we dip below 65k again best belief good dip to ape before 85" (post:2035884265175502918), and later: "Short and strong until at least October and then twap into everything in existence for echo bubble and then up only. Same shit different cycle." (post:2075048818677092382). By September 2026 he reasoned the bottom was probably in but still technically a bear: "Weighs the 4-year cycle as the primary technical frame even against his own bottom thesis" (post:2104975964144828704). He compares to 2023: "Compares current price action to past bottoms (the 2023 spike down after FTX)" (post:2108404919375946163).

He is deliberately contrarian. When people grab for bottom signals, he expects more pain: "You think 59K is bad? Wait till you see 49K." (post:2062922857089298895). When grifters leave, he expects relief rallies (post:2032512392144498905). He also trusts gut feel: "I don't like to fade my gut" (post:2052596146749542451).

For projects, he reads incentives and emissions rather than marketing. Early on he "Reads DeFi incentives and validator emissions to judge whether a product has a sustainable future" (post:1965515870836589000). He rejects wrappers: "Judges projects by whether they have their own infrastructure/edge rather than being a wrapper" (post:1984053107601633353). He distinguished burn from sell when accused of dumping: "I never sold, I burned - big difference." (post:2085152772517695589).

When defending Pons, he shifts into mechanism explanations with numbered points and concrete numbers. He explains "claim cycles, budgets, TWAP, burn rates" (post:2106778812197314652), and he "Explains mechanics in detail with numbered points when accused of wrongdoing (taxes, routing, mcap)" (post:2097602877925609720). He also judges products by direct use: "Judges products by hands-on use: tells critics they clearly haven't used the beta" (post:2106530467524571579).

He reads tradfi signals as confirmation for chain-level theses: "Reads tradfi moves (license applications, stock tokens, WSJ ads) as confirmation that tradfi is coming onchain" (post:2112975322475835650). He also uses community feedback and polls to make product decisions (post:2084753264591937918).

## Risk attitude

His risk attitude is self-aware and contradictory. He warns everyone else to be careful, then gambles. He admitted: "sometimes I should listen to myself instead of gambling LTF on high leverage liquidated on like 3 accounts" (post:1979129462013718880). He tells followers to de-risk real life first: "Pay off your mortgage, there is always money to be made. If things don't work out, you will hate yourself for it." (post:1970070095662850173). He also has a blunt risk rule: "greed will kill you" (post:2082153065465934067).

He is wary of counterparty and illiquidity risk. He warned against "hedging free airdrops with illiquid premarket perps unless you want to get blown the fuck out" (post:1985828294206296391), and said counterparty risk is "being butchered to almost zero as a design goal" (post:1986035133908979972). He expects violent washouts: "Expects a big player to get nuked off the market before the final bottom" (post:2019526140688306463).

He has become numb to size: "Acknowledges being numb to gains/losses after cycles; $10,000 no longer feels like $10,000" (post:2062810617719701592). That does not stop him from flashing exits: "EXIT THE FUCKING MARKET MATEY." (post:2054592566805360829), and "Don’t trade today, it will get ugly imo" (post:2062418191242932399).

He is extremely cautious about security and legal exposure. He warns followers not to tail his wallet: "I don't sell coins - I also don't trade on the wallet. Any action on the wallet is for testing purposes and you shouldn't follow me on anything." (post:2102057097949049194). He warns about phishing and spoofed GitHub: "Beware of Spoofing - we are never gonna post about a mascot or some example config file whatever." (post:2099837458506453128). He also refuses to discuss PONS price due legal compliance: "鉴于该代币规模巨大，且我们的金库中持有大量该代币，我必须遵守相关法律合规要求，不能在此讨论其价格或价格走势。" (post:2108578832139854011).

He openly discusses the health toll: "3-5hrs sleep, panic attacks, blood pressure dangerously high, vitamin D deficiency" (post:2100465301045313925, post:2103299031019762126). He frames this as the cost of building and says he went "all in on God, family, health, work" (post:2081068081585992049).

## Favorite narratives

**Bitcoin and the 4-year cycle** — Bearish to cautiously bullish. In 2025 he set $47K BTC, $1,200-1,500 ETH, $45-64 SOL targets and said "9 months left in this bear market" (post:2000656074400317930, post:1996980501064646853). Mid-2026 he still wanted lower: "Wait till you see 49K" (post:2062922857089298895). By late 2026 he says the bottom is likely in, but a nuke needs a black swan, and the 4-year cycle keeps him technically bearish; he expects an echo bubble in alts (post:2104975101829476377). He now DCA’s toward one whole Bitcoin (post:2108404333561930170). The change is from demanding lower levels to accepting accumulation higher.

**DeFi and stablecoins** — Bearish throughout. Early: stablecoin depeg cascade (post:1965327296056996015). Mid: "no such thing as DeFi, only MultisigFi" (post:2035807710428451140). He never became a DeFi bull; he built a launchpad and privacy rails instead.

**Privacy / Roots / Pons** — Bullish from early to late. Early: "privacy is a human right" (post:1980749499074003227). He built Roots privacy payments and later Pons. By late 2026 he says "the future is private" and ties privacy to finance (post:2106216341745148127). The focus shifts from payments to the reputation/privacy layer, but the conviction stays.

**AI / LLM economics** — Bearish on bubble, mixed on labor. He called LLM subscription prices subsidized by VC money and said hardware owners win (post:2025852190460395874). Mid-2026 he said the AI bubble will burst (post:2064989599487651963). But he also said "AI taking jobs is good" (post:2033112821752623130), so his bearishness is capital-cycle, not anti-tech.

**Solana** — Bearish, escalated. Early he was mixed on perp DEXs and included SOL as a dip buy. By mid-2026 he said "I hate the solana team, the ecosystem and everything it stands for" (post:2072345596342460787) and later mocked "Solana manlets" (post:2102569196018143718).

**Robinhood Chain / Pons / memestocks** — Not present in the first period, then became his home narrative. First he called it purpose-built for speculation (post:2078221139285795194), then "the people's launchpad" (post:2081860851250671905), then "the main battlefield of this cycle" (post:2092329203278413832). By late 2026 he says Robinhood Chain "will win when onchain gets hot again" (post:2112975322475835650) and Pons is its leading stock-token platform (post:2115624170575065535). This is the biggest stance development in the notes.

**Hyperliquid** — Mixed to bullish. Early mixed on perp DEXs; mid-2026 "the only coin im comfortable being in a juicy long is hyperliquid, short everything else" (post:2069999384960360658); later he admires why it worked (post:2092678996978774206).

**Prediction markets, InfoFi, Kaito, DATs** — Bearish consistently. He called InfoFi and airdrop farming the worst things to hit the space (post:1998398078802399535) and mocked prediction markets (post:1994159788133945605).

**Gaming / indie devs** — Bullish late: "Very bullish on indie devs and thinks gaming could have an insane run" (post:2108421151949300154).
