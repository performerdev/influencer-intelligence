# How @zachxbt thinks

## Worldview

The core belief running from 2021 through 2026 is that **on-chain evidence is the truth**. He repeatedly frames his work as letting data and the historical record speak for themselves rather than relying on narratives or reputation (post:1594353661966225423, post:1575494418911858691). This is not passive: he uses it to debunk people who appear knowledgeable but have no clue (post:1594353588872044549).

His early view of Crypto Twitter, stated in 2021 and maintained later, is that **a large portion of CT are marketers pretending to be traders, not actual traders** (post:1403719977283227650, post:1422413219516325890, post:1458480680292212737). By early 2022 he sharpened this: most big CT accounts are marketers, not successful traders or builders, and they maintain a successful appearance through deceptive means (post:1482443139524837382, post:1482451440060846083, post:1484921421876719616).

He believes **undisclosed paid shilling is rampant and harmful**. Influencers should disclose ads, and many promote scams for money (post:1471123740142493701, post:1471218844660379670, post:1498670055223181322). He extends this to VCs and launchpads: collective shilling campaigns for projects they are secretly invested in, without disclosure, are a systemic problem (post:1410356566658793475, post:1433821925470580737). Launchpads are mostly cash grabs that enrich founders and VCs at the expense of retail (post:1428065460680003587, post:1446538141653929989, post:1449036867672940550).

His view on consequences shifted over time. Early on he noted that self-policing in crypto only works when the person is genuinely disliked; otherwise people forget and revert (post:1493533784867803136, post:1493534851789639685). By mid-2022 he increasingly framed law enforcement and legal action as the right path, celebrating FBI seizures, arrests, and convictions (post:1621576193152634881, post:1770681711728177480, post:1950273651133034839). By 2025-2026 he argues that law enforcement and exchanges should act faster, and he wants exceptions made for jurisdictions like Canada that rarely prosecute "The Com" (post:2005649228408725593, post:2040056067640709563, post:2045820638355693683).

A related evolution concerns centralized actors. In 2022 he already said exchanges and projects often hide hacks and blame users instead of taking responsibility (post:1605235178468741129, post:1630665458134163476, post:1592388916577193985). By 2024-2026 this became a specific critique: **Coinbase fails its users on social engineering scams**, restricting accounts instead of stopping $300M+/year losses, and its leadership is out of touch (post:1886411879939031530, post:1880045353648681354, post:1880046118115143858). **Circle markets itself as most compliant but fails to act on clear-cut hacks or exploits where competitors do** (post:1835931110183768452, post:1835938039907959278, post:1940388873227657687). He is careful to say he holds USDC himself and does not want Circle to collapse; his criticism is about compliance decisions, not hoping they fail (post:2040056067640709563).

By 2025 he developed the thesis that crypto is in a **"crime supercycle"** enabled by politicians launching meme coins, dropped court cases, and laundering groups winning against Lazarus Group (post:1935178436970168347). He also says **KYC/AML is mostly useless in the grand scheme of things**, because crypto moves faster than tradfi so traditional strategies cannot be applied (post:1879983934110593326). A related conclusion: the crypto community is far too entitled to build products for in its current state; only private tools for compliance/LE make sense (post:1831598921396687028).

On specific threat actors: **DPRK IT workers are unsophisticated but persistent**, infiltrating hundreds of dev jobs; hiring them is a sign a startup will fail (post:1940388908124291553, post:1940388827392344261, post:1955614285343699128). He says Huione and similar SEA marketplaces are among the bigger threats, laundering billions (post:1979899778637918374). He also says threat actors are undone by their own vanity: flexing stolen funds, band-for-band contests, and leaked recordings create the paper trail that gets them caught (post:2005649220657934837, post:2014685303701713321, post:2086785507921736088).

On retail and influencers: **influencers and KOLs face zero repercussions for scamming followers**, and people must stop blindly following influencer calls (post:1947641139281965462, post:1857014727005655336). Many people do not do basic due diligence and become exit liquidity (post:1646260492053147648, post:1662588069441093633, post:1656406662838616064).

## How they reason and decide

His method has been consistent: **follow the money on-chain**. Early examples include tracing fund flows to identify wallets and exposing insider dumping (post:1410356545108467713, post:1442872479735853059, post:1457750290095091718). By 2022 he formalized this: trace stolen funds through mixers, bridges, and exchanges, publishing transaction hashes and addresses (post:1569323601995653120, post:1597605409883566080, post:1824047438061306338).

His recurring reasoning patterns:

- **Cross-reference on-chain data with social media activity** (tweets, deletions, ENS, OpenSea names) to link wallets to identities (post:1516070166643228679, post:1575494418911858691, post:1627653277440409600).
- **Analyze tokenomics**, especially vesting schedules and allocation to private rounds, to assess dump risk (post:1449036805748187146, post:1465039819764117512, post:1465716524560355328).
- **Use archived tweets and deleted content** to expose undisclosed shilling (post:1433821991858016262, post:1466202007702806529, post:1468627653469032448).
- **Compile master threads and lists** of bad actors for reference (post:1443583283648872464, post:1451627071034335246, post:1467923764394573834).
- **Reach out for comment before publishing**, but post if no response within 24 hours (post:1496163412644188162, post:1496178247234691072).
- **Use tools** like Etherscan, Breadcrumbs, Chainabuse, Wayback Machine, and Twitter search to visualize and document findings (post:1496460894997524481, post:1518600321953681408, post:1540363765165834242, post:1545843873389449218).
- **Wallet clustering and demixing**: use multi-denomination reveal heuristics to demix Tornado Cash withdrawals and link addresses (post:1732790508714430685, post:1813896353296322987).
- **Corroborate with OSINT**, leaked databases, victim testimony, and device indicators (post:1836753409749274895, post:1859233166063472697, post:1937121757028360405).
- **Timing analysis**: match amounts and timing between a screenshot or message and on-chain flows to link addresses (post:2005649194435195201, post:2005649198281363686, post:2107081662765158780).
- **Undercover work**: he went undercover as a client to gather intel, accepting a 5% loss per order as the cost of actionable data (post:2107081659824906360, post:2107081676795077095, post:2036430865862201502).
- **Hold off publishing** until charges are public or the case is unsealed, out of sensitivity to investigations (post:2054170025398026372, post:2046911569532231725).
- **Debunk misinformation** by checking on-chain facts and calling out people who appear knowledgeable but have no clue (post:1594353588872044549).
- **Use a simple ratio litmus test**: $6B market cap wiped on $52M of liquidations points to manipulation (post:2045820638355693683).

## Risk attitude

He does not present himself as a trader; his risk discussions mostly concern security, personal safety, and investigation costs.

- **Security risk**: he constantly warns about phishing, SIM swaps, and wallet drains. His rule is never use SMS 2FA; use a security key or authenticator app (post:1666894400683663375, post:1694326221511794706). He warns about seed phrases in LastPass and advises migrating immediately (post:1717901088521687330).
- **Personal safety**: he acknowledges threats and harassment (post:1472369684574089224, post:1473681869736718346, post:1538522269521166336). He also warns about home invasions targeting crypto holders in Western Europe (post:1844369955447324848).
- **Legal risk**: he faces legal threats and lawsuits, and says he is willing to fight back to defend free speech (post:1669783717236342785, post:1670828486641033216).
- **Burnout and sustainability**: he has repeatedly posted about burnout, entitlement, and considering charging for time (post:1567178109517971459, post:1627640859914129408, post:1633548505129050117). By 2025 he says donation-based funding is inconsistent and a liability for scaling (post:1861682640723558470).
- **Investigation risk**: he fronted $349.7K of his own money and lost 5% per order with no guarantee the counterparty wouldn't disappear, plus unknown personal risk (post:2107081676795077095).
- **Recovery risk**: he notes recovery is often impossible, and there is a small window for freezing funds; 50% may already be moved before exchanges can freeze (post:1979899788469399679, post:1587825715893993472).
- **Trading risk stance**: he explicitly says he did not take a position in RAVE because he would have been liquidated himself (post:2045820638355693683). He warns that shorting supply-controlled tokens gives insiders fuel to push price higher (post:2054898923450564988).
